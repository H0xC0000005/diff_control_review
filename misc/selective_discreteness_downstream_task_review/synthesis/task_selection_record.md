# T5 provisional task-selection record

Date: 2026-09-10  
Status: Candidate A accepted in principle; remaining T5 gate items await user decision

## Provisional selection

Primary family: continuous effective-service allocation under fixed phase topology at one junction, with aggregate-equivalence, finite-storage, aggregate-matched order/access, and combined held-out layers.

Required embedded diagnostic: feasible gradient-direction perturbations before full optimization.

First later task: executable fixed-time signal realization.  
First spatial extension: short arterial with interacting spillback.  
Later evaluation architecture: fixed-bank or adaptive multi-fidelity optimization.

## Decision basis

This selection maximizes direct mechanism isolation and baseline matchability while minimizing new signal semantics. It is supported by established LTM-MPC, turn-queue, lane-storage, partial-FIFO, order-tracking, differentiable-simulation, and richer-evaluation precedents. No located work combines exact continuous-mass order/access semantics with validated signal-service gradients and external decision-usefulness tests.

## Not yet decided

T5 does not fix the objective equation, green parameterization, geometry dimensions, horizon, terminal rule, scenario values, tolerance, optimizer, budget, or SUMO realization. These belong to T6 after the family and diagnostic hierarchy are selected.

No project document or implementation is changed by this provisional selection.
