## Query-refinement result

Useful terms were `effective green fraction`, `green split`, `turn-level queue`, `lane blockage`, `finite storage`, `overflow`, `spillback`, `MPC`, `gradient-based simulation optimization`, `multi-fidelity`, and `common random numbers`. Broad `traffic signal + gradient` searches produced substantial policy-gradient/RL noise; those terms will be constrained in later phases unless a work informs evaluation practice.
