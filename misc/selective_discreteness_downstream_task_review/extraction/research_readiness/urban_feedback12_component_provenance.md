## Why the grid and both ledgers belong in the report

The earlier short report mentioned the grid only as a summary and did not adequately explain its role. The link ledger retains fixed route identity and continuous chronology over the aggregate road. Local lane ledgers add realized lane membership and exact sequence. The per-lane grid stores the corresponding continuous movement composition and load, reconstructed from those same masses. The grid is not additional traffic to add to the objective.

A mixed bin cannot identify its leading movement. Conversely, an ordered list without the continuous quantities and their mapping would not explain the intended tensor state or gradient path. Both descriptions are therefore included. Continuous service removes part of a packet; remaining mass and cumulative interval endpoints change continuously, and their overlaps with fixed mass bins transmit those changes to lane loads. Fixed metadata need not be differentiated for that path to exist.

The earlier plan explicitly chooses cumulative-mass/rank bins, geometry-derived physical storage and a ledger-authoritative reconstructed grid. It does not specify a standard spatial CTM as an independent near-node propagation model. Restoring the documented grid therefore does not authorize a physical CTM replacement, smoothing, integer transfers or a simulator implementation.
