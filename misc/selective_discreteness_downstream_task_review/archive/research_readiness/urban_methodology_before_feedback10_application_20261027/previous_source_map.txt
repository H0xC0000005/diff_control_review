## Feedback 10: selected status supersedes the previous coupled candidate

The [separate answer](../../reporting/research_readiness/urban_feedback10_response.md) records the selected shared-lane starting scenario, actual-admission commitment, no-target residual and movement-specific processing policy, withdrawal of Q4, and retention of constant average receiving A. Only the physical application of movement-specific skipping to the common-feeder representation remains open. This answer-only pass derives no replacement coupled map.

All prior displayed equations are preserved as written. In particular, eq:pending-allocation and eq:old-commitment now belong to an unselected conditional waiting-quota model; their associated derivative pieces are not the current accepted waiting-state construction. The common prefix gate cannot be presented as the general logical-allocation policy. Realized-lane FIFO and actual physical access still apply.

Eq:interval-rate is now the selected receiving convention. It does not remove the dependencies in elapsed head clearance, current-head sharing or eq:service-jacobian-new. The new permission choice also leaves the existing exclusive-target mixture ablation outside the selected shared-permission starting scenario unless adapted later.

The previous full candidate is not a completed current specification after this amendment. This is decision/status synchronization, not completion of the new traffic or gradient transcription.

## Earlier clarification before feedback 10

The [expanded decision note](../../temp_content/research_readiness/joined_methodology_review.md) is reproduced in the methods PDF's decision appendix. It separates five concrete choices under the existing question IDs. All twenty-five specification labels and four compact-methods labels retain their earlier equation bodies and proposal status; this expansion approves no law.

The permission recommendation is now shared left/through access when active responsive choice is intended. The existing exclusive-target equations remain an illustration and the domain of the optional mixture ablation. A shared-permission test needs a matched comparator; no formula is silently transferred to a different movement matrix.

The receiving comparison is defined here as two possible coarse node laws. With allowance 0.6, physical receiving rate 1 and a second resident head joining halfway through, constant average receiving yields lane outputs 0.45/0.15; a consumable allowance yields 0.55/0.05. Both respect current-head sharing and total receiving, but leave different boundary states. This is exact hand reasoning, not a sourced theorem, executed simulation or return to the deferred event-history study.

The waiting example separates first assignment from retargeting. Full remaining commitment follows the current default; delaying the first assignment of new unadmitted mass would require an explicit amendment. Conditional realization of genuinely old multi-lane targets is a separate question, not a renewed mandatory fresh-allocation vote.

## Native-model qualification stays distinct

The [native-rival record](urban_aggregate_rival_source_notes.md) and [closed policy discussion](../../temp_content/research_readiness/aggregate_rival_questions.md) retain working A/A preferences. Exact state/implementation qualification is not silently replaced by the new ablation.

[Wei et al., arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.12249v2), section 2, is the concrete B1 source. Its full-law qualification remains an adoption condition, not completed by selecting a fixed-speed shortcut.

[Gong et al. (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0191261525000578), sections 5–7, is the lane-exit allocation source. The complete algorithm was not independently reproduced here. Finite-bay entry needs its own qualified composition.

[Liu and Chang, TRC 19, 130–144](https://attap.umd.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/TRC522.pdf), sections 2.4–2.6, supplies a storage/blockage candidate. Current citations use journal year **2011**, rather than 2010 from the DOI. Exact overflow notation and signal/timestep adaptation remain unqualified. Failed source-page/PDF access is not evidence of a source defect.

These are the implementation-dependent choices accepted in feedback 8. They limit principal-rival readiness; they do not reopen the macro information boundary or justify excluding a stronger legal estimator.

The controlled mixture ablation can overrestrict entry relative to an unblocked ordered prefix. Failure on V2 therefore exposes its closure limitation; it cannot replace a qualified storage-aware B2 or carry an order-necessity claim by itself.
