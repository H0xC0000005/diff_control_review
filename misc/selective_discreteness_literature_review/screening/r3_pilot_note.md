# R3 screening-pilot note

Date: 2026-09-07  
Status: Passed; full title/abstract screening proceeded without a protocol amendment

## Pilot sample

A deliberately heterogeneous 24-record sample was screened: R0001–R0018, R0022–R0025, R0029, and R0031. It contained foundational, nearest-neighbor, task-only, review, preprint, hybrid, and likely-exclusion records.

## Calibration findings

- The implemented-state rule successfully prevents labels such as “mesoscopic” or “differentiable” from deciding eligibility by themselves.
- Recent differentiable microscopic work is relevant only as a narrow derivative/construction boundary, not as evidence for the proposed aggregate/semantic state.
- A review article can be retained as secondary mapping evidence while remaining separate from primary-method synthesis.
- Related preprint/journal records cannot safely be deduplicated on title similarity alone; R0022 is therefore uncertain pending R5 full-text/version-family checking.
- Signal-control papers without a modeling contribution are retained only when they define a comparator task or validation contract.

## Decision

No unresolved criterion ambiguity prevented consistent screening. The eligibility rules were not changed. Proceed to R4 and flag the one version-family uncertainty instead of guessing.
