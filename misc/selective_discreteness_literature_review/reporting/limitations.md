# Final review limitations

Date: 2026-09-07

- This is a proportionate systematic mapping review with focused synthesis, not an exhaustive Scopus/Web of Science/TRID database export.
- Discovery combined direct arXiv search, accessible scholarly and publisher sources, an independent systematic node-model review, and two citation generations. Coverage is strong for the defined design neighborhood but cannot prove universal absence.
- The user was the sole human reviewer and Codex performed discovery, first-pass screening, and extraction. Human gates covered criteria, nearest predecessors, contextual inclusions, coding, baseline logic, and synthesis.
- Thirty-seven studies entered synthesis: 36 primary studies and one secondary systematic review. Seven records were preprints at the review date.
- Terminology such as macroscopic and mesoscopic is inconsistent across publications; coding therefore follows implemented state and update rules.
- Credibility judgments describe reporting and evidential support, not an overall ranking of scientific merit.
- Runtime and accuracy results were not pooled because networks, physics, objectives, hardware, and comparison budgets are heterogeneous.
- A full implementation comparison was not performed. Feasibility roles distinguish executable, equation/reference, conceptual, and richer-evaluator uses.
- Question B, including selection and formulation of the downstream task, is outside scope.
- Review conclusions do not approve a project model, experiment, milestone amendment, or publication claim.
