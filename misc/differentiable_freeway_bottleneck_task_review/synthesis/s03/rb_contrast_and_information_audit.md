## One primary contrast: aggregate-matched, different merging load

Compare a condition with more mass remaining in the ending lane near the merge
to one with more mass already occupying continuing lanes. Same geometry,
population, future demand/disturbance and common advice perturbations.

Match the reduced model's spatial total mass/density, mean speed, boundary
queues, retained speed-response states and specified observation/history.
If the baseline retains cumulative counts, match those too. Matching current
total density alone is insufficient. Detector occupancy is not traffic-mass
density without an explicit length/unit relation.

Candidate preparation: physically valid lane-biased initial allocation/
uncontrolled prehistory, followed by endogenous car-following/merging before
the decision time. Past differences must be recorded, not hidden under a
claim of identical scenarios. No ongoing lane-change command is a tested action.
Vehicles cannot be placed into physically impossible states to force matching.

This is a proposed preparation route, not a verified construction. Exact
history matching may make the pair impossible; approximate matching would
need a later approved residual-error bound and a comparison showing that the
residual cannot explain the decision difference. Do not silently change an
exact-match claim into a looser one.

Intended chain: hidden occupancy changes feasible transfer/receiving → changes
merge interference and response to the same advice → potentially changes
system time-spent sensitivity and action ranking. The last link is missing
evidence. Advice may only add delay, or aggregate history may already predict
the relevant discharge. Forward blockage alone does not establish useful
gradient differences.

## Gradient admissibility and evidence still required

Existing FB0030 equations17–19 and FB0038 equation11 retain branch/denominator
hazards. Paired conservation alone does not guarantee continuity or valid
simultaneous donor/receiver budgets. Density-driven fractions may be
deterministic continuous flows, not sampled categorical choices. No smoothing,
empty-state convention, mode repair or hybrid derivative is selected here.
Chen2025 adds donor-index, empty-demand, wave-sign and hard-capacity-switch
transcription hazards (equations19–28), plus discrete actions/evolutionary search
(equation36/section4.2). Its analytic Markov headway averages are not themselves
a sampled categorical mass-transfer process. See
[source-level audit](../../extraction/rb_source_studies/FB0040.md).

The recommended next diagnostic, if separately planned and approved, should first ask whether the
independent reference produces a robust same-advice decision difference,
without building a full new AD simulator. Only then would model transcription,
internal finite-difference checks and external gradient/outcome transfer earn
their construction cost. This is the provisional RB-UG1 recommendation, not
an approved experiment or approval of lane dynamics/gradient machinery.
Finite reference perturbations test action outcomes, not microscopic native AD.
Reference lane-changing discontinuities cannot be promoted to a smooth-gradient
certificate or used to justify a control-dependent categorical candidate model.

Required later progression: physically valid preparations and stated memory
match → shared-action outcome ranking including an unchanged-advice option →
robustness to residual match/response/capacity variation and held-out cases →
only if warranted, conservative candidate dynamics with reference correctness,
internal directional derivatives and independent gradient usefulness. Different
forward trajectories or derivative magnitudes alone do not pass the decision test.

Counterevidence must include no-benefit cases and response/capacity uncertainty.
