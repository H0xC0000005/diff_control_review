## VQ3 — Can the preparation/service reduction carry the intended first task?

**Problem statement.** What is the smallest defensible modeling contract in
which common speed changes realized merge preparation, and that preparation
changes complete traffic time? The task must remain an elementary
information/gradient verification.

**Assumed model-chain function.** Common advice determines longitudinal response
and exposure to transfer opportunities. Paired transfers change remaining
mandatory merging. Finite receiving and a departure allocation convert that
state into time until complete exit. The whole chain must include the travel
and interaction costs paid for preparation.

**Core gap.** S13 specifies the whole chain mathematically, but its constant
time-based transfer rate, nonbinding preparation and proportional choke batch
are analytical adaptations. No accepted source audit has derived or jointly
validated them as an applied model. Spatial lane-change desire is not the same
as realized completion; final work/clearance is not the same as mean time.

**Ownership and dependencies.** Type 2; the reviewing agent can qualify evidence
within G3, but the user owns any change of scientific law or task meaning.
Depends on accepted G1 elementary role, G2 common action/observer fairness and
G4 complete accounting/evidence separation. Close these global semantics before
source-index, record-format or implementation choices.

### Candidate A — retain the conditional S13 member as the first analytical benchmark

Use exactly the inherited ideal common-speed response, paired constant-rate

Test its information/gradient chain and scalar/grouped equivalence within the
declared domain. Any later robustness alternatives retain their own claim
boundaries. This candidate does not promote the equations to a calibrated or
independently validated traffic model.

**Pros.** It is already mathematically complete and accepted, keeps one action

**Cons.** The primary mechanism depends on the time-based opportunity and batch

applied claim without a separately qualified traffic/evaluation layer.
The inspected evidence does not further justify the fixed completion rate
across heterogeneous receiving conditions and common-speed changes. Retaining
A as the primary task would require an explicit conditional analytical role;
preserving its closed result does not require retaining its law in a revision.

**Dependencies and consequences.** Preserve conditional observer reachability
and the scalar rival. Label its result analytical; an independently coded copy
remains an internal reference, not a richer physical evaluator. This is a valid
fallback if applied qualification fails, not evidence that the original task
has been empirically verified.
