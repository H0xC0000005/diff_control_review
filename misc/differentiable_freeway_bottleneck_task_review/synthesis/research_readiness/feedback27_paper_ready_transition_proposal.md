# Accepted transition to an elementary paper-ready VSL task

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: **transition roadmap accepted by explicit user
approval; ready for the fresh agent's bounded opening plan.** The user accepted
the feedback-27 materials and requested proper ramp closure. No detailed
successor review, implementation or experiment is opened by this record alone.
Approval covers the role, scope, ordering and recommended starting direction;
it does not prove the remaining scientific claims or freeze unevidenced laws.

## What the next review should produce

Produce one VSL lane-drop task that can be written as a coherent methodology
section and implemented as the first elementary verification. It should explain
which traffic information changes a common speed decision, how that information
enters the derivative path, and which simpler representations can reproduce it.
Do not require full vehicle identity, elaborate merging kinetics or a large
network unless evidence makes them essential to the admitted claim.

“Paper-ready” means a complete, internally consistent **method specification**:
physical task, state, controls, dynamics, objective, derivatives, comparisons,
validation and claim boundaries. It does not mean empirical results, calibrated
parameters or publication novelty have already been established. Exact numerical
settings can follow an approved calibration protocol; scientific mechanisms and
meaningful interfaces cannot remain unspecified.

The three original questions remain separate: what omitted information matters,
whether it changes the controller's useful decision, and whether its gradient
predicts a useful perturbation. Added task-selection requirements are elementary
verification and a defensible connection to the urban study. They are scientific
role constraints, not a requirement to fit existing project code.

## What is already established

mass changes receiving work. The hidden state can change the optimal speed and
the analytical improving direction. Its consequential postdecision state can
be represented by one continuous scalar. Full lane state or discrete identity

## Four accepted transition stages

### 1. Bind the elementary role and check the nearest literature

**Purpose.** Decide whether the existing VSL kernel can be the requested first
verification and whether its proposed contribution survives comparison with
the closest established models. This is the early checkpoint that was missing
from the recent ramp construction.

Draft a short methodology outline immediately: one lane drop, one preparation
zone, one common bounded speed-advice action, conserved continuous traffic and
complete system time. Explain the common urban/VSL claim without equating
continuous lane preparation with discrete movement order.

Reuse the existing source map. Check the nearest aggregate VSL/capacity-drop,
lane-aware/mandatory-transfer and gradient-control precedents against the exact
proposed comparison. Identify their retained state, actions, service mechanism,
derivative validation and control-evaluation scope. Extend the search only for
a decision-critical missing claim or a close successor, without a paper quota.

**Output.** A one-task claim statement, a nearest-work comparison, an elementary
scope boundary and a short source/evidence-gap map.

**Stop or reframe if** the proposed contribution is already established, the
task requires an independent microscopic research stack to be credible, or its
connection to the urban hypothesis cannot be stated honestly. If it survives
only as a verification example, explicitly discuss that role instead of
promoting it as novel research. A colleague's endorsement supports relevance;
it does not substitute for this claim check.

### 2. Specify the smallest complete VSL modeling chain

**Purpose.** Turn the conditional kernel into an inspectable model rather than
constructing every possible lane-drop mechanism.

Define geometry and cohort/boundary scope; continuous state and units; common
advice versus realized speed; conservative longitudinal/lateral transfer;
finite receiving and allocation; timing; initial information; terminal
clearance; and full population-time accounting. Keep travel/preparation delay
in the objective so a slow advice action is not rewarded for hiding traffic.

For every component, label direct source adoption, bounded adaptation and
original deduction. Audit the exponential transfer specialization and
proportional batch service first: specify their intended regime and evidence
limits, and test the smallest relevant deletion/alternative needed to assess
whether the decision distinction is an artifact. Do not silently call advice
the actual speed under arbitrary compliance, or add an acceleration law only
to manufacture a positive result.

Use the current two-group ending/continuing representation as a starting
candidate, not a commitment that it is sufficient under every admitted law.
Add full per-lane state only if its missing information is demonstrated.

**Output.** A methods-style model contract, source-to-module table, one
hand-auditable causal example, key limiting/deletion cases and a bounded
robustness plan. Heavy equations belong in an editable LaTeX/PDF companion.

**Stop or return to Stage 1 if** closure requires a new state/model family that
breaks the elementary role, essential behavior has no justified law, or every
admitted case has one information-independent best action. An analytical
existence result may remain valid even if an applied task is not admitted.

### 3. Define fair representation and gradient tests

**Purpose.** Make the experiment diagnostic of information value, with
optimizer/controller effects kept separate.

Declare what each model observes initially, retains afterward and predicts
independently. Preserve the accepted exact-history construction only where its
reachability and observer assumptions apply; do not import a reduced snapshot
as evidence of full-history ambiguity. Compare common actions, traffic,
parameters and complete accounting. No model gets future reference corrections.

Begin with a strong aggregate baseline, aggregate plus remaining-merge state,
and the admitted grouped/lane model. A compact model reproducing the richer
model is a valid finding. A fixed local state supplied to one model but withheld
from a comparator must have an explicit scientific justification and be named
as the information contrast. Further controller comparisons use their own
matched observation/action contract.

Specify forward correctness and conservation checks; analytical/AD/directional
finite-difference derivative checks within declared active regimes; and an
independent richer evaluation of small common perturbations. Distinguish
predicted local improvement from improvement in that independent evaluator.
Use a minimal mechanism/negative-control suite before full optimization.
Review the evaluator's fitness without installing or running it.

**Output.** A matched baseline/ablation table, derivative/event contract and
first-verification protocol with outcome-blind failure interpretation.

**Stop or narrow the claim if** the admitted information has no action-relevant
effect, the derivatives cannot be defined/checked under the intended task,
the comparison relies on a deliberately weak rival, or no credible independent
evaluation exists for the proposed external-usefulness claim. Document theory
and verification roles separately if an applied claim must be deferred.

### 4. Integrate the paper framework, audit urban alignment and freeze the plan

**Purpose.** Produce one coherent research story and a reviewable execution
plan, with VSL as the first verification if the preceding evidence admits it.

Compare the two tasks through the same scientific sequence: retained
information → changed service/exit timing → checked sensitivity → independently
useful control perturbation. State explicitly that urban order and VSL merge
preparation are different kinds of information. Reuse the closed urban action,
objective, baseline and validation contracts. Identify only genuine gaps in
methodology-section completeness; any revision of a closed urban scientific
choice needs its own smallest reopening.

Prepare a short expert discussion packet showing traffic meaning, model
assumptions, source/adaptation lineage and the strong simpler rivals. Freeze
the admitted task, claim/nonclaims, component responsibilities, numerical
calibration strategy, implementation/validation sequence and stop conditions
only after methodological discussion. A source-backed framework is required;
an expert opinion alone cannot establish a novelty or gradient-usefulness result.

**Output.** The consolidated methodology framework, expert feedback record and
approved implementation/experiment plan. Implementation remains a separate
task under the repository planning and approval workflow.
