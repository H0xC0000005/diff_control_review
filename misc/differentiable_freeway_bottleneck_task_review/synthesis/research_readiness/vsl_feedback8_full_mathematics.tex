Feedback 8 mathematical report --- 16 October 2026

\textbf{Purpose.} Assemble the selected model and the mathematical work
supporting its improvements in one notation. This is a consolidation of
the S13 reference, H1/T1, selected S-E-P/R-E/O-R/W-F, the full-domain
forward argument at WF-Q1 and the physical-identifiability review at MP6-Q1.
It is not a new model selection, experiment, derivative validation or
execution plan. No rejected formula is reinstated as current behavior.

\textbf{The central chain.} Initial footprint and common advice determine
receiving-limited preparation. Boundary histories transport the resulting
streams into a finite terminal. A/E service combines present maneuver work
with retained effects of earlier E entries. Conservation and complete
departures determine local total time. The proofs qualify this chain under
declared restrictions, not all lane-drop traffic.

\textbf{Reading guide.} Sections 1--5 give the original reference, geometry,
upstream law and terminal interfaces. Sections 6--8 specify present service
and recovery. Sections 9--11 give causality, positive service, event
continuation, clearance and accounting. Section 12 records information and
sensitivity limits. Section 13 reproduces the selected analytical coexistence
construction. Sections 14--15 collect physical-identification deductions
and the source/status map. These supporting proofs are available for human
review; symbolic checks of identities are not machine certification of
quantified physical or hybrid-system claims.

\textbf{Status.} The stack is functionally complete as a conditional
mathematical model. Applied MP6, amended action/gradient and SUMO evidence
contracts remain open. Step 3 is unfinished; Step 4 remains unopened.
S13's closed certificate remains valid for S13 and is not transferred to the
amended model. ``M2'' in the review is a mechanism label, not project
implementation Milestone 2. The urban task and ramp line remain closed.
