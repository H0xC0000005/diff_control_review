# Assumptions of the standalone VSL lane-drop task

Date: 2026-10-19. **Audited statement user-accepted at GA-G1.** This consolidates
existing accepted task/model choices; it introduces no new traffic law.
It supplies the assumption subsection for the planned compact report, not
that full report or new physical/numerical validation.

## Task and comparison definitions

**TD1 — road and initial population.** A permanent three-to-two lane drop
has an ending lane E, adjacent continuing lane A and far continuing lane B.
A finite cohort initially occupies the same longitudinal footprint in all
lanes, with a uniform density within each lane and independently specified
lane loads. A and B need not be equal. Preparation is followed by a finite
terminal ending at the choke. The terminal and recovery history start empty;
there are no later external arrivals.

**TD2 — decision and fixed data.** At the decision, choose one bounded,
constant common speed advice for the entire cohort. Initial traffic, past
loading, geometry and physical parameters remain fixed when that advice
varies. The task is deterministic and has no subsequent probing or feedback
decision. Free traffic follows the selected advice-response law; queued
boundary motion may differ.

**TD3 — completion and objective.** Completion occurs at the choke. Count
all initial E/A/B population-time through complete exit, including the
entire A/E waiting tails. A completed E entry may still restrict unfinished
followers, but its own post-choke travel is outside this local objective.

**IC1 — information comparison.** All predictors share the physical model,
parameters, action set and actually admitted predecision observations, with
all lawful inference allowed. The central comparison gives one predictor
E and combined A+B information and another the individual lane loads.
Realized adjacent load, a quantity revealing it, or future service/history
is not smuggled into common inputs. Informative permitted histories remain
informative cases; concealment is not assumed for every case.

**IC2 — equal-information peer.** Under this uniform initialization and
known empty terminal/history, adding one initial adjacent-load statistic
reconstructs the three lane loads. That compact predictor is an obligatory
peer of the explicit per-lane predictor. Initial information sufficiency
does not eliminate the history subsequently generated by the dynamics.

## Global modeling assumptions

**MA1 — aggregate representation and homogeneous preparation.** Represent
traffic as continuous vehicle-equivalent mass. Use triangular-diagram,
first-order boundary/travel/storage ingredients with a homogeneous
preparation closure. Resolve lane loads and constrained exchange, without
individual maneuvers or longitudinal variation inside the preparation body.
This is not a general spatial reconstruction of merging traffic or an
exact reduction of a published multilane CTM.

**MA2 — state-mediated advice response.** Common advice sets unblocked
motion and the free-flow branch of the selected triangular-diagram family.
Wave parameter, jam density, geometry and physical interaction/recovery
coefficients stay fixed. Advice changes merge service through the resulting
traffic state and marks; changing advice alone cannot rescale an unchanged
local interaction. The work coefficient's reference anchor stays fixed.

**MA3 — restricted two-stage lane coupling.** Before the cutoff, E can
transfer into A subject to own-A demand and residual receiving. The selected
physical interaction-length law determines transfer opportunity. A successful
early transfer becomes ordinary A traffic; it carries no separate forced-E
work or recovery history. No ordinary lateral transfer occurs in the
terminal. Residual E merges at the choke through one shared A/E service
budget. B has separate service; no other lateral exchange is represented.

**MA4 — aggregate point-service closure.** Use the selected fixed-coefficient
E maneuver/mismatch work law, free-advice and queued equilibrium boundary
marks, and current-state priority/cooperation rule. A fitting free A offer
has priority; queued A uses cooperation and unused-work redistribution.
The specified shortfall, actual-zero and equality conventions determine
queue transitions. Representative marks are modeling assumptions, not
measured whole-lane speeds. A can queue; no universal free-A condition is
imposed. Undisturbed receiving is a fixed ceiling, while actual shared
service depends on the predicted recovery history.

**MA5 — E-only recovery with retained recent-mass history.** Only forced
E discharge deposits the chosen transient service-loss profile. A affects
E service/marks and consumes the shared budget but deposits no separate
profile. Entries contribute according to their fraction of a fixed
one-vehicle-equivalent mass scale, including before that amount has passed.
Retain at most that latest E mass; new entries retire oldest weight while
all profiles recover with physical age. This is an aggregate renewal
assumption. The current budget alone is insufficient dynamic state; the
law retains relevant age/mark history and does not preserve the strictest
past restriction.

## Domain and additional conditions

Use the exact sufficient domain in AS1, Section 1: positive physical scales,
free initial densities, ordinary combined E/A underload, separate B service,
conservative terminal storage and a strictly monotone service root over
the full advice interval. These bounds protect homogeneous preparation and
complete forward continuation. They do not establish general upstream
spillback, arbitrary arrival histories or a calibrated traffic regime.

Matched-history ambiguity needs its stated loading conditions. The positive
finite-action certificate has further large-work/weak-mismatch/fast-recovery
conditions and keeps A free; the coupled-A-delay witness is separate.
Ordinary complete derivatives require their stated regular branches,
event crossings and history cutoffs. Forward-admitted nonregular cases and
common-action outcomes remain in the task.

Conservation, positive service, clearance, compact-peer equality and the
conditional information/action results are deductions to check under these
definitions and assumptions. Useful numerical gradients, learning difficulty,
optimizer superiority and applied physical validity are not assumed.
