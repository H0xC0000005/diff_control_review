The verification asks whether a small initial lane statistic changes useful
common-speed advice, and whether the simulator gradient carries that
information correctly through complete TST. The selected conditional traffic
model is the evaluation world. Another simulator is an optional transfer
extension, not a requirement for this first standalone task.

## Fixed task and information

Use the selected H1/T1, S-E-P, R-E, O-R and W-F law and the whole original
advice interval. Freeze initial traffic, geometry, coefficients and one-
vehicle-equivalent renewal normalization while advice varies. Preserve the
uniform initial footprint, initial A/B symmetry, empty terminal/history,
no postdecision arrivals, finite storage and completion at the choke.
Complete TST includes all affected initial mass and both A/E waiting tails.
Retired recovery weight never removes traffic from accounting.

All cases must satisfy the retained analytical admission bounds. Disclose
whether parameter values are analytical, source supported or empirically
identified. No empirical status is inferred from mathematical admissibility.
No coefficient, speed normalization, observation window or objective endpoint
is fitted separately to an advice or desired result.

| Role | Permitted information | Required comparison meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **A: aggregate history** | Every admitted lane-blind predecision density, flow, speed and cumulative-count channel; shared geometry, parameters and preparation-family description. | Permit any lawful deterministic inference/history state and disclosed uncertainty or prior. Identical admitted histories cannot be given hidden member labels. |
| **A+E: compact augmentation** | A's inputs plus current initial E mass. | Reconstruct lane densities and autonomously predict the full selected law, including recovery. No future service or post-action E trace is supplied. |
| **G: explicit lane state** | Exactly the same information as A+E, encoded by the initial lane state. | Match A+E's objective, state predictions and defined sensitivities. No superiority over the sufficient compact encoding is presumed. |

The stronger exact-history construction is a conditional observer certificate,
not a field detector design. If any actually permitted channel identifies E
mass, A may exploit it; report aggregate sufficiency instead of suppressing
the inference. No probability distribution over ambiguous preparations is
required for the pairwise impossibility result. A chosen risk/prior policy
must be disclosed and fixed before its action comparison; the theorem does
not choose one on the aggregate observer's behalf.

## Separate questions and acceptance layers

| Layer | Required evidence before interpreting the next claim | Failure or limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Forward task correctness** | Independent reference/hand cases; mass and lane-transfer conservation; sending/receiving and finite storage; correct source/mark/queue guards; complete cohort clearance; agreement of departure-integral and travel-plus-queue TST. | A failed forward law blocks derivative interpretation. Correct AD cannot repair it. |
| **Information and reconstruction** | Reproduce the admitted aggregate-history equality and the initial E reconstruction. A+E/G predict autonomously with identical inputs. | A newly informative permitted channel changes the interpretation to aggregate sufficiency. A hidden future trace or unequal input invalidates attribution. |
| **Action relevance** | Evaluate the complete objectives and feasible action changes in the same selected task, including boundary advice and all declared cases. Report pairwise regret or certified near-optimality against a strong scalar reference. | Different residual E, clearance or prediction values do not alone prove different useful actions. Common fastest advice is a valid result. |
| **Gradient correctness** | Compare the continuous analytical/event-aware reference, a converged independently checked forward model's directional differences, and its AD derivative separately. Record event signatures and complete-clearance accounting. | AD/difference agreement on one inaccurate discretization is insufficient. Frozen clocks/history, smoothing or excluded forward boundaries change the contract. |
| **Within-task gradient usefulness** | Before interpreting an optimizer, check predicted directions against complete-TST changes from an independently checked evaluation. Use feasible perturbations and error bounds; then compare the selected advice with same-information references. | Gradient existence or a decrease in its own training loss alone is insufficient. A correct derivative can still fail the proposed finite step or be irrelevant to choosing a common optimum. |
| **Optimizer advantage, optional stronger claim** | Same task/model/information, objective, feasible interval, initialization and disclosed computation resources; count objective calls, derivative work and accuracy. | Direct search or finite differences matching AD does not invalidate information-sensitive guidance. No AD-necessity or speedup claim follows automatically. |

These references evaluate the defined task independently of the optimizer;
they do not constitute an independent traffic world. No external behavioral
validation, real-freeway benefit or minimum discrete-order requirement is claimed.

## Reference cases: each has a distinct job

1. **Neutral/free-passage controls.** Use no E, or the already proved free-
   admission condition. Retain the new exact common-action interval in the
   matched family even with positive baseline maneuver burden. Check the
   travel-only objective and its derivative analytically.
2. **Deleted-mechanism reference.** The archived fixed-work H1/T1 law has an
   exact sustained-queue objective and derivative in its declared branch.
   It is a reference/rival, not the current law. Do not transplant its
   large-work action split or small-work classification without qualification.
3. **Current-law information/action existence.** The new uniform comparison
   bound supplies a nonempty range with positive mismatch and finite positive
   recovery where one common slowdown has opposite utility for the matched
   pair. It establishes conditional possibility, not default task settings,
   opposite infinitesimal slopes or an optimizer performance result.
4. **Consequential coupled-delay/renewal reference.** Retain the earlier exact
   complete H1 family with A waiting and active old-weight retirement. It
   checks mechanisms absent from a pure E queue. It is a separate parameter
   family from the new information/action existence proof; no joint witness
   or preferred action is inferred.
5. **Regular and nonregular event diagnostics.** Include arrival front/rear,
   H1 branch changes, A/E queue events, active mismatch/profile changes,
   renewal fill and passage of source/mark events through the old cutoff.
   Retain forward-admitted grazing and coincident cases without promising
   ordinary gradients at them.

These are analytical jobs for a later proposal, not permission to run them.
Main evaluation cases must be fixed without screening on hidden preferred
actions, derivative signs or whether the richer predictor wins. Report the
full case/admission accounting, including common-action and negative results.
Calibration and held-out action evaluation remain distinct if any empirical
parameter evidence is later introduced.

## Gradient details that cannot be omitted

Advice changes preparation at fixed progress, all arrival clocks, actual
service and speed marks, and the subsequent recovery account. Use the
regular implicit service derivative with fixed physical W-F coefficients.
For recovery, retain both the moving source/mark event terms and old-rank
retirement. Keep recovery history across queue clearance.

Use an action-independent horizon beyond the proved uniform clearance bound.
The differentiated departure integral and travel-plus-A/E-queue expression
must agree. A zero queue value at an episode endpoint eliminates its endpoint
area value; it does not justify freezing the event or dropping its effect on
later traffic. The mathematical note gives the corresponding formulas.

For stable smooth event sequences, use double precision, a feasible
perturbation-step ladder and numerical resolution/refinement records. At
advice bounds use the allowed one-sided perturbation. Separate continuous-
model error, numerical forward error, derivative-estimation error and AD error.
The later execution plan must fix the solver, tolerances and error budgets
using these references and conditioning, before held-out action outcomes.
No unrelated urban numerical defaults or unsupported tolerances are adopted.

At a nonregular case, first establish the forward value and continuity/side
behavior. Report finite one-sided derivatives only when they exist. Otherwise
report an undefined/nonfinite derivative and the actual finite change. Keep
such cases in the task and scalar action assessment. No generalized-gradient
controller, smoothing or alternative queue rule is selected here. If a later
implementation requires such a choice, reopen the smallest affected contract.

Global action assessment must not assume that every coupled objective is
smooth or that its infimum is attained. A declared scalar reference needs a
certified bound/near-optimality statement, including endpoints and event
boundaries as applicable. An unproved grid minimum is reported at its
resolution, not as an exact optimum.

## Strong simpler rivals and fair attribution

| Rival | Why it is a serious comparison | Attribution boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Full aggregate history/inference | It can retain all information derivable from the admitted longitudinal record, not only current density. | Any missing-E effect is an information claim under the observer contract, not proof that aggregate models cannot control VSL. |
| Exact A+E compact predictor | It reconstructs the selected model at the same information as G. | Equality is a sufficient-state finding; representation size or full lane state need not win. |
| Fixed receiving/work and stationary-equivalent recovery reductions | They can explain some of the same mean discharge with less state or history. Preserve their exact limiting cases and transient limitations. | A different constitutive law is a model-form comparison, not a pure information test. Do not label it source-exact unless qualified. |
| Literature aggregate/history/capacity-drop models | Wei--Han--Wang, Jin--Jin and Hegyi/METANET show capable aggregate mechanisms; Carey and Nagalur Subraveti provide continuous lane-aware rivals. | Reuse the accepted source screen. Any future adopted source model requires faithful equations/action and parameter qualification; no hybrid straw baseline is created. |
| Analytical sensitivity, finite-difference guidance and bounded scalar search | This is one bounded constant-advice decision. An independent action search and exact limiting cases are strong accuracy references. | Match information and resources. Useful gradients need not be uniquely necessary or faster. |

Published VSL cost-variation methods remain prior art, including Delle
Monache, Piccoli and Rossi's different outflow-tracking task. Novelty of VSL,
gradients or lane-aware continuous traffic is not a premise of this protocol.

## Outcome interpretation fixed before execution

| Outcome | Direct interpretation |
|---|---|
| An admitted aggregate observation identifies E | That observation is sufficient for this task; allow the inference. |
| Matched histories, different complete-time predictions, common best advice | Prediction ambiguity without a required action distinction in that regime. |
| Matched histories and incompatible near-optimal actions | Conditional control value of the omitted information within the defined law. |
| A+E and G agree | The compact initial statistic is sufficient; no fuller representation advantage follows. |
| Continuous references, refined differences and AD agree | Correct implemented sensitivity in the declared regular/event regime. |
| Predicted feasible changes improve independently checked complete TST | Within-task direction usefulness, bounded by the step and event regime. |
| Correct local gradient gives a poor finite step | Step/global-action limitation; do not reinterpret derivative correctness as optimizer success. |
| A scalar search or simpler model matches the result | No stronger optimizer or state-necessity claim. Retain the simpler explanation. |
| A nonregular point has no finite derivative | A limitation to report with the actual finite action cost, not a forward case to discard. |
| No physical/SUMO evidence is supplied | Keep the standalone result; applied MP6 and external transfer remain unestablished. |
