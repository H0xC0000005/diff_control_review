## What is being qualified

The primary model retains homogeneous H1 preparation, finite T1 terminal
storage and eligibility, S-E equilibrium speed marks, S-E-P current-state
priority/cooperation, W-F immediate work, R-E's E-only recovery source and
O-R recent-mass renewal. Completion remains at the choke line. The single
shared account may retain enough predicted history to update itself.

The physical question is whether these restrictions can describe the same
lane drop over the same advice interval. Separate fits that each explain
one speed or one component do not answer that question. The conditional
forward-domain proof is an input; physical evidence must meet its retained
bounds as well as explain the observations. No bound is weakened here.

## How observations distinguish the mechanisms

The following order avoids using an incorrect speed or recovery law to
manufacture an apparently successful immediate-work fit. It is an evidence
dependency order, not a requirement to estimate every parameter in a
statistically independent experiment.

| Part of the traffic story | Informative development evidence | What would be supported; what would remain open |
|---|---|---|
| **Undisturbed motion and receiving** | Physical spacing/headways, free motion under the common advice, a discharge observation with enough demand to expose service, and transient propagation where wave speed is used. | Constrains geometry, jam density, wave-speed meaning and undisturbed receiving. Underloaded outflow is only a lower bound on service. A stationary headway match alone does not identify transient wave speed. |
| **Preparation before the fixed cutoff** | Lane counts and realized lateral transfers along the homogeneous preparation window, for known initial conditions and advice. Establish whether donor demand or adjacent receiving limits each informative segment. | An unconstrained decay segment can constrain the opportunity length; a receiving-limited segment then constrains its receiving penalty. If only their product is visible, report that ambiguity. A scripted attempt is not evidence that a transfer succeeded. |
| **Speeds used at forced entry** | Actual E insertion speeds and relevant A passage speeds, together with lane discharge and free/queued state. Use a disclosed observation scale and replicate/aggregation treatment in the later execution plan. | Tests the free-advice and queued-equilibrium marks. It does not assume advice equals realized speed during native acceleration, nor that a time-averaged flow determines an individual speed. Persistent systematic speed error is a constitutive failure, not a cost-calibration opportunity. |
| **Restriction left by admitted E** | E admission times and insertion-speed observations, plus queued A discharge after E stops. Additional histories with different E spacing and speeds test overlap and retirement. | An A-only saturated tail exposes the account's residual service without current E work. It can constrain recovery and challenge recent-mass renewal. A single tail need not identify all parameters or establish renewal under overlapping entries. |
| **Additional work during mixed admission** | Binding mixed A/E service intervals after recovery has been constrained, with distinct A-flow/speed-gap combinations. | Separates baseline maneuver burden from mismatch dependence when the covariate varies. Nonbinding intervals provide feasibility inequalities, not a measured work equality. The total observed lane-changing headway must not be copied as an extra factor if it already includes the modeled recovery burden. |
| **The complete joined explanation** | New development conditions spanning the supported free/queued transitions, mixtures and advice, followed by held-out reference evaluation after freezing. | One set must predict preparation, speed marks, mixed departures and recovery together. Mechanism-level errors cannot be hidden by a good total-TST score. A numerical fit alone does not establish causation or global identifiability. |

The mathematical note derives the conditional tail equality, the two-column
work-identification test, preparation-scale confounding and the stationary
headway correspondence. These are deductions about the selected model, not
empirical results or a promise that the required informative observations
exist in the primary task.

## Physical parameters and retained restrictions

- **Preparation opportunity and receiving penalty:** common across advice.
  Their spatial interpretation is retained; no return to an unqualified
  fixed completion proportion per second is inferred.
- **Speed marks:** the present flow-to-speed relation remains an equilibrium
  assumption to test. The recovery square-root intermediate quantity is
  not a vehicle-speed trajectory. Actual bounded acceleration constrains
  the source interpretation of the acceleration parameter, while discharge
  tests its boundary consequence. It is not silently relabeled a free
  per-advice tuning coefficient.
- **Immediate work:** the fixed dimensional mismatch coefficient is retained.
  The original reference anchor fixes its units once; changing advice or a
  search box must not change it. Baseline extra work and mismatch dependence
  must satisfy the existing nonnegative and monotone-root domain conditions.
- **Recovery:** the selected physical acceleration and one-vehicle-equivalent
  recent-mass normalization are retained. The latter is not a discretization
  bin or an additional freely fitted window. An observed need for a different
  physical window requires reopening O-R's G3 contract.
- **Storage and workload:** retain ordinary underload, initially empty
  terminal/recovery, independent initial E/A/B loads with actual total mass
  as accepted at AS1, finite cohort and the sufficient
  H1-protection bounds over the entire original advice interval. These are
  declared restrictions, not claims about all freeway traffic.

Source illustration values and the earlier long-geometry/slow-recovery
analytical witness are not a jointly supported numerical range. This pass
selects no task parameters, sampling windows, error tolerances or fit losses.
Those must be concrete, justified and frozen before held-out action results
in the later execution proposal. Observations must discriminate the mechanisms
at the declared scale; merely loosening error tolerances is not admission.

## Avoiding duplicate effects without changing the model

The selected formula already subtracts recovery from the shared budget
before allocating current work. Consequently, an immediate-work estimate
must explain the **remaining mixed-entry burden conditional on that budget**.
Adding the recovery profile's integrated loss to the maneuver factor would
charge its effect again. This is accounting for the existing formula, not
a newly selected physical decomposition.

The physical decomposition still needs evidence. An A-only tail constrains
post-entry restriction, distinct mixed conditions constrain remaining work,
and a joint prediction checks that neither term merely compensates for the
other. Diagnostic comparisons that omit one effect, and a faithful compact
capacity-drop rival, remain useful. They are comparison candidates, not
substitutions for the selected primary or newly authorized experiments.

The evidence can also challenge R-E: after conditioning on the admitted E
history, a systematic extra dependence on A acceleration/history could
expose an omitted source or an invalid boundary reduction. It does not
automatically justify an A penalty. Similarly, different residual service
under indistinguishable retained histories could challenge sufficient state.
These findings would be reported before any scientific change.

## Information, independence and outcome discipline

Development observations may include native individual trajectories. They
are used to qualify common physical parameters and constitutive assumptions.
They do not become a future service oracle, replayed model output, or extra
initial information supplied only to the richer controller. Every predictor
must subsequently generate its own future state from its admitted inputs.

The chosen SUMO role is independent native motion and merging. A development
script may express lawful intentions under the eventual bridge contract; it
must not prescribe successful transfer, crossing speed, shared discharge or
recovery to manufacture agreement. Additional diagnostic inputs can be
applied in development after a separately approved plan; they do not expand
the primary finite-cohort control task.

Freeze one common physical model and parameter set before using held-out
preferred actions, objective derivatives or policy improvement. Do not fit
to a desired derivative sign, introduce an external slowing seed, change
the observation window, or require an interior optimum. Development admission
must be based on physical criteria without selecting only cases favorable
to an information or optimizer claim. Report the full admission accounting.

The conditional E non-requeue theorem relies on the monotone H1 pulse,
selected free-speed marks, recovering budget and remaining primary bounds,
not just finiteness. Bursty native finite-cohort requeueing is a possible
model discrepancy, not an automatic native-evaluator failure. Its material
effect on the intended comparison must be evaluated honestly later.
