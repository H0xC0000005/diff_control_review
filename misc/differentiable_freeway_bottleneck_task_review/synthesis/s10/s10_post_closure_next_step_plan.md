## Purpose

Continue the freeway-control line without reopening the closed permanent-lane-
drop conclusions or treating one branch's negative result as evidence for
another. The sequence prioritizes a physically meaningful VSL extension, retains
an analytical clarification route, and moves to ramp metering only after the VSL
line has a documented stopping reason.

“Positive” means that a branch satisfies its predeclared claim and evidence
contract. It does not mean that every stage must support added mesoscopic state.
A strong aggregate-sufficiency result, a bounded obstruction or a defensible
inconclusive result is retained as evidence rather than tuned away.

## Step 1 — Temporary lane-closure recovery with VSL

### Admission question

Can a fixed, exogenous lane reopening create a source-supported path from hidden
near-zone lane/gap/order configuration to recovery discharge and a different
useful upstream VSL decision under the same declared strong aggregate history?

### First review boundary

Open only a methodology/task-admission review. It must:

- fix closure and reopening times independently of control;
- retain lane-uniform infrastructure speed advice;
- define recovery and all-demand accounting, including residual queues;
- identify a source-supported state-to-discharge mechanism rather than assume a
  favorable post-reopening capacity;
- retain the strong aggregate history/observer alternative; and
- separate event-sensitive forward behavior, decision relevance and gradients.

Stop at a user gate before a full protocol, equation set or experiment.

### Outcome-dependent handoff

| Admission/result | Next action |
|---|---|
| No defensible hidden-state-to-recovery mechanism | Close the branch; consider Step 2 only if it has an independently supported permanent-drop mechanism. |
| Recovery matters, but strong aggregate history captures it | Record bounded aggregate sufficiency for this task; Step 2 is optional and must justify a distinct mechanism. |
| Hidden state changes recovery but not the useful VSL decision | Record forward-resolution value without control value; Step 2 may test the different analytical coupling. |
| Hidden state changes useful VSL | Continue with minimum-state review, then gradient review; do not fall back merely because gradients are not yet established. |
| Decision relevance exists but reopening gradients are unsuitable | Review hybrid/nonsmooth sensitivity or a non-gradient controller; do not label the information result negative. |
| The exact evidence contract is infeasible or inconclusive | Step 2 may clarify the logical mechanism without claiming native evidence. |
