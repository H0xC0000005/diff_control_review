## Smallest meaningful proposed diagnostic

Reviewer hypothesis, not a finding: compare matched demand/geometry/advice and
matched observed aggregate state while altering one omitted variable—fixed
response composition OR lane occupancy near a forced merge. Does a feasible
advice perturbation's ranking change in an independently calibrated evaluator?
A mean-response/history-aware B0 must be included before attributing failure to
cohorts/lanes. No new contrast was simulated here. Matching observed aggregate
state does not match its entire future history; the diagnostic must explicitly
state which history is retained and which variable is deliberately omitted.

Cohorts and lanes are two information axes, not proven sequential necessities.
A lane-only intermediate might be needed for attribution; it is a proposed
G2/G5 refinement, not permission to add a fourth construction component now.
If simpler dynamics explain the contrast, that is evidence against richer state,
not a reason to weaken B0. If no decision reversal/benefit survives, stop or
choose a substantive sufficiency question rather than inventing a new algorithm.
