## Why SUMO appeared in the proposed evidence chain

SUMO was assigned one narrow role: act as an independently implemented richer
reference whose native car-following and lane-changing dynamics generate complete
trajectories. A bounded run could test whether the frozen preparation bank
actually contains two trajectories that are exactly identical through the
declared lane-blind history yet differ in hidden lane state. A later, separately
planned action experiment could test whether those states have different useful
common-advice choices.

That is empirical existence evidence for one versioned synthetic domain. It is
not required to assess whether the proposed methodology is logically sound, and
even a positive run would not establish a minimum state, a general traffic
theorem, field validity or useful differentiable-model gradients.

## What can be achieved without execution?

| Method | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Static documentation and literature audit | Whether the provider exposes the required actuator/state concepts; whether published mechanisms make the causal chain plausible; whether the proposed inference exceeds the sources. | The realized trajectories, exact full-history equality, action scores or occurrence frequency in the frozen case. |
| Static source-code/algorithm audit | Version-specific dependency paths, anticipation variables, default semantics, determinism requirements and structural reasons a proposed equality may be impossible or fragile. | Usually not the exact numerical trajectory or action ranking without executing/fully formalizing the hybrid program. |
| Analytical conservative traffic construction | A transparent conditional existence proof or obstruction under stated equations; isolation of the minimum mechanism needed to break fixed-service arrival dominance. | Native-SUMO behavior, empirical calibration, field relevance or claims beyond the stated model class. |
| Observability/identifiability analysis | Conditions under which hidden lane state is recoverable or non-recoverable from the declared aggregate history; clarification of unknown-input ambiguity. | A ready-made result for an unspecified hybrid lane-changing model or useful control consequences without a service/objective model. |
| Existing trajectory/intervention data | Potentially test the information and decision links without installing SUMO, if complete matched histories, hidden lane state, common interventions and all-cohort accounting exist. | The required claim when those measurements/interventions are absent; ordinary observational data cannot manufacture the counterfactual action menu. |
| Rich-reference execution | A reproducible bounded existence/failure result under one frozen provider/version and scenario. | General impossibility, minimum sufficient representation, differentiable gradient correctness or cross-fidelity usefulness by itself. |

## Methodological judgment

For the current review, **static and analytical methods are the appropriate
tools**. They can finish the methodology product by:

1. auditing the claim/evidence chain and the exact observer/input boundary;
2. deriving necessary conditions for hidden information to survive full history;
3. deriving the advice-dependent merge/service condition needed to escape the
   fixed-service obstruction;
4. separating what a positive, negative or invalid future experiment would mean;
5. specifying the smallest candidate refinement and general gradient path only
   conditionally, without implementing it.

Static documents alone cannot truthfully complete the empirical claim that an
exact native pair exists or that it changes useful advice. An analytical result
can replace that empirical claim only by making its model assumptions explicit
and limiting the conclusion to that model class. The correct methodology-only
outcome may therefore be a complete conditional contract plus a clearly marked
future evidence obligation.

## Available methodology-only continuations

### Route M1 — close with a static methodological sufficiency audit

Audit whether S08's proposed future experiment would answer the intended bounded
claim, enumerate confounds and interpretation limits, and archive it without
executing. **Pros:** stays wholly within review and prevents overclaiming.
**Cons:** leaves the information-to-decision existence claim empirical and open.

### Route M2 — analytical certificate or obstruction

Reopen the earlier analytical route only to specify the smallest conservative
shared-advice/merge/service model and seek a conditional proof or obstruction.
**Pros:** may bridge the conceptual information-to-decision link transparently
without a simulator. **Cons:** requires a substantive model contract; choosing
rates or penalties to force the result would be circular, and the result would
not certify native or field behavior.

### Route M3 — static provider source audit

Inspect a fixed SUMO source version without installing or running it, focusing on
anticipation, desired-speed response, lane-changing state and output semantics.
**Pros:** sharpens native feasibility and may rule out some preparation ideas.
**Cons:** is version-specific and unlikely by itself to prove exact numerical
history collision or decision separation.

These routes may be combined as M1 plus a bounded M2 or M3, but combination does
not upgrade their conclusions to empirical evidence. No continuation is selected
by this clarification.

## Recommendation

Treat S08 as a fully specified **future empirical protocol**, not the next action
of this review. For the present methodology-only scope, use M1 as the closure
baseline and add M2 only if a conditional analytical bridge is worth the extra
modeling assumptions. M3 is useful only if a source-level native-feasibility
question remains decisive after M1. SUMO execution is best only when the research
objective explicitly changes from methodology review to bounded empirical
existence testing.
