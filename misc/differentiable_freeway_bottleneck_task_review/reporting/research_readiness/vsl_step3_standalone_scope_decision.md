# Standalone VSL task: SUMO becomes an optional extension

Date: 2026-10-19. **User-approved scope amendment. Step 3 remains open.**

The current formulation defines its own traffic world: initial lane mass and
common advice determine preparation, forced merging, recovery, departures and
complete travel time. It is sufficient to begin asking whether retained
information changes a good decision and whether the full-objective gradient
guides that decision correctly inside this task. Reproducing the task in SUMO
is a further test of transfer, rather than a prerequisite for this first study.

This is a change to the evidence scope, not a new traffic model. The selected
conditional forward law, action interval, initial information, finite cohort,
local completion line and complete TST are unchanged. The elementary companion
role alongside the closed urban task is retained.

## The approved change and its claim boundary

The user explicitly removes the SUMO ladder from the work required before
Step 3 closes. The model-to-S-P-to-S-L ladder and its sources remain available
as an optional future extension or revision. No SUMO correspondence review,
native admission or transfer result is required for the current standalone
protocol. The earlier S3-M1 selection is preserved as the strategy for that
optional extension; it is no longer a mandatory stage or an unresolved choice.

This amends G1/G4 and VQ4 only as needed to make the standalone task the current
verification target. It does not change the project-wide requirement to
distinguish forward correctness, gradient correctness and decision usefulness.
Within-task usefulness still needs evidence about actual complete-time changes
and action quality, checked against independent analytical or numerical
references and strong simpler rivals. Differentiability, a nonzero gradient
or a decreasing internal optimization loss is insufficient on its own.

Applied MP6 remains open: no jointly supported physical parameter range or
empirical traffic validity is established. Its physical admission contract is
retained for a future applied claim. For the present task, mathematical domain
admission and transparent assumptions remain mandatory; physical-validation
gaps bound the claim rather than block the standalone review. No external
traffic-usefulness or gradient-transfer result follows from this amendment.

   the A/A+E/G information ladder against the selected coupled law; distinguish
   hidden-state prediction error from a changed preferred action; specify
   complete-TST sensitivity references and treatment of moving arrivals,
   recovery, queue transitions and final clearance. Preserve full aggregate
   history, scalar sufficiency, strong model-form and optimizer rivals, and
   common-action or negative outcomes. Earlier S13 or predecessor certificates
   do not automatically establish these results for the current law.
2. **Consistency and the user closure gate.** Consolidate the resulting
   standalone comparison protocol, evidence layers, outcome interpretation and
   scientific limits. Audit them against the fixed model and information
   contract, then present the planned review result and stop for user review.
   Step 4 and any implementation or experiment require their own later opening.

The first block is the immediate next substantive review. This amendment and
blocker check do not execute that review or mark its questions answered.
