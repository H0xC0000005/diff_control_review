20 and A+B 80, with A/B equal to 60/20 or 20/60. Both level-1 and level-2

numbers are an explanation, not an admitted scenario or action certificate.

## Why this is a sound and structurally small direction

In the subsequent discussion, the user reaffirms the level-2 rival: it knows
E and combined continuing traffic, while the true initial A/B split is hidden.
The current equations provide no reason that this contrast must be excluded.
They already distinguish the adjacent receiver from the far lane. Different
adjacent loads can change spare receiving, subsequent transfer and forced
service even with the same E and continuing totals. This directly tests the
information discarded by grouping two continuing lanes.

Initial symmetry was convenient for the earlier sufficient-scalar reference;
it is not required by the choice to keep each lane homogeneous along the
approach. The natural first route preserves the existing motion, transfer,
terminal, work and recovery laws. No extra physical mechanism or longitudinal
resolution is needed merely to specify unequal initial lane loads. This is
structurally lightweight, subject to checking the admitted domain and proofs.

There are nevertheless two changes, not just a masking operation. The true
initial-state family must allow independently specified A/B loads, and the
level-2 observer must receive only their combined information. If the physical
family still imposes equality, hiding the two lane values conceals nothing.
Derived quantities that identify adjacent mass must not be leaked as shared
inputs. A lawful estimate or uncertainty model inferred from the admitted
history remains a serious rival, rather than being forced to use a known-
wrong equal split.

The remaining work is bounded: generalize initialization/mass bookkeeping and
check the existing per-lane and combined-load bounds; qualify indistinguishable
level-2 observations; then test complete-time prediction, useful actions and
gradients. This is not an argument that asymmetry necessarily changes the best
advice. A compact added adjacent-lane statistic may reconstruct the three
initial lane masses and should remain an equal-information competitor.

The user has clarified the target; no reason is found to retain initial
