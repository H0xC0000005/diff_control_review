## Assumptions and scientific limits

The candidate assumes a common fixed finite footprint, no later arrivals,
initial continuing symmetry, uniform local exchange, a fixed physical
interaction scale, a declared progress-based rate offer and a triangular
free-flow response family over a bounded action range. Homogeneous invariance
is proved only while boundary holding does not disturb that motion.
These are proposed H1 adaptations, not calibrated laws or a frozen contract.

Binding early lateral service need not cause longitudinal waiting because
unmerged traffic may advance. Binding compulsory service at the permanent
lane end is different: it may hold traffic and affect the upstream state.
This is the principal remaining joint-domain risk. There is no proof that
every homogeneous compact holding approximation fails, and no instruction
to return to spatial H2/B automatically.

Applied validity, rate-scale provenance, a fresh reachable observer witness,
gradient correctness and independent decision usefulness remain unresolved.
No positive information gap was required or manufactured.
