# The selected point model now has a complete conditional forward evolution

Date: 2026-10-16. **W-F selected; J4-W1 closed. The joined forward review
is complete at WF-Q1, an evidence checkpoint. Overall Step 3 remains open.**

The main advance is that the point model can now be followed from the first
arrival through complete clearance **for every permitted constant advice in
the original elementary domain**. The analytical argument no longer covers
only the specially constructed cohort near the upper advice. This is
mathematical qualification under the declared traffic laws, not evidence
that their speed marks or parameter scales reproduce a real lane drop.

## What the selected model means for traffic

E's immediate merging work responds to the passing A flow and the modeled
speed difference. The coefficient is fixed. When A flows freely, lowering
its actual speed can make entry easier for queued E in the local comparison.
When both streams are already queued below the speed signs, changing an
inactive sign alone no longer rescales their interaction.

The same point component still has two linked effects: a maneuver consumes
current shared service, and a slow admitted E stream can leave a temporary
recovery restriction for following traffic. Recent E admissions refresh that
one recovery account. This predicted memory is part of local traffic state;
the model receives no future receiving trace or future reference speed.

W-F is now selected as an exact expression for conditional qualification.
Its particular flow/speed formula remains an adaptation. Literature supports
extra receiving space for lane-changing traffic and treats priority as a
separate modeling choice; it does not provide our complete node.
[Carey, Balijepalli and Watling, author manuscript](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/77315/7/Carey%20et%20al%202013_with_coversheet.pdf)

## Why the full forward check becomes manageable

The accepted H1 setting supplies one finite arrival pulse. During that pulse,
E arrivals decrease as preparation succeeds and A arrivals increase by the
same amount. Their combined ordinary flow remains below undisturbed service.
The pulse ends once the initial cohort has become eligible to leave.

That simple structure gives more than a convenient example. It limits the
queue events that the selected model can produce.

**E can wait from the front, but cannot keep clearing and re-forming.** If
the first arriving mixture fits the undisturbed work budget, later mixtures
also fit: they contain less E maneuver work. Both streams stay free and the
recovery account stays neutral. Otherwise the first conflict makes E wait,
with free A receiving its selected priority.

Once E clears, new E arrivals have the free speed mark and create no new
recovery loss. Older loss only ages or is retired, so service recovers. At
the same time E's offered arrivals keep falling. Its free offer therefore
continues to fit, whether A is still queued or has also cleared. There is
at most one E waiting episode in this primary model.

**A may have several waiting episodes, but they cannot accumulate infinitely
fast.** A first queues when its offered flow no longer fits the current
budget. Cooperation then gives it half of that budget while E is queued.
For A to clear, its offer must fit that smaller cooperative flow. It therefore
returns to free passage with a positive service margin. A later queue birth
requires that margin to disappear. The derived positive budget bound and
bounded rates of change impose a positive separation between repeated A
events. This is a consequence of the selected allocation, not a new timer,
latch or numerical queue threshold.

These findings do not say that physical jams are stationary or that waiting
mass alone determines road traffic. T1 still distinguishes physical stock
from mass eligible for downstream service. The argument concerns the
conservative terminal balances and the selected boundary rules.

## What now follows for information and gradients

With total initial mass, the initial ending statistic, continuing symmetry
and known empty terminal/recovery history, the model predicts the entire
subsequent state for each advice. The compact observer and explicit lane
representation therefore have the same forward prediction throughout this
conditional domain. Recent recovery history is generated internally; it is
not an additional initial measurement. This is not a claim that one current
scalar reading is sufficient to update the recovery account.

The prior pre-decision aggregate-history construction is unaffected by the
terminal substitution: its loading remains upstream and the terminal is
empty at the decision. That preserves an information ambiguity under those
declared channels. It does not by itself prove different preferred actions
or useful gradient directions under the new joined law.

There is also a useful negative control. **Remove immediate extra maneuver
work, and ordinary underload keeps the whole primary cohort free.** The
speed-difference term and recovery source never activate because both streams
initially pass at the same free speed. Complete time then favors fastest
advice for every preparation. This is a conclusion of the present model,
not a claim that maneuver work is universally the only cause of congestion.

The queue-selected speed mark can jump when a queue starts. At a critical
front that just fits freely, the alternative queued branch can already have
strictly lower E service. Consequently, we cannot assume a smooth objective
across queue activation merely because forward evolution is unique. The
next sensitivity work must preserve the declared event distinctions and
strong scalar-search/capacity-drop rivals. No full-objective discontinuity,
AD result or policy advantage is asserted from this local finding alone.
Discontinuous boundary service has a literature precedent with its own
selection rule and proof; that precedent does not validate this one.
[Jin, Gan and Lebacque (2013 preprint), Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.2660)
