## 2. Comparable task design; different depth of model specification

At the **task and comparison-design level**, the urban work is substantial
and comparable: it has a concrete junction, physical action, complete
objective, mechanism-isolating variants, strong simpler rivals, lawful
comparison rules and separate evidence layers. It should not be restarted
from task selection.

At the **complete-model and compact-report level**, it has not reached the
same state as the finalized VSL report. These are the main differences:

| Concern | What is already established | What remains for a report at the later VSL standard |
|---|---|---|
| **Concrete traffic laws** | The action, service-capacity map, mass accounting and objective are explicit. The formal contract names ordered-prefix eligibility, bay receiving and accessibility operators and describes their causal paths. | Those operators are partly schematic. The accepted impact audit explicitly leaves ordered-access equations/Jacobians, the chosen B1 equation set and B2 storage augmentation to later plans. Recover any later approved definitions first; otherwise expose their ownership and ask only the necessary methodological questions. A report cannot silently supply them as editorial detail. |
| **Global modeling assumptions** | Continuous mass, fixed structural routes/order, continuum service, finite storage, scenario symmetry and derivative qualifications appear across the records. | Assemble an explicit root assumption set and distinguish physical assumptions, task restrictions, numerical defaults and conditions for particular claims. Check compatibility against the actual selected operators. This is recovery and consistency work first; a new consequential premise needs a decision. |
| **Information and sensitivity precision** | Aggregate-matched pairs, feasible service directions, the full state-sensitivity recursion and V0–V4 causal paths are defined. | Specify exactly what each selected rival observes or infers, and demonstrate matching at that declared level. Derive the relevant local sensitivities after the traffic operators are fixed. The existing recursion states the required chain; it does not close unspecified operator derivatives. Boundary handling must fit the actual discrete-time/event formulation, without importing VSL's saltation/history construction automatically. |
| **Provenance and readable presentation** | The literature-adoption matrix already distinguishes transcription, adaptation, composition and code reuse. The current formal report explains the task mathematically. | Put precise source/adaptation/defined-here credit beside each defining equation, introduce the traffic mental picture and symbols locally, and synchronize historical exact-order/proposal wording with the accepted decisions. An absent drop-in implementation is not a novelty or gradient-usefulness argument. |

The second and fourth rows contain substantial documentary work. The first
and dependent parts of the third may require genuine methodological choices.
Their deliberate deferral was compatible with the original review's scope;
it does not invalidate that closure. It does prevent calling the current
urban artifact a completely specified model in the same sense as VSL.

The practical starting point is therefore a short completeness audit and
bounded refinement/report plan. It should separate **already decided and
ready to write**, **recoverable from later approved records**, **needs a
scientific decision**, and **belongs to implementation/evidence**. Numerical
calibration, optimizer settings and physical validation are not prerequisites
for explaining the task. An urban analytical action certificate identical
to VSL's is also not a required reporting standard.

## 3. Agent choice: recommend a fresh urban agent

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh agent with a focused handoff — recommended** | Gives the urban work its own scope, authorities and checkpoints. Reduces accidental transfer of VSL assumptions and keeps the completed VSL effort easy to audit. The reading list preserves continuity. | Requires initial reading and status reconciliation; freshness alone does not guarantee better reasoning. |
| **Continue in this agent under a new urban assignment** | Retains the user's presentation preferences and the recent comparative inspection; less initial context transfer. | Carries a long VSL history and a different scientific remit. Requires deliberate separation of urban decisions, terminology and approval history; model-completion questions still need their proper owners/gates. |

I recommend the first approach because this is an independent task with
different model dependencies, rather than a further VSL amendment. For
example, urban effective-service fractions are not executable signal timing;
urban external-perturbation requirements are not removed by making SUMO
optional for VSL; and urban P8 does not already select exact FIFO. These
distinctions are easier to preserve in a focused urban workstream.

I have prepared a handoff and copyable opening prompt for that route. They
authorize nothing by themselves: a fresh agent starts only when the user
invokes it. Its first bounded assignment is to recover the authorities,
classify the gaps and propose the smallest refinement/report plan, stopping
before an unapproved law or order-resolution choice. This agent has not
revised the urban package, launched another agent or resumed development.
