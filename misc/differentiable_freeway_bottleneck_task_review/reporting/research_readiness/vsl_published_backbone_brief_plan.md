The approved brief is retained below.

**Question.** Which established model can supply most of the traffic
dynamics needed for our information, decision and gradient questions, with
zero or very few additions from other modeling works?

The aim is a defensible, simpler route for the colleague discussion. It is
not to prove a globally best model or defend every feature of the current
formulation.

**Work.**

1. **Recheck a small native-model shortlist.** Start with Wei–Han–Wang and
   Jin–Jin for aggregate VSL, and use the archived Carey/Subraveti evidence
   to identify the stronger lane-resolved starting point. Reuse existing
   extractions; reread primary methods only where a decision depends on
   them. Extend the search only for a specific gap in these candidates.
2. **Assess each backbone substantially intact.** State what its native
   states, laws and scenario already provide; which RQs they can answer;
   and what changes to action, observations, scenario or complete-time
   accounting would be needed. Check meaningful information comparisons,
   sensitivity limitations and known source cautions. Distinguish essential
   research requirements from contingent choices in our present model.
3. **Identify the smallest justified additions and compare routes.** First
   examine what experiment/comparison design can achieve without new traffic
   laws. If a mechanism is necessary, identify its component, source and
   interface changes. Prefer one principal model and, where needed, one
   supplementary modeling source. Explain any further source or new closure
   explicitly. Judge added assumptions and coupled validation burden, as
   well as whether the model is understandable and adequate for the RQs.

**Output.** One concise note: a closest-work/gap summary; a candidate table
showing **retained backbone → necessary changes/additions → RQs supported →
remaining burden**; and a recommendation with clear limitations. An unchanged
model or a finding that no simple route is supported is a valid result.
Identify any required task/claim reframe explicitly. This supplies material
for the accepted response ladder without drafting the email.

**Boundary.** This is one focused comparison, with no intermediate formal
checkpoints. It ends with the note. If a consequential unresolved modeling
or task choice prevents an answer, present that choice without selecting
it or constructing another hybrid. No simulator development, installation,
calibration or traffic/gradient experiments. The current model/report and
urban work remain unchanged; implementation progress does not select the
backbone. Citation count alone is not the simplicity criterion, and no
positive information gap, interior optimum or numerical usefulness is assumed.
