## Scope, evidence and result

The user selected A-H for the next review sprint and expressed no present
justification for a CTM-like short spatial model. The pass therefore tested
the strongest compact reading under the accepted S13 finite cohort and P1
own-lane-first early-merging direction. It kept the separate compulsory,
finite-service lane-end stage, common VSL, continuous mass and complete-time
objective. It did not reopen S13 or the urban task, or resume the closed ramp
line.

The review found a conditional compact longitudinal clock on S13's
synchronized free-flow branch. It also found that this clock and one
whole-zone receiver stock do not yet identify local own-lane demand or
receiving along distributed early opportunities. S13's instantaneous
batch does not declare a finite upstream passage profile. The source
literature supports homogeneous-link boundary histories and a separate
cell-local R1 rule, but the inspected papers do not prove their one-stock
composition for this task. A hand comparison showed that the same four
units of target mass can leave one or two and one-half units of receiving
work per second when its passage profile differs. The finding is a
conditional reduction limit, not a theorem against every compact model.

The report compares a deliberately homogeneous one-receiver interaction
window, a few physically local cumulative-flow interfaces and a distinct
link-queue rival. The first two are unselected Type 2 methodological
alternatives. The pass stops before selecting a cohort profile, service
law, observation contract, terminal choke law or MP6 operating domain.

## Assumptions, limitations and next allowed action

The compact travel result assumes S13's synchronized cohort, fixed geometry,
one lane-uniform advice and an uncongested common realized speed. Binding
upstream service can create waiting or differentiated arrival that invalidates
that shared clock. The hand cases are logical/arithmetical checks, not field
evidence. Neither a calibrated representative-receiver law nor an applied
common-VSL speed/merging regime has been established. Forward fidelity,
gradient correctness and independent usefulness remain untested and
scientifically separate.
