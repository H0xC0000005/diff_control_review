The target is a comparison in which E traffic and combined continuing traffic
are known, but their distribution between adjacent A and far B is not. A
crowded A can make the same E population harder to merge even when the
continuing total is unchanged. The next work should first ensure this is a
valid initial condition of the traffic world, then determine what is genuinely
hidden from the rival, and finally examine its consequences for advice.

This is a narrow amendment within the reopened Step 3, with a dependency on
G3's initial-family contract. It does not restart the completed mechanism
review or open another simulator-development milestone. Existing equations
and proofs are reused where their assumptions permit; symmetric results
remain references, not answers to the intended asymmetry question.

## First: make independent A/B loads a valid initial condition

**AS1: initialization and forward-contract requalification — review
completed and user-accepted.** Specify E, A and B initial traffic independently in the
underlying task. Each lane remains homogeneous along the same footprint;
different lane loads do not require longitudinal heterogeneity. Total mass
is counted from all three lanes, without deriving B from A by symmetry.

Audit the dependency chain from those initial values through adjacent
receiving, preparation, lane-specific arrivals, terminal service/recovery,
storage, conservation, complete clearance and TST. Keep the existing motion,
transfer, allocation and recovery laws as the candidate. Check which proofs
already depend only on separate E/A combined-load and far-B bounds, and which
used initial equality and must be amended. Verify that the admitted family
actually contains unequal A/B pairs at fixed E and continuing total.

Keep the uniform footprint, empty terminal/recovery state, finite cohort,
one common constant advice, finite storage, local completion and fixed
physical coefficients. No equalizing A/B motion, extra lateral dynamics,
spatial cells or new penalty is introduced simply to permit asymmetry.

**Output and gate:** a compact root initialization/domain amendment, a
component/proof dependency map and a clear result: the existing traffic laws
support a nonempty asymmetric family, or a specific scientific obstacle
requires a decision. Stop after this bounded review checkpoint, or earlier
if it needs a changed traffic mechanism, motion or admissibility meaning.
Do not freeze an amended forward contract by asserting that the change is
“only visibility.”

## Second: establish the information contrast fairly

**AS2: qualify what Level 2 conceals relative to Level 3 — review
completed and user-accepted.** After the forward
family is qualified, compare preparations with the same initial E and A+B
traffic but different A/B splits. Check all admitted observation/history
channels, not only the totals at one instant.

| Information role | Meaning in the amended review |
|---|---|
| **Level 1: whole-link aggregation** | Full admitted link-level longitudinal observations/history. Retained as context; it is not the central new contrast. |
| **Level 2: E and combined continuing traffic** | The admitted aggregate inputs plus initial E and continuing totals, with all inference lawful from its permitted history. The true A/B split is not separately supplied. |
| **Level 3: per-lane initial traffic** | The same common inputs with E, A and B specified separately; future queues and recovery are predicted. |
| **Compact equal-information rival** | Level 2 plus one initial adjacent-lane statistic. Under the retained homogeneous initial family, check that this reconstructs all three initial lane masses and matches Level 3. |

Shared geometry/physical coefficients are not the same as hidden initial
state. In particular, an A-dependent combined E/A load cannot be handed to
Level 2 as a known parameter if it reveals the concealed split. Neither a
future E trajectory nor future receiving is an initial predictor input.
The action remains the existing one-shot advice; no feedback-observation
controller is silently added.

Level 2 may estimate A from its history, retain uncertainty or use a disclosed
prior. It is not forced to believe an equal split. If permitted observations
identify A, report that sufficient inference. Do not hide a revealing channel
or narrow the observation window to rescue an impossibility claim.

**Output and gate:** the true initialization versus predictor-input contract,
a qualified indistinguishable-pair construction or an honest inference result,
and the compact rival's sufficiency statement. A new observation restriction
or changed preparation/history family is a scientific choice and triggers
the smallest affected gate.

## Third: recheck decisions and gradients, then return to Step 3 closure

**AS3: action relevance, complete gradients and final consistency —
review completed; original Step 3 user closure pending.** Trace
the hidden initial split through actual preparation and forced service to
complete TST. Distinguish different local receiving, different predictions,
different finite action utility, and different defined gradient directions.
The first does not establish the later claims.

Reuse the full-objective sensitivity chain where valid: independent initial
loads are fixed within each advice comparison, while preparation, moving
arrivals, service, queue events and recovery aging/retirement respond to the
common advice. Recheck the references for the asymmetric family; do not
rename the recent symmetric action result as the new certificate. Preserve
regular-event gradient conditions, nonregular forward cases, scalar search,
strong aggregate/history/model-form rivals and common-action/negative outcomes.

**Output and final gate:** an amended plain findings report, supporting
mathematics/PDF as needed, the standalone comparison/evidence protocol and
final global consistency audit. Return to VSL-S3-G1 for user closure of the
original Step 3. Stop earlier if restoring the contrast requires a new law,
state, objective, observation/action or derivative meaning. Numerical tests,
implementation and Step 4 remain separately gated.
