## The traffic meaning of the completed component

The meter now represents a stream whose restriction is refreshed by recent
E passages. Each admitted E portion adds its speed-conditioned recovery
profile. While the account contains less than the reference mass, those
contributions accumulate. Once it is full, new admitted mass retires the
same amount of oldest profile weight. All retained profiles continue to
recover with physical time, including periods with no new E discharge.

This gives three changes to the reading: **new entry, recovery of what
remains, and retirement of what has been replaced**. They update one
aggregate budget. No separate service ceiling follows each departed portion.
The physical reference mass is unchanged by numerical step size, and
retiring a profile never removes traffic from the road's mass balance.

A's own departures do not add another recovery loss. A still affects E's
admission and receives its share of the current budget: free A has the
selected priority, while queued A cooperates. Those allocation decisions
use current traffic; the recovery component retains the recent information
needed to predict how its reading changes.

## How A can queue even though ordinary arrivals fit

The key is the timing inside H1. As preparation proceeds, more traffic
has already merged into A, so A's eligible arrivals rise while E's fall.
Their combined ordinary arrival rate can remain below the undisturbed
ceiling throughout.

At the front, the remaining E maneuver work makes E wait while A passes.
Slow admitted E then lowers shared service. If A's later offered flow rises
above that reduced budget, A must wait as well. Both queues then use the
cooperative branch. No externally supplied bottleneck, ordinary overload
or new spatial state is required by this mathematical construction.

The proof first isolates this build-up before the recent-mass window fills.
It then ends the finite arrival pulse shortly after A has begun waiting.
The E backlog is large enough that completing the same cohort necessarily
fills the renewal window later. With no new arrivals, both queues drain;
the account may continue recovering after every traveler has completed.
The first retirement removes still-active old profile weight, so renewal
changes the budget update rather than merely changing inactive bookkeeping.
Its contribution to that update is genuinely nonzero.

This resolves the previous **possibility** gap: A delay is not excluded by
the selected renewal closure. It does not show renewal is necessary for
A delay, that renewal beats addition, or that a slower advice is optimal.
The earlier constant-A protection result remains valid in its limiting
case; increasing H1 A arrivals supply the difference here.

The construction may require long geometry, very slow positive recovery,
and a small positive mismatch coefficient. Zero recovery and zero mismatch
are used only as auxiliary proof limits, then removed. They are not adopted
as the primary model. No numerical traffic trajectory or preferred task
parameter set was produced.
This construction is not a proposed evaluation scenario for the paper.

## What still needs scientific qualification

The equations now define a concrete conditional recovery component. Their
traffic interpretation still rests on the following explicit assumptions.

- **Representative departure speed:** the queued boundary trace is used as
  an insertion/recovery mark. A flow-derived trace is not independently
  established as the actual crossing speed.
- **Recent-mass renewal:** retiring old weight approximates an affected
  stream being refreshed. A faster later passage can retire a stronger
  older profile before acceleration alone would remove it.
- **Physical scales:** the renewal mass, recovery acceleration, maneuver
  work and preparation opportunity scale require a jointly credible range.
  The existence proof does not supply one.
- **Separate burdens:** immediate maneuver work and later recovery must
  represent different effects, so the same obstruction is not charged twice.

These are limits of the same compact point component. No need for CTM,
approach-speed ODEs, another zone or extra initial observations has been
established by this pass.

The source point reference motivates isolated recovery and the restricted
stationary comparison. It does not provide this complete transient renewal
law or the proofs here. The admission template and speed marks likewise
remain declared adaptations.
