**Variable speed limit control for severe non-recurrent freeway bottlenecks.**
Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies, 51, 210–230.
[DOI: 10.1016/j.trc.2014.10.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2014.10.015).

Requested path: `archive/user_supplied/FB0021.pdf`.

Why critical: G1 sensitivity overlap and G2/G3 strong-baseline adequacy for
incident/work-zone restriction, rather than only a permanent lane drop. The

retrieval failed; ResearchGate reports no full text. Author Academia listing
was discovered but retrieval failed; targeted open-PDF searches did not yield
