## Accepted S12 result

S12 closes with **AMR-A — admit one model family for a separately gated full
analytical contract/certificate review**.

The admitted M2 family consists of continuous per-lane first-order traffic mass,
endogenous mandatory transfer from the ending lane, finite target-lane receiving
shared by longitudinal and lateral entrants, and a source-bounded extra
gap/spacing cost for lane-changing traffic. It retains one common lane-uniform
VSL action.

S12 establishes that this family:

- has direct primary-source modeling support;
- can be conservative and physically receiving-bounded;
- can make realized service depend on lane state and action-conditioned
  evolution rather than an attached fixed capacity;
- removes the advice-independent-service premise behind the S05 fastest-arrival
  obstruction over a nonempty ordinary regime; and
- has an aggregate limit and explicit falsification conditions.

## Claims not established

S12 does not establish:

- a reachable consequential pair with exactly the same declared strong
  aggregate history;
- a different useful common-VSL action under complete all-cohort accounting;
- necessity of lane contrast relative to a strong aggregate capacity-drop
  model;
- a frozen VSL-response, mandatory-transfer or receiving-priority equation;
- parameter calibration, field validity or minimum sufficient state; or
- a correct or useful combined gradient.

AMR-A means “worthy of exact analytical testing,” not “core research question
answered” and not implementation authorization.

## Approved successor boundary

Insert **S13: targeted M2 analytical contract and certificate review** before
the planned ramp-metering task-admission review. Its
[Phase-A proposal](../../s13_00_m2_analytical_contract_certificate_review.md)
is the current continuation.

S13 should attempt to freeze the smallest source-to-equation contract, construct
or rule out the exact strong-history witness, test complete-accounting action
distinction including the fastest feasible action, retain an aggregate
state-dependent-capacity countermodel, and state the general gradient path only
if decision relevance is established.

Opening S13 authorizes planning only. It does not authorize equation adoption,
derivation, implementation, simulation, SUMO, calibration, optimization or
gradient calculation. Stop at S13-PG1 for user discussion and approval.
