## Accepted S13 result

S13 closes with **M2C-A — conditional decision-relevant certificate**.

Within the approved conservative continuous-mass M2 member, two preparations
with the same declared strong aggregate predecision history and different hidden
ending-lane fractions can require different unique common lane-uniform VSL
actions. Common VSL changes preparation-zone residence time; residence time
changes remaining mandatory transfer; remaining merge mass changes finite-
receiving work at the choke. In the certified open regime, one state selects the
fastest endpoint and the other a slower interior action.

The same hidden state changes the locally useful analytical gradient direction
inside the fixed smooth active regime. An observer restricted to the identical
aggregate history must make the same initial choice in both states and therefore
has strictly positive worst-state regret.

## Accepted claim boundary

M2C-A establishes a source-bounded methodological possibility and complete
conditional information-to-decision chain. It does not establish:

- a calibrated or field-valid exponential transfer law;
- prevalence of the certified regime in real freeway traffic;
- necessity of full per-lane state, order, gaps or vehicle identity;
- robustness to every conservative transfer or receiving-priority law;
- implementation-level derivative correctness;
- independent downstream gradient usefulness; or
- superiority over every augmented aggregate model.

The elementary contract compresses consequential postdecision lane information
to remaining mandatory-merge mass. The accepted result therefore proves that
the declared aggregate observer can omit consequential information; it does not
prove that a complete lane-resolved representation is the minimum remedy.

## Prospective review ladder for a research-ready task

The following sequence is recommended if the user continues the present VSL
lane-drop line. It is a roadmap only; none of these stages is open.
The accepted standalone roadmap is retained in the
[deferred research-readiness plan](../../synthesis/s13/s13_deferred_research_readiness_review_plan.md).

### Stage 1 — model-family robustness and minimum-information review

**Aim:** Turn the S13 existence member into a bounded theoretical study design.

Review a small preregistered family of transfer and service closures, perform the
causal deletion ablations, classify fastest/interior/slowest/indistinguishable
regions, and compare nested representations:

1. strong aggregate history;
2. aggregate history plus merge pressure or remaining merge mass;
3. ending-lane versus continuing-lane grouped state; and
4. fuller per-lane state only where the compressed state fails.

**Required output:** a primary theoretical claim, justified model family,
minimum candidate state, ablation matrix and stop conditions. A finding that one
continuous scalar is sufficient is an admissible positive result.

**Gate:** proceed toward an applied task only if at least one source-compatible
nontrivial regime survives without post-result tuning and the model ladder can
make a fair information comparison. Otherwise close as a conditional theory
result.

### Stage 2 — traffic-domain, parameter and reference-evaluator admission review

**Aim:** Decide whether the theoretical family can support a traffic-valid
experiment and identify the domain in which that claim would apply.

Review evidence for transfer opportunity, gap/receiving cost, speed response,
geometry and demand bounds; define calibration versus held-out information; and
select an independent richer evaluator capable of representing the mechanism.
SUMO may be a candidate evaluator, but installation or execution is not needed
to decide the methodological contract.

**Required output:** admitted traffic domain, source-supported parameter
strategy, reference-model responsibilities, observable data requirements and
explicit failure/deferral conditions.

**Gate:** either admit one credible evaluator/domain pair, retain the result as
theory-only, or abandon the applied claim. Do not substitute an available tool
for a missing validity argument.

### Stage 3 — matched comparison and gradient-usefulness protocol review

**Aim:** Freeze the experiment that could answer the external research question.

Specify reachable preparations or an approved matching method; common action
family; complete all-traffic accounting; strong aggregate, scalar-augmented and
lane-resolved candidates; internal analytical/AD/finite-difference checks;
active-set handling; independent perturbation evaluation; held-out regimes;
seeds, tolerances, metrics and failure interpretation.

**Required output:** an outcome-blind, reproducible protocol separating forward
fidelity, derivative correctness and downstream gradient usefulness.

**Gate:** approve the complete comparison and acceptance criteria before any
model implementation or traffic run.

### Stage 4 — integrated research-ready task and execution-plan freeze

**Aim:** Convert the accepted theory, domain and protocol into one executable
research plan.

Consolidate hypotheses, claims and non-claims; component responsibilities and
interfaces; model equations and state; data/configuration requirements;
implementation and validation order; computational budget; reproducibility
artifacts; user checkpoints; stop rules; and publication/report products.

**Required output:** an approved task definition and implementation/experiment
plan. Under repository governance, this stage should map the work into the
appropriate Phase A–C plan before any Phase D/E execution.

**Gate:** explicit user approval of the frozen research plan. Only then may
implementation or experiment execution begin.
