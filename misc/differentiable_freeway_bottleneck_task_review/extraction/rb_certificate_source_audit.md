## Construction discipline

The conditional free-flow class is not the calibrated whole FB0030 scheme or
native SUMO. Homogeneous desired response does not imply lane-independent actual
speed in interacting traffic. Its early reachable contrast is not the prescribed
last400m contrast. Unknown past lane allocation is disclosed, while total demand,
known-empty initialization and full aggregate history remain fixed. These limits
are part of the findings, not waived acceptance tests.
