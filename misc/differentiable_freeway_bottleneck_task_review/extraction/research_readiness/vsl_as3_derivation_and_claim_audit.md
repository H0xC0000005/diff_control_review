fast recovery and keeps A free. The earlier A-delay/active-retirement witness
is separate; no joint strong-delay/action/physical range is proved. AS2's
illustrative work coefficient is not newly certified to yield different
useful advice merely because the same initial split is reused.

Regular-chart sensitivity is the mathematical reference for a later numerical
verification. It is not a run of AD, finite differences or policy training.
Nonregular events stay in the task with truthful continuity/side/finite-change
reporting. Both complete-TST forms and every clearance tail remain required.

The new standalone protocol retains strong history/model-aware inference,
the exactly sufficient compact peer, faithful model-form challengers and
scalar search. Information value, learning difficulty, representation size,
gradient correctness and optimizer advantage are separate claims. A common
best action or a simpler rival matching performance is an accepted outcome.

Applied MP6 remains unexecuted and SUMO optional. No physical novelty,
calibrated freeway benefit, discrete-order necessity or empirical training
result is established by this review. These limits do not block the user's
chosen standalone methodology gate, but they limit its paper claims.
