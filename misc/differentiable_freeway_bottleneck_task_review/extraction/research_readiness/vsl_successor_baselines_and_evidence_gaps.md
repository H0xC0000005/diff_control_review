## What a serious simpler rival already does

An aggregate model can represent more than current density and a fixed exit
capacity. It can retain speed and cumulative-count history, change its service
with traffic state, estimate latent quantities from observations, and support
effective VSL control. A lane-aware model can remain entirely continuous.
Neither a VSL label nor continuous lane transfer is a novelty claim.

The proposed unresolved comparison is narrower: under declared initial
observations and a common action, does extra merge preparation improve the
prediction of independently useful speed changes, and what is the smallest
adequate augmentation? The source screen below does not establish that no
published work answers that comparison.

Feedback 2 fixes the review preference: useful, information-sensitive gradients
first; novelty of the particular verification second. An aggregate peer's cost
gap, optimization difficulties, need for an augmentation or absence from a
search cannot substitute for independently checked usefulness. The A/A+E/G
information contrasts, faithful source-model comparisons and same-model
optimizer comparisons retain separate attributions. A lawful scalar repair
and successful direct search remain strong rivals, not exclusions.

## Two comparisons that must remain distinct

**Controlled information comparison.** Under one declared law, A has the full
aggregate observer; A+E adds initial ending-lane mass; G carries the equivalent
two-group state. A's estimator, learned prior or aggregate capacity/history
state may be as strong as the allowed data permit. Identical exact histories
must yield the same initial prediction under a common deterministic inference
rule. A+E/G equality is required in the S13 member.

**Source-model comparison.** A faithfully qualified published aggregate model
may predict the same useful changes for different reasons, or may need only a
compact augmented state. Evaluate it as a genuine challenger. Differing speed
dynamics, capacity laws, observations or action maps prevent attribution to
information alone. A published lane-aware model may likewise defeat the proposed
need for more state. Record actual source methods rather than generic names.

The source screen supports these rivals' inclusion, not their equation-level
fitness for the successor. Faithful adoption, conditional adaptation and
original deduction will remain separate columns in the later model contract.

## Small optimizer references

The inherited member has a closed analytical optimum. Use it as a correctness
reference if that member is later implemented. For a one-dimensional action,
bounded direct search over the same interval is an inexpensive, serious rival;
report its resolution and objective calls before making efficiency claims.
Same-model optimizer comparisons share initialization, physical bounds,
objective, scenario and budget. A feedback controller observes new traffic over
time and requires a separate observation/action contract; it cannot be compared
as if it were only another optimizer for the one-shot action.

Finite-difference gradient estimates belong among gradient-based comparisons;
derivative-free direct search remains a distinct rival. Useful perturbation
guidance does not require either method to lose. Noise, instability or local-
optimum failures are untested optional claims, not a presumed consequence of
aggregate information loss. Any later efficiency claim must expose objective
calls and differentiation/computation costs rather than compare iteration
counts alone.

## Evidence gaps and stopping consequence

| Gap | Needed evidence in a later authorized review | Consequence if unavailable |
|---|---|---|
| Advice versus realized speed | State the ideal response regime and qualify any independent evaluator's intervention mapping | Keep the result conditional; do not add acceleration or compliance kinetics implicitly |
| Constant exponential transfer | Source-compatible intended regime and one small conservative neighboring-law audit | No empirical transfer-law or family robustness claim |
| Proportional batch service | Population/arrival assumptions, finite-work interpretation and a small priority audit under the same complete-time objective | No priority-independent mean-delay claim; no objective substitution |
| Strong observer collision | Reachability and equality of all declared history channels in the admitted model/domain | Conditional S05/S13 certificate only; approximate or snapshot matching needs an explicit amendment |
| Scalar availability and sufficiency | Define initial observation versus inference; test autonomous scalar prediction against grouped state | No deployable sensing claim; no full-lane necessity from withholding a sufficient statistic |
| Traffic-domain parameter validity | Supported/calibrated bounds with development and held-out separation | Open analytical parameter regimes do not establish traffic prevalence |
| Derivative correctness | Analytical/AD/double-precision differences under a declared event signature | Analytical gradient only, not implementation validation |
| External usefulness | Independently admitted evaluator, common physical perturbation and complete accounting | No traffic-control transfer claim; shared-equation checks remain internal |
| Precise novelty | Closest actual comparison and decision-critical successors | Verification role can remain; publication novelty remains unqualified |

Field evidence is deliberately mixed: S06/FB0039 reports little change in lane
fractions at equal occupancy in the inspected dataset, and common lower limits
can increase lane changing. These cautions preclude assuming that slower advice
always improves preparation or that a same-flow comparison isolates hidden
information. The eventual negative/control cases must survive into reporting.

No new empirical result or absence claim is made by this opening. A compact
model or strong aggregate challenger winning is a permitted scientific outcome.
