## Reachability and what the matched record proves

The loading is the same H1 donor-branch transport construction already
disclosed in Section 2 of the 14 October information/sensitivity note. Before
loading the road is empty. The common lower past speed keeps both initial
splits donor-limited; lane inputs preserve a common uniform body while the
front advances to the required initial footprint. The front does not reach
the terminal before the decision, preserving AS1's neutral terminal/account.

The general sufficient loading inequalities ensure nonnegative A inputs,
free densities and donor feasibility. The AS1 split family satisfies them
for the same nonempty small-footprint-to-preparation-scale range already
used by the earlier recipe. An exact rational-exponential choice verifies
strict margins without evolving traffic numerically. This selects a valid
existence subfamily; it does not exclude other AS1 histories or weaken their
admission bounds.

The matched record proves that no deterministic Level 2 state estimate can
identify both true splits from the same input. It does not prove every
history conceals A or that every controller needs different advice. No
regret or opposite-gradient result is deduced from an information collision.
The possibility of a disclosed prior, uncertainty-aware prediction or lawful
inference remains part of the strong rival.

## Inference that must be allowed

During a strictly receiving-limited part of preparation, exact E density
decline at known speed and with known coefficients determines A through
the retained transfer law. Its inferred conserved E+A load then determines
the initial A/B split. This is a sufficient noiseless identification case,
not a robust detector-estimation guarantee. The body must be present; a
changing total E mass requires dividing out the known footprint before
taking the density derivative.

During strictly donor-limited preparation, E decline is independent of
unused A receiving. It constrains A but generally leaves an interval of
possible loads. The constructed pair lies inside that interval. At a branch
equality, the donor derivative alone does not identify which unique receiving
state was present. An actual informative channel overrides the ambiguity
claim for that particular record; it is not removed to keep the example.

## Compact equal-information peer

Level 2 plus initial A mass reconstructs E, A and B exactly by the common
footprint and total mass. AS1's unique forward law then gives identical
predicted histories, service, queues, complete TST and any defined objective
derivative to the explicit per-lane initial predictor at every advice.
This is initial-information sufficiency, not a one-state reduction of the
predicted terminal/recovery dynamics. It is not evidence that AD or a larger
state representation is uniquely necessary or superior.
