## Direct source reading versus this review's deductions

**Carey: direct reading.** Section 4 offers eager, location-fraction and planned-
location demand. The last explicitly attaches desired completion to distance.
Section 5 charges additional receiving space to lateral entrants and offers
several priority rules. R2 allocates the effective receiving demand proportionally.
The inspected model has no VSL controller.

**Reduction deduction.** Constant receiving shared by two simultaneous,
fully exposed class queues under those work weights yields S13's proportional
co-clearance if no other constraint binds. This is an algebraic projection with
stated assumptions, not a native spatial result. The source does not make early
lane changes interaction-free.

**FB0030: direct reading.** Equation 13 defines sending from free-flow speed,
density and capacity. Equation 14 makes desired lateral flow a fraction of that
sending. Equations 6–7 and 10 define incentives, including distance to the lane
end; equations 15–17 introduce receiving restrictions. The latter retain the
previously recorded branch/denominator cautions. No source repair was attempted.

**Reduction deduction.** A free, prescribed-motion completion rate proportional
to speed accumulates the same opportunity over a fixed distance. That is the
explicit conditional diagnostic in the PDF; it is not a claim that the whole
native two-step model is invariant to VSL. Native density, receiving and
cooperation can break that scaling and require their actual state.

**Wei: direct reading.** The model's entry-speed cohorts and cumulative count
state make aggregate history a serious explanation for VSL response. Its
different motion/capacity laws are not inserted into the S13 matched ladder.

**Information deduction.** The accepted scalar reproduces S13 exactly; an
aggregate history collision cannot be promoted to a native reachable witness
in either inspected traffic model. No universal macro insufficiency follows.

## Access and scientific limits

Carey and Wei were inspected through primary web text. Two requested Carey PDF
screenshots failed with a web cache miss. The R2 factor and textual allocation
were available in the extracted primary text and existing S12/S13 audit.
The projection is therefore labeled a conditional interpretation, not
certification of every Algorithm A2 update or a rendered-equation inspection.
FB0030 PDF pages 6–10 were read locally with already installed `pypdf`; no
dependency was installed and no full-text copy was newly archived.

The missing evidence is substantive, not merely access: the inspected sources
do not identify one admitted speed/preparation/receiving contract with the
intended fixed-time response. The bounded audit does not establish that no
such contract or evidence exists elsewhere. Further search/derivation that
selects a different traffic interpretation is held at the gate.
