## Boundary between evidence and the revised plan

No source inspected here supplies the complete proposed M2 member. The
recommendation to qualify compulsory interaction, ordinary queueing and
recovery together is a methodological synthesis. Their coexistence does not
justify adding loss terms from separate models or charging a queue twice.

Likewise, macro representability is a promising direction, not proof that
the current retained state is sufficient. The next pass must specify and
qualify that closure. No empirical parameter, microscopic gap trace or
future SUMO output was used to manufacture a slowing episode or control
advantage. No new traffic experiment was performed.
