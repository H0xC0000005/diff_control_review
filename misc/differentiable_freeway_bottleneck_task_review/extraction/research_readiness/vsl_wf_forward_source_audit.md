## New deductions under the selected assumptions

The uniform H1 pulse, existing causal guards, R-E and O-R imply the following
review deductions: an E queue can only begin at the front and cannot re-form
after clearance; repeated A events have a positive separation; finite-regime
continuation therefore extends to the entire original constant-advice range
in the retained primary domain. Conservation/storage, uniform finite
clearance, compact initial-information reconstruction, the no-extra-work
negative control and the local activation-flux gap are also deductions.

These are mathematical arguments about our declared model, not published
findings or numerical traffic evidence. Symbolic checks verify supporting
identities and static inequalities, not the quantified event proof or its
physical validity. The theorem does not cover arbitrary demand histories,
initial queues, ordinary overload or a general invariant LTM node.

## Still not literature-established

Fixed interaction strength and actual-speed dependence have a stated physical
interpretation but remain a constitutive adaptation. Queue-flow boundary
speed as insertion speed, the renewal mass and treatment of old profile
retirement, and the immediate-work/recovery partition still require joint
physical qualification. Source ingredients do not by themselves establish
their applied coexistence or a VSL policy benefit.
