## Deductions made in this pass

The two FD speed branches, undisturbed finite-cohort trajectory under the
retained low-load domain, and nonempty compatible ordinary-overload domain
are analytical review deductions. The zero-effect result is conditional on
the stated speed-recovery-only completion and unique causal evolution; it
is not attributed to a source as a theorem about all M2 laws.

**Inference corrected after feedback 6:** a source's congested regime
does not prove ordinary overload above undisturbed capacity is the required
cause, or justify an exclusive F/D choice. Compulsory interaction can reduce
effective service and produce a queue even under ordinary underload. The
old exclusive gate and D-first recommendation are withdrawn; the conditional
algebra is unchanged. Qualify entry, actual discharge speed and recovery
together. This does not approve an exact law or primary-domain amendment.
No empirical parameter, SUMO output or future speed is used to supply it.
