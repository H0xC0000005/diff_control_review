## What is a deduction and what remains a physical hypothesis

- **Selected direction:** free A first, followed by queued cooperation,
  preserving S-E-P's proposed behavior when E clears before A.
- **Exact clearance deduction:** a literal priority toggle based on two
  strictly positive queues has no nonnegative absolutely continuous
  continuation in the stated local example. It is not a new primary scenario.
- **Derived profile family:** the earlier isolated recovery calculation now
  accepts a variable predicted speed mark. Finite support, integrated work
  and Lipschitz bounds follow from those equations.
- **Two explicit source hypotheses:** R-E deposits profiles from E admissions;
  R-AE additionally deposits profiles from slow A admissions. Both sum them
  into one account. Neither is selected; linear overlap and representative
  speed attribution remain proposed reductions.
- **Own conditional proofs:** finite integrated service loss and clearance
  for any admissible causal solution of either law; local fixed-mode causal
  construction; an A-only-tail renewal identity; a constant-A protected limit.
- **Not established:** whole hybrid existence/uniqueness, physical overlap,
  primary H1 A-delay/MP6, empirical calibration, SUMO correspondence or useful
  gradients. Neither the source authors' proofs nor M1's proof transfers.

Ordinary slow A traffic could renew a disturbance physically; the sources
read here do not determine that every such passage is an additional isolated
restriction. R-E's omission and R-AE's renewal are therefore explicit,
decision-facing modeling assumptions. No external slowing seed or change
to the ordinary-underload domain follows from this review.
