## Original chain

Section 2.1, pages 210–211, Figure 1/equations 1–2: homogeneous kinematic-wave
traffic, triangular fundamental diagrams, constant upstream demand, distinct
stable bottleneck capacity and congested discharge. A non-recurrent event
reduces capacity, then its removal restores ordinary capacity; demand is below
that restored capacity. State is aggregate density/flow with wave geometry;
speed, flow, time and distance define fronts and queue areas.

Section 2.2, pages 212–214: progressively lower upstream speed steps clear the
initial queue; an acceleration zone then supports higher discharge. Activation
and deactivation locations/times follow leading/last-vehicle trajectories and
wave intersections. This is not a fixed-sign ordinary advisory-response model.

Section 2.3, pages 214–216, equations 7–8/Figure 4, and Appendix A, pages 224–229:
total delay includes the recovery tail until complete queue clearance. Analytical
parameter sensitivities show a tradeoff between clearing the initial queue
quickly and sustaining higher discharge. Larger speed-step intervals do not
uniformly help under severe bottlenecks. Sections 3–4 study under/overestimated
capacity and adaptive correction; overestimation can reactivate the queue.

## Adoption and evidence boundary

Adoptable reference responsibility: complete population/recovery accounting,
capacity-estimation counterevidence, and idealized queue-clearance checks.
Not a new reopening novelty gap. A future adaptation must keep closure and
reopening exogenous, use the same fixed-zone advice set, and represent realized
driver response; it cannot silently adopt moving event-based actuation.
Conservation follows KW/wave accounting, not a tested candidate tensor update.
The sensitivities are analytical within this model, not checked microscopic
derivatives or information-resolution ranking comparisons. Moving wave/front
control requires event-sensitivity scrutiny if differentiated literally; this
is not stochastic categorical sampling. Page 223 leaves driver reaction,
compliance, non-equilibrium effects, enforcement and field testing unresolved.
