# FB0030 — endogenous mandatory-merge model screen

Nagalur Subraveti/Knoop/van Arem,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/21680566.2019.1700846), Transportmetrica B 7(1),
1758–1779. [Archived final published PDF](../../archive/full_text/FB0030.pdf),
24 pages including repository covers. Repository/copyright year 2019;
article header says 2020. Preserve this discrepancy. Methods, validation and
discussion inspected at printed pages 1760–1774; E3 selected locations.

Equations 1–4: per-lane CTM densities (veh/km), longitudinal/lateral flows,
paired transfer conservation, CFL condition. Equations 6–11: deterministic
density-based lateral-demand fractions with downstream density lookahead,
keep-right, route-to-lane-end and cooperation incentives. Equations 12–22:
demand/supply, lateral receiving limitation, then modified longitudinal flows.
Calling fractions 'probabilities' does not make this a sampled discrete process;
the results discussion explicitly notes absence of stochasticity.

Important cautions: empty-density denominators need an exact limit convention;
fractions/bounds and shared donor/receiver budgets need an independent audit.
Printed equation 17 has a special branch at theta=1 distinct from its lower-
branch limit. Free-flow/congested cooperation switches and equations 18–19
capacity-drop mode changes also need continuity checks. Do not silently repair,
smooth, or certify the complete published scheme as gradient-admissible.

Field A13/A12 calibration/validation, including lane drop, primarily supports
lane-density prediction versus regression. Congested duration differs by about
an hour; advice-response/anticipation and memory effects are identified as
missing. Proposed reuse: mandatory-route incentive and constrained continuous
transfer structure only, with common advice response added and exact conservation
audited. No source speed-control ranking or useful-gradient validation.
