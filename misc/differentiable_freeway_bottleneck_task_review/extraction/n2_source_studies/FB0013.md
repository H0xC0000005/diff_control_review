## Original model and comparison

Pages 3–5, Figure 1, equations 1–5: homogeneous METANET segment density
(vehicle-equivalents/km/lane), mean speed (km/h), total flow (vehicle-equivalents/h),
continuous conservation and speed relaxation/convection/anticipation. The lane
drop contributes a speed penalty; desired speed is bounded by the posted limit.
Geometry: 10 km upstream, five lanes dropping to two, five speed-control sites;
passenger cars, VISSIM 5.40/Wiedemann99. Parameters calibrated to VISSIM; a
different-demand forward validation is reported on page 9.

Pages 6–9: Case 1 has separate closing/open lane-group speed actions (40
variables); Case 2 link-wide speeds (20); Case 3 additionally prohibits rightward
lane changes. Discrete speed choices, PSO search. METANET produces an initial
link-wide schedule, then VISSIM reoptimizes. Equation 10 minimizes a critical-
density discrepancy in METANET; VISSIM minimizes stops with travel-time and
throughput constraints. Thus objectives and search fidelity also change.

Page 12, Table 2: no-control stops 9.43; METANET schedule 8.46; VISSIM-reoptimized
lane-group Case 1 4.81; link-wide Case 2 5.00; Case 3 4.59. The 48.99% reduction
for Case 1 is against no control. Its stops advantage over Case 2 is 3.8%.
Case 1/2 travel times are 611.09/617.55 seconds versus baseline 570.92.
These are reported optimization outcomes, not a state-only causal comparison.

## Transfer boundary and evidence

Useful responsibilities: aggregate speed dynamics, calibration workflow,
strong link-wide microscopic-search comparator. Not a lane-group METANET
resolution ladder. Proposed adaptation would hold link-level advice, objective,
population and search budget fixed; this adaptation is not implemented/approved.
Conservation equations are inspectable, but storage/positivity and speed bounds
would need independent checks before adoption. No inspected derivative check,
matched information-only action ranking, or independent useful-gradient result.
Permanent lane drop; no complete reopening/queue-clearance contract. Page 13
data/model inputs available by request, not an accessible reproduction bundle.
