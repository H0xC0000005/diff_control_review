## FB0038 — Subraveti/Knoop/van Arem2020

DOI10.1177/0361198120948055. Archived13-page final copy with repository CC BY
cover. Printed367–377: endogenous incentive-based per-lane CTM baseline versus
optimized lateral fractions using MATLAB SQP; natural lane changing is disabled
in controlled cases. Equation12 counts origin waiting. Printed376–377:
reported discharge gain above4.5%, with spatial spreading and balancing among
continuing lanes; tested2–1 adaptation has no TTT improvement. These support
mechanism and conditional counterevidence, not shared-speed gradient necessity.
Equation11 inherits a receiving-branch hazard; no whole-model AD certificate.
Different lateral actuator and lane-specific FD parameters remain confounds.

## FB0039 — Soriguera/Martínez/Sala/Menéndez2017

DOI10.1016/j.trc.2017.01.024. Archived18-page final open paper.
Sections4.1/5.1–5.3/7, printed264–273: common fixed limits affect lane-flow
distribution; lower limits at moderate demand can increase lane-changing;
high stable flows remain possible. Fixed three-day limits and punctual radar
enforcement at one site do not validate dynamic voluntary advice.
Section4.1 notes the measurement section is not itself an active bottleneck.
No matched hidden-state pair, internal derivative or independent control-gradient
evidence. This is mechanism/counterevidence, not proof of causal gradient value.
