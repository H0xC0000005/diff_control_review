## Retained information: narrower than the initial abstract label

Pages4–7/section4.1.1/equations1–21: four vehicle types determine expected
headway/fundamental-diagram parameters through a Markov spatial-composition
calculation, penetration/truck rates, aggregation factor and maximum platoon size.
The section4.1.2 rollout/equations22–29 carries cell/lane counts, density, speed
and intercell transfers; it does not supply four separate transported class-mass
balance equations or an evolving intra-lane vehicle-order ledger.

Consequently the earlier discovery label “four-class CTM” must be qualified:
four-type-aware expected flow law plus lane-resolved traffic state, not evidence
for persistent four-class states. Section5.1 fixes aggregation factor0.
An analytically evaluated probability in a constitutive law is not a simulated
control-dependent categorical mass shift. No such sampling operation is stated
for these CTM transfers. This distinction does not certify the entire scheme
as continuous or differentiable.

## Equation-level reuse limits — no repairs approved

Exact locations: pages6–8/section4.1/equations15–31; pages10–11/equations36–37.

- Equations22/28 provide a paired transfer/accounting structure and receiver
  scaling. But equation25 prints sending from lane j on its right-hand side
  while its donor indices identify lane k. Literal transcription does not
  establish the corresponding donor budget. No index correction inferred.
- Equation26 uses a positive speed-difference fraction and a residual stay
  fraction. Admissible parameter bounds, time-coefficient units and the
  simultaneous outgoing budget must be checked, not supplied by new clipping.
- Equation27 divides receiving by total desired sending. Empty-demand limits
  are unspecified in the displayed expression; a valid conservative limit
  would need a later explicit contract.
- Equation19 prints a negative backward wave velocity; equation24 uses that
  symbol in receiving, whereas the usual receiving coefficient uses a positive
  magnitude. The sign/magnitude convention needs reconciliation before reuse.
  This is a documentary transcription ambiguity, not a reproduced negative-flow
  failure of the authors' implementation.
- Equations20–21 and accompanying page7 prose prescribe abrupt capacity
  attenuation above critical density. Continuous count state does not eliminate
  that mode-switch hazard. Ordinary min/max flux kinks are a different issue.
- Cell length, count-per-step capacities, density length units and controlled
  speed propagation need a declared consistent transcription/update contract.
  Equations23–24 alone do not supply an audited speed-dependent travel-time path.
- The modulo restriction in equation36 is a discrete action lattice; NSGA-II
  does not validate ordinary AD. Equation37 does not specify in its displayed
  correction how all constraints are reapplied. No repair or constraint weakening
  is adopted.

These limitations prevent wholesale reuse as an AD-ready candidate. They do
not refute the physical lane-information hypothesis or authorize a new smoother.
