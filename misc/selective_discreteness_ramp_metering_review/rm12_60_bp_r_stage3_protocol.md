model-reduction comparison. First establish the physical/control/accounting
bridge; then specify which retained state could preserve the relevant response.
BP-R asks relative representation adequacy, not an equal-full-history ambiguity
theorem. No finer-state necessity or universal best representation is presumed.

conserved traffic mass, finite receiving/storage, shared declared physical
initialization/forcing with explicit projections and memory, and complete
all-stream TST through clearance including entrance waiting. Source event-based
references are candidates for qualification, not approved mass quantization.
No private reference truth, future-state corrections, unfair control interfaces,
unjustified extra lanes/ramps or unsupported recovery term. A qualified compact
sufficient model is a valid result.

package change or production implementation. General gradient/study-readiness

   Transcribe source regimes, meter permission versus release/entry, receiving,
   storage, disturbance/reference state and event semantics. Follow only the
   nearest primary citations needed to resolve an actual transfer gap. Do not
   splice favorable fragments from different models as an established law.
2. **Bind the finite task/control/accounting contract.** Make geometry, state,
   units, conservative balances, feasible rates, public initial/boundary data,
   update order and clearing tail explicit. Distinguish source laws from proposed
   adaptations. Establish a credible rate-to-service-to-exit/TST route, including
   holding costs; inspect inactive/queue-relocation and trivial-policy limits.
   If adoption of a new physical/reference/actuator contract is necessary, prepare
   concrete alternatives and stop at G2 before treating a candidate as approved.
3. **Define strong independent reduction roles.** Within the qualified task,
   specify projection, initial memory, parameter/estimator access, state/update
   interfaces and common controls. Keep credible spatial, congestion-memory,
   moment and lane-aggregate competitors. State which response/comparison claim
   remains unresolved; do not require a positive performance result in advance
   of future experiments. A lane-aggregate model already retains lane contrast.
4. **Audit and synthesize.** Check conservation, receiving/storage, complete
   accounting, causality, information/action fairness, equivalence/inactive
   regimes and whether the study can distinguish meaningful reductions. Identify
   formulation gaps, bounded extensions/ablations and any structural sensitivity
   obstacle. Produce a concrete formulation if justified, compact sufficiency,
   scoped control irrelevance/rejection, evidence-limited closure or an amendment
   gate. Do not automatically enter Stage 4.
