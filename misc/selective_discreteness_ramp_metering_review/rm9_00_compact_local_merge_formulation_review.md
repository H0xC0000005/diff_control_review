## Fixed inputs and open evidence

Preserve single-ramp open-loop action, finite storage/receiving, all-stream time
through clearance, shared forcing and strong road-total density/flow/speed/count
plus ramp history. Candidate additional information is lane decomposition, not
an assumed discrete vehicle identity. A scalar equivalent to a lane contrast is
an equally valid representation. Source-level per-lane states may be inspected
as a reference without adopting all of them for the eventual model.

Precise observer measurement functions, source reduction, shared receiving
allocation and derivative events must be explicit before a claimed certificate.
Do not silently set identical lane speeds, erase independent speed information,
assume instantaneous within-group mixing or give ramp traffic guaranteed entry.
An analytic reduction counterexample is not a realized matched-history pair.

## Sequence and acceptance

1. Recheck critical source equations and actual observation conventions.
2. Audit exact grouping, equivalent-scalar information and stronger aggregate
   reconstruction. State assumptions for every inference.
3. Screen shared donor/receiver and finite-ramp access. A meter permission and
   realized merge flow are distinct stages.
4. If an unambiguous source-backed law survives, propose the smallest feasible
   exact-history/action construction and examine sensitivity. Otherwise stop
   before inventing one and present the smallest methodological alternatives.
5. Produce findings, a mathematical PDF plus TeX, claim/provenance and status
   records. Validate artifacts and links; report full-chain gaps separately.

A pass is successful if it either advances a defensible analytical construction
or identifies the first exact obstruction with consequences and a concrete next
decision. It need not manufacture a positive hidden-lane result. Numerical
simulation, derivative tests and richer-reference evaluation remain outside scope.

## Stop and reopen conditions

Stop for an unapproved mixing closure, lane-parameter symmetry selected solely
to hide information, a changed speed observation, unresolved finite receiving/
priority, source branch repair, a stochastic/event estimator or changed action.
Reopen only the smallest affected G1/G2 contract; keep G0 intact.

The derivative/action certificate is conditional on those contracts closing.
No SUMO installation or experiment follows from this protocol.
