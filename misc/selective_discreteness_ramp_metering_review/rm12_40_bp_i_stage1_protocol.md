## Purpose and approved boundaries

Audit whether one genuine local rate-meter setting supplies an operational
information domain that is not already covered by the earlier conditional
sufficiency findings. Preserve the original information question for this pass.
Knowing only initial aggregates is not equated to complete physical knowledge;
finite point measurements do not by themselves prove history ambiguity.

The audit is methodology/source work. It does not select new dynamics, change
the actuator/objective, solve a complete inverse problem, install/run SUMO,
execute traffic, or verify numerical/useful gradients. Keep common available
information/history, public benchmark inputs, conservation, receiving/storage,
all-stream TST and strong aggregate/memory competitors as inherited contracts.
BP-R was conditional at opening and has now been selected after result discussion;
it is a separate comparison claim, not a change to this completed audit's results.

## Work sequence

1. **Inventory the operational channels.** For each mainline/ramp compartment
   and detector, identify physical location, quantity/support/units, aggregation,
   raw versus reported records, initial calibration/state and actual controller
   use/access. Separate what is documented as available, what the hardware could
   measure, and what the paper merely does not report. Distinguish permission,
   actual release and actual merge entry.
2. **Inventory history and initial knowledge.** Record the information epoch and
   all documented earlier records, operating initialization, queue estimates and
   public model/input knowledge. Do not presume a cold start or finite memory
   without source support. Research videos/reference truth are not automatically
   controller inputs, and neither model gets privileged fine initialization.
   Source uncertainty about future inputs is recorded; it does not silently
   replace the inherited shared/public forcing contract with private randomness.
3. **Check the existing sufficiency premises.** Assess which known-state/history
   and equilibrium-observation results apply, fail to apply, or cannot yet be
   assessed. A theorem whose assumptions are absent is inapplicable, not refuted.
   No exact reconstruction or non-observability claim is inferred merely from
   point-versus-section measurement definitions. Do not adopt a new observer law.
4. **Synthesize the domain contract and stop.** State actual source support,
   analytic deductions, unsupported fields, shared-controller fairness and the
   smallest next evidence question. Report one of the dispositions below and
   review the next stage with the user before continuing.

## Outputs and dispositions

Prepare source locations/access notes under `extraction/rm12/bp_i/`, a domain/
sufficiency-premise assessment under `synthesis/rm12/bp_i/`, and a findings/handoff
under `reporting/rm12/bp_i/`. Archive reasoning/decisions in the RM12 dialogue;
create source/search records only for work actually performed. Do not redistribute
paper binaries or alter historical ledgers just to relabel evidence.

The findings must distinguish:

- **Domain admitted:** source support for a meaningful operational information
  domain leaves a consequential-state question outside the current established
  sufficiency result. This enables a proposed Stage 2 history/action audit; it
  does not prove hiddenness, mesoscopic value or useful gradients.
- **Sufficiency/overlap:** the documented domain is covered by an existing
  sufficient/reconstructible case, or the proposal merely repeats old B's
  premises. State the scope and revisit BP-R/closure in end-stage discussion.
- **Evidence-limited:** initial knowledge, accessible history or the relevant
  source transfer cannot be supported. Report the missing facts; no universal
  negative is inferred. BP-R still requires a defensible physical control task.
- **Methodological conflict:** a genuine setting requires different sensing,
  forcing, actuator, task or claim semantics. Present its smallest amendment
  under G2 and stop before adopting it.

Mixed evidence is allowed: some premises may be supported and others unknown.
Do not force every outcome into a binary success/failure or continue until an
ambiguity example appears. Absent useful rate leverage cannot be repaired by
changing the information/representation claim alone.

## Acceptance and next user gate

The stage is complete when operational channel/init/history assertions are
traceable or explicitly unsupported, sufficiency premises are compared fairly,
source facts are separated from deductions/adaptations, and the disposition
identifies a bounded next question. Document/source integrity is checked; no
numerical traffic/gradient criterion is part of this stage.

Stop at BPI-G2 if adopting a scientific change becomes necessary; otherwise
stop at BPI-G3 with the completed source-domain findings and next-step assessment.
Never automatically open BP-R or the rest of the roadmap. There is no mandatory
pre-opening question about selecting additional lanes, recovery laws, exact
control resolution, gradient estimators or reference software: those are not
needed for the current source audit and remain evidence-dependent later choices.
