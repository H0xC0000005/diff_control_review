## What we are trying to learn

Can a compact model remember enough about the creation and recovery of merging
disturbances to avoid a penalty when the reference has no binding restriction?
A sufficient small summary is a valid outcome. We do not assume that vehicle
identity, a detailed trajectory list, or an arbitrary recovery timer is needed.

## Authorized work

1. Qualify source event accounting from each model's own actual transfers,
   including public initial threshold progress and all descriptor-generating
   transfer streams. Permission, approach admission and merge entry stay distinct.
2. Check simple recovery summaries against the actual constrained-motion rule.
   Separate exact restricted-regime deductions from general approximations.
3. Assess a source-preserving retained-state proposal and its service/interface

   model, complete system-time leverage and gradient usefulness.
