Read-only local seed search in the prior task evidence matrix found no ramp/ALINEA/merge keyword hit. Existing lineage evidence was retained as architectural context; this is not a claim that the full personal archive contains no relevant paper.

Bounded citation follow-up: RM0002 points to Gomes–Horowitz (RM0001), ALINEA (RM0006), and nonmonotonic traffic models; RM0004 points to lane-specific CTM and merge-ratio work. The S5 search checked order/safety and mesoscopic alternatives; RM0007 supplies a useful counterexample to any claim that freeway/TSC inherently require separate backbones. This is not exhaustive citation-network snowballing. Broad RL, multi-ramp coordination, automated lane-free control and VSL optimization results were not expanded because they change the intended task or add no necessary evidence for this gate.

The D1 targeted continuation followed the missing mechanism rather than expanding generic ramp-control coverage. It located a first-order ordered-cohort diverge precedent, a full partial-FIFO node specification, and an elementary microscopic benchmark. That evidence established the internal coherence of narrow B1-P, but feedback 20 raised the stricter question of whether it deserves the role of an orthogonal parallel task.

The feedback-20 pass closes the additional role-comparison gaps:

| Question | New evidence | Finding and remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Mesoscopic ramp-metering precedent | RM0027–RM0029 | Meso value is predominantly DTA/route/departure response, stochastic/event resolution and network scale; exact order is not established as the characteristic ramp mechanism |
| Direct differentiable/gradient ramp control | RM0024–RM0026 | Direct freeway predecessors exist; they operate on aggregate first-/multiclass CTM/LWR states or smoothed CTM, weakening generic differentiability as a selective-meso contribution |
| Freeway-specific AD value | RM0024, RM0025, RM0030 and cross-package Seo record | Strong for many time-dependent controls, MPC and calibration; weak for a single small control vector and not inherently representation-sensitive |
| Orthogonal second-task role | Joint D1 and feedback-20 synthesis | B1-P has application and partial causal-chain distinctness but lacks a distinct representation hypothesis; recommend secondary transfer fixture only |

This expanded coverage is sufficient to decide B1-P's requested role. It is not an exhaustive ramp-metering review and does not prove that no other freeway task can qualify.
