recovery without requiring a finite acceleration lane. A therefore has a
standalone physical question. Its known ramp-queue/entry-speed example does not
establish hiddenness. [Primary merge paper](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.gatech.edu/dist/f/3571/files/2023/03/merges.pdf).

extra recovery state is considered. Its queue-discharge setting still needs
ramp-specific justification. [Selected Chapter 3](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7dx478g4/qt7dx478g4.pdf).

For C, the final ramp/lane-control paper has explicit acceleration-lane geometry,
but only one cell along that lane. Its prioritized ramp entry and joint/control
setting cannot establish distributed finite-opportunity or unchanged strong-
history claims. More spatial detail cannot be presumed hidden when the aggregate
competitor receives matched per-cell observations. [Sections 2.1, 4 and 6](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_3728cefd-b910-49de-a341-d342fbbd5369).

These findings distinguish a supported physical direction from a research-ready
task. Neither supplies a full conservative adapted law, genuine matched-history
action distinction, complete TST benefit or useful gradient chain.
