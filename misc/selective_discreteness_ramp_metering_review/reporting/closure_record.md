## Accepted scientific conclusion

Ramp metering is not adopted as a parallel new task under the current project
formulation. A formulation that remains compatible with the continuous-mass,
first-order architecture offers little distinctive evidence beyond strong
macroscopic ramp-control and gradient methods. A formulation with stronger
freeway-specific mesoscopic value would require substantial additional behavior
and engineering outside the present project. This is a project-role conclusion,
not a general rejection of ramp metering, differentiable freeway control or
mesoscopic simulation.
