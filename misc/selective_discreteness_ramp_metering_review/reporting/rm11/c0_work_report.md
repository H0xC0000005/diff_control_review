## Assumptions and methodological limits

Inherit strong aggregate mass/flow/supported speed, ramp information and full
available history; public common inputs/parameters; single-ramp common feasible
open-loop rate schedules; finite receiving/storage; all-stream TST through
clearance. Two mainline lanes remain a default aim, not a filter hiding genuine
source geometry. No sensor removal, private forcing, extra lanes or invented
traffic pair is used to ensure ambiguity.

Exact physical initialization and exact public deterministic evolution imply
conditional information sufficiency. Initial aggregate statistics are a different
premise. Spatial model resolution cannot add unavailable observations or exact
fine initialization. Lane-macro models already retain lane information. Source
mean/probabilistic laws and model-derived speed are not silently converted into
exact non-equilibrium sensing or validated transient closure.

The conclusion is bounded admission failure, not universal impossibility. A
published all-in-one certificate is unnecessary, but neither the inspected source
packet nor the static synthesis establishes the required history/action chain.
No numerical absence or cost argument substitutes for that methodological gap.
