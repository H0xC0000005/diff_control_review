## The conclusion in traffic terms

**BP-R leaves a credible model-family lead, but does not yet establish the
complete research task.** Rate control can plausibly change how the merge serves
traffic, rather than merely move waiting between queues. The unresolved question
is whether finer disturbance detail changes useful rate decisions after a strong
compact model has retained congestion memory, ramp conditions and credible
capacity physics.

Think of holding ramp traffic briefly so that fewer slow entrants disturb a busy
mainline. The mainline might discharge more effectively, but the ramp vehicles
wait longer. A useful decision must improve the complete exit-time balance,
counting both streams and the clearing tail. A compact model that predicts that
balance correctly is sufficient for this task. We have not established that
finer lane/cohort information changes that decision, or that useful gradients
require it. This is an explanatory mechanism, not a constructed positive case.

The surviving family is **a local active merge with entry/acceleration-dependent
discharge**, with evolving merge disturbances as the candidate retained state.
It is qualified for a further formulation inquiry, not promoted to a fully
admitted study. Exact task laws and minimum representation are still unselected.

## BPR-G3 — disposition and next bounded question

**Decision:** the user selects candidate A. Candidate B is unselected. The
candidate reasoning below is preserved; selection authorizes the bounded
formulation attempt, not its future equations or a promised positive outcome.

**Problem statement:** is the partial family support enough to justify one
bounded formulation attempt, or should this line be parked now? A completed
positive task and a universal failure are both unsupported.

**Assumed model-chain function:** Stage 3 would turn source family → conservative
local task → common feasible rate schedules → complete TST → qualified independent
reductions into one coherent contract. Stage 4 would address sensitivity and
study-readiness only if that contract survives. Neither stage executes traffic.

**Core gap:** no inspected source joins finite receiving/storage, finite demand
and clearance, the common open-loop rate interface, continuous-mass task semantics
and a strong compact-versus-richer control-response comparison. Existing physics
and accounting precedents cannot be silently assembled as a proven model. The
surviving lead warrants investigation, not automatic scientific adoption.

### Candidate A — accept the partial result and open one formulation bridge

Start from the qualified local entry/disturbance family. First settle whether a
single source-faithful reference/task route can meet the accounting and physical
boundaries; then define independent compact/memory/lane-aggregate comparison
roles, projection and acceptance obligations. Source events may be qualified as
reference detail, but a continuous-mass adaptation requires its own explicit
decision and support. A missing law is not filled by a convenient recovery term.

**Pros:** preserves local rate control, complete TST and the approved relative
claim; gives a concrete deliverable rather than another broad mechanism survey;
directly addresses the remaining bridge. Compact sufficiency and formulation
failure remain valid outcomes.

**Cons:** positive control-relevant residual is not assured. A reference or
continuous-state transfer may require a scientific gate. If compact models cover
the relevant response, this will not yield the intended richer-information study.

**Dependencies/consequences:** close Stage 2 on its partial/evidence-limited result.
Only Stage 3's bounded formulation is opened; stop at a concrete adoption choice
or its completed result. No simulation, derivative check or Stage 4 opens. Keep
the source family's actual geometry justified rather than increase lane count to
manufacture a distinction. Do not assume minimum or maximum rate is non-dominant.

### Candidate B — accept the result and park this family

Close Stage 2 with the current physics/comparison lead and explicit missing
certificate, without a further formulation attempt. Preserve BP-U/task re-selection
and the original operational-information route as separately selectable future
work; no alternative opens by parking the family.

**Pros:** avoids repeatedly extending an unsupported joined chain; keeps the
negative/partial evidence honest and leaves a clear colleague-discussion record.
No new modeling assumptions are needed.

**Cons:** leaves a feasible relative-fidelity question unresolved; stops before
testing whether one coherent formulation can bridge the identified gaps.

**Dependencies/consequences:** branch work ends at the documented result. A later
restart must select its specific contract and question; parking is not an
impossibility claim or approval of timing/multi-ramp changes.

**Recommendation accepted: A**, as a single bounded formulation attempt.
There is enough physical/family support to justify that inquiry, and the revised
claim removes the old in-principle-information prerequisite. There is insufficient
support to approve a research-ready task now. Failure to produce a coherent
source-to-task bridge should end the attempt; no further mechanism is added just
to obtain a positive outcome.
