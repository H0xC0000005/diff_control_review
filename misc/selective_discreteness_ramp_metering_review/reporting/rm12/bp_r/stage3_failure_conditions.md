# Stage 3 — explicit failure and stop conditions

Date: 2026-10-06. Requested planning clarification before substantive formulation.
These conditions make the inherited Stage 3 admission boundaries explicit; they
are **not newly found failures or literature theorems**. No task law, numerical
tolerance, universal novelty requirement or derivative method is adopted.
[Candidate A selection](user_closure_and_stage3_selection.md) remains approved.

The target is a defensible local ramp-rate task in which the adequacy of retained
traffic state can be studied through control response and complete system time.
It is not a requirement to discover unknown information, invent a new state
variable, beat every aggregate model or find a universally best representation.

## Interpretation of the user's three examples

**1. Supported information sets are already covered by existing methods.**
This establishes prior art and identifies serious comparators. It does not by
itself establish that the relevant response/gradient comparison has been answered.
An existing representation can be studied for how much of it must be retained,
under which conditions it suffices, and whether it preserves useful sensitivity.
If existing work already establishes the same task, representations, common-rate
response, complete objective and intended sensitivity claim over the relevant
domain, the remaining contribution must be identified. An exact duplicate can
justify parking the study, but no general new-algorithm requirement is imposed.
Use N1 below for contribution reassessment, rather than silently making novelty
an additional mandatory Stage 3 scientific criterion.

If prior work already answers the complete intended question, that can be a
successful source-supported review conclusion even when a proposed new study
adds little. Scientific resolution and novelty of a new project are separate.

**2. No fixed representation works universally; genuine ambiguity breaks
assumptions or differentiability.** Tradeoffs and regime dependence are compatible
with this study. A family-dependent state requirement may be a useful answer.
BP-R does not require an equal-full-history ambiguity pair, so inability to
construct one is not a failure here. Nonsmooth events are not alone proof that
credible sensitivities are impossible. The actual negative would be absence of
any defensible task/comparison or sensitivity route within the approved scope.
Unsupported smoothing, freezing event consequences, integerizing physical mass
or private reference information cannot be used to manufacture such a route.

**3. Aggregation already resolves the task.** This is a real negative for the
claim that finer detail is needed **if** a qualified independent compact model
preserves the response relevant to the admitted rate-control problem. Matching
mean capacity, one trajectory or one final optimum is weaker. Such sufficiency
is a useful scoped result, not failure of the review procedure or proof that all
freeway control problems are aggregate-sufficient.

## Stage 3 admission conditions and evidence burden

| ID | Human-readable failure condition | What would justify it, and the scope of the conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| S3-F1 | **No coherent supported traffic task can be formed.** The entry, disturbance, receiving/storage and clearance rules cannot be joined without unsupported or contradictory assumptions. | Name the incompatible rules or missing transfer. A defensible analytic adaptation is allowed; a single paper covering the entire chain is not required. Contradiction supports scoped rejection; missing support after the bounded frontier supports evidence-limited non-admission. |
| S3-F2 | **The meter provides no relevant control leverage.** Feasible rate changes cannot affect the complete control outcome, or merely relocate waiting while leaving exit histories unchanged. | A static argument or source result under the declared demand/initialization/action domain. Include ramp and entrance waiting through clearance. An inactive/empty-ramp example is an equivalence control, not a rejection of an otherwise active family. |
| S3-F3 | **A qualified compact model is already sufficient.** Added lane/group/order detail provides no response needed by the admitted control question beyond a credible independent aggregate/memory/statistic closure. | State the particular compact model, projection, dynamics, input interface, control family and regime. Show preservation of the relevant rate response; do not infer it from average-capacity fit or deterministic initial-state knowledge alone. One adequate compact closure can defeat a finer-state necessity claim in its domain; no theorem about every macro model is needed. |
| S3-F4 | **The remaining fine-model difference has no control relevance.** More detail changes local traffic descriptions, but not the outcome or decision-sensitive response that the study targets. | Trace the discrepancy to complete TST and common rate interventions. Different queue locations or unrelated microscopic patterns are insufficient. The same final optimum alone does not establish this: useful local sensitivity or perturbation prediction could still differ. |
| S3-F5 | **The apparent advantage cannot survive a fair comparison.** It depends on private fine truth, future-reference inputs, extra actuators, weaker initialization, per-intervention fitting or omitted waiting. | Identify the confound and attempt the smallest in-scope correction. Reject the purported certificate if it disappears or cannot be repaired. If repair requires a different scientific contract, stop for adoption rather than declaring all possible tasks impossible. |
| S3-F6 | **The task only survives by abandoning the agreed setting.** A viable case requires an unsupported law, changed physical mass semantics, unselected release timing, extra ramps/lanes to force distinction, or a different objective. | Name the smallest contract that must change and why. Source micro events may be qualified as reference detail; they are not automatically permission to quantize mainline mass. A justified new contract is an amendment option, not an automatic rejection or approval. |

These six conditions cover task admission and representation/control relevance.
They can stop an attempt without claiming a useful-gradient theorem. Each result
must name its domain; a failed candidate is not proof that every variant fails.

The word aggregate is not itself a sufficiency statement. A lane-aggregate model
already retains lane contrast: its adequacy may remove the need for group identity
while supporting retention of lane information. Every F3 conclusion must name
the actual sufficient state, not claim that all fine traffic information is
dispensable because a source labels its model macroscopic.

## Later study-feasibility concerns, kept distinct

**S4-F1 — no credible control-to-objective sensitivity route.** If the eventual
adopted law structurally removes the relevant rate sensitivity, or its intended
derivative ignores the decisive event/state dependence and no justified approved
alternative can represent it, the gradient study cannot advance as proposed.
Flag a demonstrable structural conflict during Stage 3; otherwise leave detailed
derivative semantics for Stage 4 and the applicable component review. Absence of
numerical gradient validation during this methodology review is not a failure.
Finite gradients, internal objective decrease and mere differentiability are not
positive usefulness certificates either.

**S4-N1 — no remaining meaningful study question.** Existing work might already
establish the full intended chain; alternatively, the only comparison might be
an adequate model versus a deliberately crippled baseline, with no meaningful
state-reduction test, equivalence regime, ablation or mechanism question. This
requires contribution/study-readiness reassessment. Prior use of all candidate
variables alone is insufficient to establish it. A comparative or negative study
can be legitimate; insisting on a new algorithm or a particular novelty threshold
would require an explicit user decision. This is not silently added as a hard
Stage 3 gate.

## Evidence-limited is not proven negative

**S3-E1 — the bounded evidence cannot establish the joined task or consequential
response.** If the nearest source/static frontier ends with an essential unbound
law, accounting transfer or response claim, report that field as unestablished.
Do not keep adding mechanisms until a positive appears, or convert missing
evidence into a sufficiency/impossibility theorem. A well-posed unresolved
performance question can survive as a later experiment obligation; an unbound
traffic/comparison contract cannot.

The reference itself must have a declared justification and role. Comparing a
rich model with its own chosen simplification can establish a relative model-
family statement; it cannot establish physical truth by definition. Qualification
must match the intended claim. Exact field calibration or numerical experiments
are not automatically required to formulate a conditional methodology task.

## Operational decision rule

1. **Continue to a formulation result** when one coherent justified local task,
   meaningful feasible rate response, fair independent strong reductions and a
   specific unresolved representation/control question are explicit. Exact
   numerical performance and gradient correctness/usefulness may remain later
   validation obligations. Do not manufacture a positive hypothesis as a premise.
2. **Close with scoped sufficiency or control irrelevance** when a supported
   argument resolves F2, F3 or F4 negatively for the intended added-detail claim.
3. **Stop for a methodological discussion** when F5/F6 or a demonstrated
   sensitivity conflict requires a concrete new contract; provide its smallest
   viable alternatives before adopting it.
4. **Close the bounded attempt as evidence-limited** when an essential task or
   comparison contract remains unsupported under F1/E1. State what would permit
   reopening. This does not require exhaustive review of all freeway models.

No numerical tolerance, required effect size, cost cutoff, universal superiority
or new novelty bar is introduced here. If a later approximate comparison needs
a tolerance, its meaning and acceptance must be decided explicitly. The stage
does not fail merely because its positive hypothesis has not yet been confirmed
by an experiment that this review does not authorize.

## Ownership and status

The six Stage 3 conditions clarify already accepted scope/fairness/accounting and
the scoped-disposition workflow. Evidence transcription and static assessment
are reviewer-local; a new law, actuator, knowledge contract, derivative meaning
or acceptance threshold is a user-owned global adoption choice. N1 is a later
contribution discussion, not an approved mandatory novelty test. Source-to-task
problems split into evidence F1/E1 and any necessary scientific transfer F6.
No closed decision is reopened merely by listing these possible outcomes.
