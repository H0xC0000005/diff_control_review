The strategy below is preserved; its earlier planning-only execution boundary

**The proposed ordering is sound.** First ask whether the traffic field and
moving restrictions can already describe and advance the same traffic through
a compatible interface. If they cannot, qualify the smallest explicit
adaptation. If that bridge requires unsupported traffic behavior or still
cannot form a coherent model, reconsider the field representation where the
restrictions act. Do not retain ordinary CTM merely to preserve earlier work.

## 1. Look for an existing common interface

Explain traffic counts, restriction positions, accepted flows and update times
in one common state. Check whether the already supported methods can supply
and consume those quantities consistently under the approved meter, priority,
birth and motion choices. A coordinate conversion, unit conversion or explicit
ownership of a transfer is not automatically a new physical law.

“No adaptation” here means no additional adaptation beyond approved choices,
including K.A; it does not mean undoing the review's existing source-to-task
decisions. The aim is one coherent evolution, not merely two matching endpoint
inventories. Restrictions must read the same traffic that their effects alter.

There is a hard limit: changing the name of an interface cannot restore an
empty gap erased by whole-cell averaging. If an existing representation does
not retain the required spatial information, report that concrete limit rather
than silently feeding it a richer reference field. The failure is scoped to
the audited representation/domain, not proof against every CTM-based method.

## 2. Qualify a necessary explicit adaptation

If the first audit leaves a genuine interface/evolution gap, define the smallest
source-grounded adjustment. Explain what changes and why it follows from the
existing traffic mechanism. The current boundary-aware candidate A is relevant:
its reconstruction has a local proof, but its joined motion/flux evolution
still needs qualification. A nearby source method is support, not automatic
equivalence to the accepted K.A/N.A/IT/E1 task.

Distinguish a numerical or representational specification from adding traffic
physics. Numerical methods require their own conditions and qualification;
that does not automatically invent new driving behavior. An added empirical
blockage penalty, arbitrary spacing, launch law or unjustified restriction
interaction would be a physical amendment, not a neutral bridge.

Do not label a change to what the field stores as “interface-only” merely to
keep it in step 1. Boundary-aware A already changes the relevant field basis,
so steps 2 and 3 can overlap. This ordering expresses preference for justified
minimal changes; it is not a strict taxonomy of disjoint algorithms.

## 3. Reconsider the representation in the affected domain

If the supported adaptation is inadequate or requires a forced mechanism,
consider continuous count/wave candidate C where moving restrictions act.
The first target remains the same macroscopic traffic physics and control
task, with a representation that naturally retains the necessary information.
Changing only the ramp is insufficient for the demonstrated mainline mismatch.

The user's ramp-plus-mainline-service-accounting candidate D is a separate
possibility if an effect law is changed or proved to be an equivalent reduction.
It does not automatically follow from replacing ordinary cells. Its physical
and possible meter/birth amendments must be explicit before adoption.

## Mapping to the existing options

| Proposed step | Current option/role | What would justify progress |
|---|---|---|
| Existing interface alignment | Common A/C formulation and interface audit | A coherent own field/motion/flux evolution, or a documented specific incompatibility |
| Explicit justified adaptation | A is the current nearest candidate; not automatically selected | A supported conservative evolution preserving the declared mechanism, or a scoped failure/evidence gap |
| Representation reconsideration | C first if the physical mechanism is retained | A qualified shared count/wave representation; D only if a service-effect change/reduction is explicitly considered |

This is **an A/C qualification strategy**, rather than approval of A alone or
selection of C immediately. C may supply a common reference already during
step 1; borrowing its notation/reference does not adopt its field realization.
A and C may be numerical and reference realizations of the same model.
Unqualified error tolerance B is not part of the proposed route.

Missing literature detail is not proof of impossibility. Each bounded step
should distinguish a positive compatible construction, a supported obstruction,
and an unresolved evidence gap. Moving to the next step requires explaining
which occurred, without weakening the shared consistency criterion or searching
indefinitely for a general theorem. A complete forward representation still
does not establish clearance, useful metering gradients or necessity of finer
information.
