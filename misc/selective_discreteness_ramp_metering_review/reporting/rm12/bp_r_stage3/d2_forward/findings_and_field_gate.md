## Q.J.F.R — what does the regular-clock field refresh preserve?

**Problem statement — Type 2 / user.** Does the next interval start from the
completed ordinary traffic profile, or from a declared numerical reconstruction
using cell totals and restriction/count anchors?

**Assumed model-chain function.** The field is the state between the service
allocator and the next interval. It determines when traffic can reach a port,
the receiving available to other streams, the traffic speed ahead of a
restriction, and the own speed history used at the next request clock.

**Core gap.** The earlier anchor reconstruction proves conservation, jam bounds
and preservation of all selected counts. It does not prove preservation of the
ordinary waves and arrival times between those anchors. The new D2 pair makes
that distinction explicit. We cannot silently turn an admissible reconstruction
into an exactly equivalent state refresh. The previous A1/A2/C numerical
families remain unselected and do not settle this information contract by name.

### A — carry the completed spatial profile into the next interval

At the methodological reference level, retain the completed ordinary density
pieces or equivalent full count profile, together with every exact restriction,
actual transfer and required own history. A regular clock refreshes requests
and kinetic permissions and adds E1 records; it does not replace the ordinary
traffic profile with new averages. Joint waves and port flows remain the source
of the next state.

**Pros:** preserves the spatial arrival information needed by D2; keeps the
exact-chain comparison anchor clear; separates later removal of restriction
information from ordinary traffic-field coarsening; follows the present
exact-first priority without another physical assumption.

**Cons:** an autonomous realization still needs qualification for the admitted
record/port encounters and repeated updates. Prescribed-path theory and the
static node result do not supply that entire coupled construction automatically.
It does not settle clearance or prove a useful controller response.

**Dependencies/consequences:** bind field carryover as a shared reference
contract. A1 moving boundaries, A2 fixed cells with internal pieces, or a
continuous count/wave method may later realize it at an explicitly qualified
accuracy. This choice does not require selecting an implementation algorithm
or proving a general arbitrary-network continuum theorem now. No added vehicle
population or microscopic car-following is involved.

### B — qualify an explicit mass-and-anchor numerical reconstruction

Declare a finite numerical model whose regular refresh rebuilds a jam-bounded
piecewise field from cell totals and all restriction/count anchors. Keep every
record, conserve every fraction of mass and preserve the selected empty gaps.
Treat changed ordinary profiles and timing as a numerical approximation with a
specified reference/refinement relation, rather than calling the refresh lossless.

**Pros:** has a direct connection to the finite-volume source lineage; makes
the finite model level explicit; can preserve all restriction anchors and
conservation while remaining a legitimate numerical candidate if its accuracy
is qualified. A projection difference alone does not refute macroscopic models.

**Cons:** the current pair proves that mass-and-anchor information alone is
insufficient for exact ordinary-field equivalence. Timing differences could
confound an eventual claim about omitted restriction information. Existing
reconstruction and source convergence results do not certify this adapted
multi-record, D2 and E1 stack.

**Dependencies/consequences:** explicitly approve and qualify the ordinary-field
projection and its comparison/reference contract. Exact metadata approval is
not permission for an unspecified field error. This is not the previously
declined arbitrary count-mismatch tolerance; all retained anchors must still
match. B is a possible numerical qualification route, not an already accepted
coarser reference or permission to conduct an experiment.

**Provisional recommendation: A.** First finish the shape-preserving reference
chain, then consider coarser field realizations and reduced competitors. The
reason is causal and comparative: future service needs arrival timing, and
this review should not mix ordinary spatial-field loss with restriction-state
loss. Runtime and storage cost play no part in the recommendation.
