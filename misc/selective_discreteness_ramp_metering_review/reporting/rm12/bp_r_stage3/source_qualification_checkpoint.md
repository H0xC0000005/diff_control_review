## Core conclusion in traffic terms

We can now say what the meter does: hold some offered traffic upstream, admit
the rest into a finite ramp approach, and let that approach determine later
entry into the freeway. The complete objective counts all those waiting places.
That interface is [documented](../../../synthesis/rm12/bp_r_stage3/upstream_admission_interface.md).

The source family gives a credible reason for this control to matter: different
ramp congestion and entry conditions can create different moving disturbances
and freeway discharge. The next missing part is how its exact trajectory and
competing-flow rules implement that mechanism while conserving the continuous
traffic inventory. We have not found evidence refuting the chosen task.
