\section{Scope, notation and what acceptance established}
Use two physical lanes per local longitudinal cell. Mass $n_{i\ell}$ is continuous
vehicle equivalents; $L$ is cell length, $h$ the timestep, and
$N^{\max}_{i\ell}=L k^{\rm jam}_{i\ell}$ its finite storage. Distinguish total
observed cell mass $N_i=n_{i1}+n_{i2}$ from lane maximum storage.
Let $c_\ell=v_\ell/L$, $\gamma_\ell=w_\ell/L$. Ordinary lane sending/receiving are
\begin{equation}
 D_\ell(n)=\min(c_\ell n,C_\ell),\qquad
 S_\ell(n)=\min(C_\ell,\gamma_\ell(N^{\max}_\ell-n)).
\end{equation}
The accepted step bounds $h c_\ell\leq1$, $h\gamma_\ell\leq1$ imply
$hD_\ell(n)\leq n$ and $hS_\ell(n)\leq N^{\max}_\ell-n$.
The bounded discretionary propensity generates at most one positive direction
in a two-lane cell, with actual lateral rate
$\lambda_{\ell j}\leq\min(D_\ell(n_\ell),S_j(n_j))$.
After conservative exchange,
\begin{equation}
 n_\ell^L=n_\ell+h(\lambda_{j\ell}-\lambda_{\ell j}).
\end{equation}
The accepted kernel then limits longitudinal movement by
\begin{align}
 \bar D_\ell&=\min\{D_\ell(n_\ell^L),D_\ell(n_\ell)-\lambda_{\ell j}\},\\
 \bar S_\ell&=\min\{S_\ell(n_\ell^L),S_\ell(n_\ell)-\lambda_{j\ell}\}.
 \label{eq:residual}
\end{align}
Ordinary forward interfaces and the Default priority merge use these budgets.
An upstream meter still bounds upstream release; it is not automatically a
bound on instantaneous downstream entry. Ramp and external waiting need their
corresponding conserved states. Exact local geometry and observer support are
not yet frozen by handout acceptance.

\section{What passes mathematically; a lossless information coordinate}
The lateral step keeps each lane in $[0,N^{\max}_\ell]$ and conserves their sum.
Residual budgets are nonnegative. With accepted forward/merge incoming rate
$f^{\rm in}_{i\ell}\leq\bar S_{i\ell}$ and outgoing rate
$q_{i\ell}\leq\bar D_{i\ell}$,
\begin{equation}
 n^+_{i\ell}=n^L_{i\ell}+h(f^{\rm in}_{i\ell}-q_{i\ell})
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 0\leq n^+_{i\ell}\leq N^{\max}_{i\ell}.
\end{equation}
Every internal accepted transfer appears once with each sign. Thus the
operator is conservative and storage-bounded, conditional on corresponding
ramp/boundary balances. This is a proof of an update, not complete-task geometry
or physical-fidelity acceptance.

For two lanes, one lane coordinate together with road total retains the entire
cell's lane information:
\begin{equation}
 z_i=(N_i,b_i),\qquad b_i=n_{i1},\qquad
 (n_{i1},n_{i2})=(b_i,N_i-b_i).
\end{equation}
Equivalently use $\delta_i=n_{i1}-n_{i2}$, giving
$n_{i1}=(N_i+\delta_i)/2$, $n_{i2}=(N_i-\delta_i)/2$.
This is a lossless reparameterization of the accepted two-lane reference,
not compression of several longitudinal cells into one zone-wide scalar,
proof that contrast is hidden, or necessity of individual vehicle identity.

With positive density denominators and fixed strict branches of min/max,
the propensity, conservation and priority operators are differentiable
compositions. At a switch or empty-state denominator, an ordinary derivative
is not asserted. Correct branch algebra alone does not verify the full task's
gradient or independent one-step usefulness.

\section{The new consequence: an implicit forward-service debit}
Consider an ordinary cell away from the ramp-entry interface. Both pre/post
sending functions are on uncapped free-flow branches, and downstream residual
receiving is sufficient for both forward offers. Suppose lateral transfer
$\lambda>0$ goes from lane $d$ to lane $t$:
\begin{equation}
 n_d^L=n_d-h\lambda,\qquad n_t^L=n_t+h\lambda.
\end{equation}
The accepted residual law yields
\begin{align}
 \bar D_d&=\min(c_d n_d-hc_d\lambda,c_d n_d-\lambda)
           =c_d n_d-\lambda,\\
 \bar D_t&=\min(c_t n_t+hc_t\lambda,c_t n_t)=c_t n_t,
 \label{eq:free}
\end{align}
since $hc_d\leq1$. Consequently the total longitudinal rate is
\begin{equation}
 Q=c_d n_d+c_t n_t-\lambda.\label{eq:debit}
\end{equation}
The comparison with lateral movement disabled has the same initial state
and ordinary sending parameters: $Q_0=c_d n_d+c_t n_t$.
This is a local ablation diagnostic, not two identical-observation histories.

\section{Meaning, a hand calculation, and source limits}
Equation~\eqref{eq:debit} does not require receiving congestion, lane-speed
equality, a speed-dependent ramp cost or an explicit capacity-drop coefficient.
The donor loses one unit of forward-rate budget per unit lateral-rate transfer;
the target cannot increase sending beyond its original budget.
The debit persists as $h\to0$ at fixed cell state/length/propensity. It is
therefore not merely a disappearing finite-timestep truncation effect.

\paragraph{Hand-calculated feasible branch.}
Use illustrative consistent units, $L=1$, $h=1/4$, $c_d=1$, $c_t=2$,
$n_d=6$, $n_t=2$, $\mu=1/2$, $\kappa=1$. Select finite capacities, storage
and downstream receiving sufficiently large that all stated branches are strict.
The propensity and transfer are
\begin{equation}
 A=\frac12\frac{6-2}{6+2}=\frac14,\qquad \lambda=A c_d n_d=\frac32.
\end{equation}
Post-lateral masses are $45/8$ and $19/8$. Their ordinary sending rates are
$45/8$ and $19/4$, but the residual budgets impose
\begin{equation}
 q_d=\min(45/8,6-3/2)=9/2,\qquad
 q_t=\min(19/4,4)=4,\qquad Q=17/2.
\end{equation}
The no-lateral initial offer is $Q_0=10$; the difference is exactly $3/2$.
Mass is still conserved. No traffic trajectory or numerical experiment was run.
The lane mobilities are different, avoiding a symmetry selected to conceal state.

\paragraph{Scientific interpretation.}
A common movement resource can be a deliberately stipulated model: sideways
and forward transfers compete for that resource. This is not mathematically
impossible. But the accepted proposal's conservation proof does not establish
that within-cell lane changing consumes longitudinal service one for one in
physical traffic. The earlier phrase ``no capacity-drop penalty'' must be read
narrowly as no \emph{explicit} acceleration/capacity-drop law. It is not a
no-throughput-cost claim: \eqref{eq:debit} demonstrates an implicit debit.
The earlier explanation was insufficiently explicit about its magnitude.

Source FB0030 [1], printed equations 20--22, adjusts sending with both lateral
inflow and outflow; the accepted target's old-sending cap is not that complete
source coupling. Its unresolved branches are not repaired here. RM0018 [2]
specifies sharing at an input-to-output node; it does not establish the debit
for a transverse within-cell transfer. Neither inspected source validates
\eqref{eq:debit} as this task's physical lane-changing cost.

A meter perturbation could alter lane contrast, hence $\lambda$ and this debit.
A resulting internal gradient may be correct for the stipulated resource law,
but does not independently prove a real metering benefit, hidden information,
or total-time improvement through common clearance. Different final departure
history is still required; a local flow difference is not sufficient.

\section{Conditional strong-observer reconstruction, not a certificate}
This diagnostic retains an \emph{independent} speed channel rather than silently
replacing speed with total flow divided by density. On the ordinary strict
free-flow branch above, suppose known distinct lane speeds $v_1\ne v_2$ are
also the lane passage speeds at the co-supported observation. Let the observer
receive $N=n_1+n_2$, $Q=q_1+q_2>0$ and passage-weighted speed
\begin{equation}
 V=\frac{v_1 q_1+v_2 q_2}{Q}.
\end{equation}
Then the two individual realized flows reconstruct algebraically:
\begin{equation}
 q_1=\frac{Q(V-v_2)}{v_1-v_2},\qquad
 q_2=\frac{Q(v_1-V)}{v_1-v_2}.
\end{equation}
Define $x_\ell=q_\ell/c_\ell$ and $\epsilon=N-x_1-x_2$.
Assume both lane masses are positive, and only the discretionary coefficient
$\mu\in[0,1]$, $\kappa\in(0,1]$ operates. If $\lambda=0$, then $\epsilon=0$
and $n_\ell=x_\ell$. If lateral flow is active, \eqref{eq:free} gives
\begin{equation}
 \epsilon=\lambda/c_d>0,\qquad x_d=n_d-\epsilon,\qquad x_t=n_t.
\end{equation}
The active propensity is bounded by the unbiased density difference:
\begin{equation}
 \epsilon=A n_d\leq\frac{n_d(n_d-n_t)}{n_d+n_t}<n_d-n_t,
 \qquad n_d>n_t>0.
\end{equation}
Hence $x_d>x_t$ and the donor is identifiable from normalized realized flows.
Recover the original state without knowing the hidden branch in advance:
\begin{equation}
 d=\mathop{\rm argmax}_{\ell\in\{1,2\}}x_\ell,\qquad
 n_d=x_d+\epsilon,\quad n_t=x_t.
\end{equation}
At zero transfer, $n=x$ directly; no argmax is needed.
Thus, under this explicitly stated speed map and branch, even a snapshot of
aggregate mass/flow/independent speed identifies lane occupancy. A full equal
history cannot conceal different current lane masses in this subcase.

This is a \textbf{conditional reviewer theorem}: the accepted kernel does not
itself supply a calibrated independent detector-speed map, so the diagnostic is
not adopted as the complete observer. It excludes capacity/supply-bound cells,
empty recipients, equal lane speeds, different observation supports and changing
lane passage speeds. No conclusion of global congested-state or field
reconstruction follows. No weaker speed channel or exact history pair was
substituted to make the research answer positive.

\section{The concrete alternative for RM9-UG3.B}
\noindent\textbf{Proposed, not adopted.} Keep the bounded lateral exchange and
its finite mass/storage proof. Treat it as a transverse occupancy transfer,
separate from the longitudinal face's flow capacity. After it, recompute
\begin{equation}
 D^B_{i\ell}=D_{i\ell}(n^L_{i\ell}),\qquad
 S^B_{i\ell}=S_{i\ell}(n^L_{i\ell}).\label{eq:B}
\end{equation}
Ordinary longitudinal flows use these limits. Mainline and ramp offers still
share the same longitudinal receiving budget via the Default allocator.
No lateral transfer is also charged against the original longitudinal-rate
budget. This changes the physical resource interpretation; it is not a clerical
removal of a redundant min or an approved correction.

With incoming longitudinal/ramp rate $f^B\leq S^B(n^L)$ and outgoing
$q^B\leq D^B(n^L)$, the same step bounds give
\begin{equation}
 n^+=n^L+h(f^B-q^B)\in[0,N^{\max}].
\end{equation}
Internal transfer signs still cancel. Ramp/external balances remain mandatory.
Total accepted transverse-plus-longitudinal incoming rate need not be below the
\emph{original} longitudinal $S$; it is not being claimed to use one flow-capacity
resource. Both stages do respect mass/storage at their respective states.
This distinction resolves the earlier ``double capacity'' concern only by
explicitly changing which resource the two movements share. It requires approval.

On the same uncapped, unimpeded ordinary branch,
\begin{equation}
 Q_B=c_d(n_d-h\lambda)+c_t(n_t+h\lambda)
     =Q_0+h(c_t-c_d)\lambda.
\end{equation}
There is no universal $-\lambda$ forward debit; changes follow the redistributed
state and differing lane mobilities. At fixed state as $h\to0$, this local step
rate approaches $Q_0$. This is not proof of perfect traffic fidelity, a globally
best unmetered policy, or a positive hidden-state result. The propensity's physical
spatial scale, step splitting, full observer and geometry still require review.
No restored source branch, acceleration state or deferred X1--X3 mechanism is
added. Only the residual flow-capacity debits are proposed for reconsideration.
