## What the literature admits

The empirical merge mechanism and continuous/hybrid model structures provide a
credible starting family. The source packet also contains serious aggregate
alternatives and a nearby gap-timing predecessor. The candidate task is therefore
more concrete than generic differentiable ramp optimization, but has no inherited
proof that lane information is needed.

| Level | Information / dynamics | Status in current task |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | Mainline road-total state/history, ramp queue/history, common demand and geometry | Required strong comparator; avoid a snapshot-only straw baseline |
| M0+ | History-derived capacity memory; admitted speed dynamics and separate stream flows | Must be considered before claiming omitted local-state value |
| M1 | Added compact local merge statistic | Candidate; establish whether history already identifies it and whether its dynamics close |
| M2 | Local shoulder lane versus remaining lanes, or detailed lane-continuous state | Candidate family; never assume M2 adds information beyond every M1 encoding |
| M3 | Local gaps, acceleration/relaxation or particle interactions | Possible mechanistic reference; gradients and same-rate interpretation unresolved |

This is a sufficiency ladder, not an approved implementation or predetermined
complexity hierarchy. A two-group occupancy split adds one independent contrast
once the local total is known. Encoding that contrast as one scalar retains the
same information. Calling it scalar does not make it a weaker baseline.
A scalar discharge penalty, conversely, need not have adequate state dynamics.
The relevant test is predictive/intervention sufficiency under the declared
observer, rather than the number of variable names.

## The strongest unresolved information test

First transcribe the complete observer: spatial cells/stations, temporal samples,
road-total density/counts/flows/speed, ramp mass and history, initialization,
geometry, forcing and prior controls. Determine which memories it identifies.
Two different lane snapshots are insufficient if earlier speeds or flows reveal
which case occurred. A finite history starting after an unobserved event cannot
silently replace the complete declared history.

If two feasible histories match exactly, ask whether a common meter-rate
perturbation has different all-stream consequences. A forward difference alone
is weaker than different preferred actions. If a compact additional statistic
resolves the difference, report that sufficiency rather than assert full lane
necessity. An observationally identical pair supports a limitation of that
observer, not impossibility for every conceivable macro model.

## Source support versus proposed bridge

Source support and restrictions are in the four [source studies](../extraction/rm8_source_studies/isolated_merge_and_hybrid.md).
The proposed bridge is a compact local mechanism audit: conservation and finite
receiving, history identifiability, fair perturbations, then derivative meaning.
No source has been shown here to supply that complete bridge. Its success is
an open research question; a strong negative or sufficient compact model is a
valid outcome.
