## What is genuinely different from the earlier observer?

The source supports an operational detector-input setting. It does not certify
the earlier benchmark's exact whole-section mass and equilibrium, mass-weighted
section-speed channels. This is a legitimate reason to re-examine transfer of
the RM9 observer result, rather than introduce additional lanes to manufacture
hiddenness. The site's geometry is evidence, not an adopted task change.

However, a published algorithm's input list is a lower bound on what its program
uses, not an upper bound on what a fair competing controller could access. The
strong comparator must retain available lane/event records, other detector
streams, historical logs and public commands. A restrictive information contract
cannot be inferred from silence about those records. Hardware capability also
does not prove that all possible records were retained or accessible.

## Why this does not repeat old candidate B — and where it would

Old RM9-UG5.B would classify uncertain starts under the approved elementary law
and observer C. BP-I first asked whether a genuine operational contract supports
a different, justified starting domain. The source-support distinction is real,
and the expanded-access audit is new work. But it stops short of binding the
complete contract. Assigning an unknown start to the old law now and searching
for more roots would return to B without resolving that prerequisite. Likewise,
discarding logs or making future acceleration private would change the question.

## Disposition and remaining burden

**Evidence-limited, with an operational-input distinction supported.** The bounded
audit is complete, but the original hidden-history route has not passed full
domain admission. It supplies neither a positive history/action certificate nor
a universal/scoped site impossibility proof. Some sufficiency results cannot be
transferred automatically; that alone is not a positive research result.

If the original route is retained, the next bounded evidence question is the
actual site information interface: its initialized physical knowledge, earliest
accessible records, lane/event format and release/entry coverage. Only after
that boundary is supported can a candidate residual be tested against complete
history, strong aggregate memory and useful common rates. More modeling detail
does not answer the missing factual prerequisite.

BP-R offers a different claim: test whether qualified aggregate/memory reductions
preserve intervention response relative to a richer, source-supported family,
with common declared initialization and forcing. It does not require proving
irreducible information loss to an ideal-history observer. Its adoption remains
a user decision, and it still needs genuine control leverage and complete
accounting. No physical mechanism or comparator is selected here.
