## Forward chain attempted

1. **Admission:** permission and approach receiving bound the accepted boundary
   transfer. Waiting is debited and approach population credited once.
2. **Traffic propagation and requests:** own KW traffic and source lane-choice
   support produce through/lateral requests and approach demand. Existing
   restrictions must affect the same traffic solution used for these quantities.
3. **Allocation:** N.A spends one receiving budget at the mandatory merge and
   reallocates unused shares. Local discretionary transfers retain source IT.
4. **Accepted transfers and sources:** paired changes update population and
   movement counters. An accepted lateral move already advances longitudinally
   to its target section; it is not moved twice.
5. **Restriction creation and evolution:** accepted counters determine descriptor
   generation under the chosen clock. Each retained record uses own downstream
   traffic, source acceleration and its count condition, feeding back to service.
6. **Exits/objective:** integrate every population inventory, including outside
   waiting, to clearing or a certified integrable tail. Only exits remove mass.

This is a coupled problem; the numbered interfaces are not a proof that an
arbitrary sequential split gives the correct solution. Naming a solver operator
does not establish its existence or provide a missing reset rule.

## A newly resolved accounting subproblem

A source-free lane has a cumulative label that is constant on a zero-relative-
passing path. With lateral sources, that statement needs a correction. Define
the lane label by accepted upstream boundary count minus population between the
boundary and a position. Its time derivative is local flow minus all net lateral
sources upstream of that position. The derivative on a moving path additionally
subtracts speed times density. Thus a path with zero relative passing changes
its label by the negative of the upstream lateral-source total.

This is exact conservation bookkeeping, with no recovery approximation. It
can be computed from own accepted transfer ledgers. An entering unit upstream
of the path lowers this label by one; an upstream exit raises it by one.
Ordinary boundary arrivals have a different role because they also advance the
upstream boundary count. The full derivation and discrete paired identity are
in the PDF.

The formula holds on regular segments and in the appropriately defined weak/
discrete balance. A point source exactly on a path requires a one-sided source
attachment convention; the formula alone does not choose it. At a point ramp
boundary, the lane's boundary count includes both accepted incoming streams.

## What exact retention removes, and what it cannot supply

Retaining joint states removes the prior need to reconstruct the unknown
within-group position/speed distribution. Retaining own field/history removes
the need to borrow reference speed estimates. Exact source accounting removes
an erroneous global constant-label assumption.

However, retaining every record after birth cannot specify when it was born.
The supplied journal defines sampled cumulative floors. It does not uniquely
specify the whole approved point-node reset, insertion phase/location for all
sampled discretionary births, simultaneous events and source-correct count
attachment. A continuous actual-threshold model is a plausible explicitly
identified extension; sampled births are another source-nearer specification.
Neither complete joined law has been adopted.

The local clock example demonstrates that the same accepted counter endpoint
and same number of retained records permit different activation times if the
clock is unbound. It is not a reached common-control traffic pair, an equal-
history ambiguity certificate or a TST improvement example. The gap is a law
interface, not insufficient memory or unacceptable exact-state cost.

## Conditional equivalence and inactive behavior

If a well-defined common joined update commutes with descriptor renaming and
the retained state preserves all its arguments, independent models initialized
with equivalent exact states have equivalent states and exit histories at every
step. Induction proves equal full TST if clearance/tails are also common and
qualified. This is conditional on the joined update, not a proof that it exists.

An inactive constraint must not impose an extra scalar debit. Retaining a
nonbinding record is harmless only when the source coupling truly ignores its
nonbinding constraint while retaining its own trajectory for future activity.
Its prior waves remain in the population field. No permanent deletion based
only on present inactivity is needed or adopted.
