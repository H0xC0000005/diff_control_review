## 2. Dependent bookkeeping established

At each regular update, retain the original sampled desired movement masses.
Unspent numerical claims equal those masses minus integrated actual transfers.
They are nonnegative and decrease only when the corresponding actual transfer
occurs. They own no population or individual route identity. Pair each actual
flow once; do not debit a claim as if it were a second traffic ledger.

Use own traffic availability and one receiving resource. Stop a movement
exactly when its allowance is fulfilled. For a constant local accepted rate,
the time to allowance exhaustion is its unspent amount divided by that rate;
it is an event bound, not a public-interval nominal rate ceiling. An exhausted
movement cannot retry lane choice inside the same regular step. Subthreshold
E1 counters and unserved population stay in the state. Reset/refresh desired
requests only at the next approved public clock.

Joint donor outgoing mass must obey its own physical supply. The aggregate
source ceiling under the inherited CFL follows from conservation and maximum
speed; individual oriented ceilings do not follow automatically from that
aggregate ceiling. An exact fully blocked-through/available-lateral case
demonstrates a correct early allowance-exhaustion event and subsequent field.

## 5. Source qualification and its limit

Original IT supplies current discrete-step incoming oriented demands and a
common proportional receiving factor. In a source-native sampled update,
those arguments stay at the regular time. A richer internally evolving field
makes a temporal interpretation necessary.

Primary node literature distinguishes demands from competition priorities;
fixed priorities can remain constant when queues increase physical demand.
Demand-proportional instantaneous rules can be non-invariant, but source-
consistent wave completions can exist. The new local formula demonstrates
that possibility on the actual one-fork/two-target graph under declared
conditions. It is not a proof for all oriented networks, both constrained
recipients, zero-speed/coincident records or quota-depleted modes.

The parallel recipient in both local kernels must have supply at least the
donor's maximum oriented through flow; the common-capacity free recipient
in the comparison meets this condition.

The dynamic-system node paper has cumulative transferred amounts and
completion events. Its internal node execution and input deadlines cannot
simply be identified with our physical regular clock when actual arrival/
receiving changes. No borrowed deadline or new rate parameter is adopted.
