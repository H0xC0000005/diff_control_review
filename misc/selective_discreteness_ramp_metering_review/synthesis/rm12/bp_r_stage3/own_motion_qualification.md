## Qualification results and exposed defects

**Positive:** source-backed finite-step feedback exists; a density-average
predictor and conservative field/node/reset phases can be written explicitly;
all exact records can remain present. Pairwise zero-step limitations do not
make that information mathematically inadmissible. Continuous source transfers
are compatible with discrete effect descriptors.

**Not qualified for freezing:** the displayed spatial predictor, one-sided
attachments, time phase and projected activation are not all bound by the
published methods for our adapted task. The direct zero-capacity boundary
transcription has a finite-step mismatch when a free-speed path is nonbinding.
The count theorem does not guarantee convergence of feedback on a shrinking
window, especially once paths and accepted sources are endogenous.

The free-speed diagnostic assumes an empty/free-compatible recipient road,
one already retained boundary record, positive offered through demand, free
downstream and no other sources during the diagnostic. It tests a local
transcription; it is not a reached pair of metering policies or an amendment
excluding births and lane changes from the admitted task. Ordinary numerical
error is permitted, but no unqualified failure is silently recast as an
accepted exact inactive result.

## Clearance, TST and comparison

Finite offered and initial mass and forward-directed cell transfers bound the
cumulative mass carried by advancing moves. Cumulative-floor progress then bounds total
new descriptors, with initial remainders included. This is a geometric
bookkeeping deduction, not an information-budget/cost condition. It fails as
stated if a later task admits cyclic/backward population transfers; such a
task change would require rechecking it. The bound is for the declared finite
grid, not a uniform bound under arbitrary mesh refinement.

Finite descriptors do not prove physical or numerical clearing. Finite-time
count convergence controls finite-time inventory/TST under common offered
arrivals, not the full clearing tail. Uniform objective convergence does not
imply derivative convergence. No reached rate-to-exit leverage certificate or
gradient result has been produced.

Strong spatial aggregate and memory rivals can also retain wave/receiving
effects; this pass does not establish fine-record necessity or macro failure.
The exact chain remains the reference endpoint to qualify before a genuinely
reduced comparison. Stage 4 stays unopened.
