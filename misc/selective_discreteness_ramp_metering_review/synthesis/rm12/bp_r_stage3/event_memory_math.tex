\section*{1. Threshold progress is exact event bookkeeping}
Let $e$ denote one source descriptor-generating transfer stream (origin cell,
origin lane, target lane). At consecutive source sampling times, $g_e^k$ is
its cumulative actual transferred vehicle-equivalent mass. Let $m_*>0$ be
the source threshold unit, $\Delta n_e^k\ge0$ the actual mass transferred
between these samples, and $z_e^k$ its dimensionless remainder. Then
\begin{equation}
 g_e^{k+1}=g_e^k+\Delta n_e^k,\quad
 z_e^k=\frac{g_e^k}{m_*}-\left\lfloor\frac{g_e^k}{m_*}\right\rfloor,
 \quad 0\le z_e^k<1.
\end{equation}
The new descriptor count $b_e^k$ and next remainder satisfy
\begin{equation}
 b_e^k=\left\lfloor\frac{g_e^{k+1}}{m_*}\right\rfloor-
 \left\lfloor\frac{g_e^k}{m_*}\right\rfloor
 =\left\lfloor z_e^k+\frac{\Delta n_e^k}{m_*}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad z_e^{k+1}=z_e^k+\frac{\Delta n_e^k}{m_*}-b_e^k.
\end{equation}
The identity follows by removing the integer part of $g_e^k/m_*$.
It handles more than one threshold crossing in a sample. No within-sample
birth interpolation is added. Use the same placement of sampling times as
the source's cumulative-transfer convention, not a new event scheduler.

All traffic mass $\Delta n_e^k$ remains in the paired donor/receiver balance.
The descriptor count is metadata. Zero new births does not imply zero old
restrictions, and a descriptor's existence does not imply that it binds.
Store a remainder for every generating stream, not just a single ramp total.
The result is conditional on given own-model transfers; it does not force
two independently evolving models to produce identical births.

\section*{2. Source-sampled free motion admits age-based reconstruction}
On a level road, the supplied acceleration is
$a(v)=a_0(1-v/v_{\max})$, with $a_0>0$ (m/s$^2$) and vehicle parameter
$v_{\max}>0$ (m/s). The KW free speed $u$ is a different quantity.
Consider only the subcase in which downstream traffic offers speed $u$
throughout a restriction's path, $0\le v_b<u<v_{\max}$, and
$r=1-a_0\Delta t/v_{\max}\in(0,1)$.
Let $v_n$ be realized speed after $n$ source motion steps since birth.
The supplied desired-speed and downstream-cap operations reduce to
\begin{equation}
 v_{n+1}=\min\{u,rv_n+a_0\Delta t\},\qquad v_0=v_b.
\end{equation}
Induction gives
\begin{equation}
 v_n=\min\{u,v_{\max}-(v_{\max}-v_b)r^n\},\qquad
 x_n=x_b+\Delta t\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}v_j.
\end{equation}
The position sum uses the supplied current-speed update, not the next speed.
For the induction, the affine map is increasing and maps $u$ to a value
above $u$. Once clipped to $u$, the subsequent clipped value remains $u$.
The first source step attaining $u$ is
\begin{equation}
 n_* = \left\lceil
 \frac{\log((v_{\max}-u)/(v_{\max}-v_b))}{\log r}
 \right\rceil.
\end{equation}
The continuous free-motion calculation is only a diagnostic limit:
\begin{equation}
 V(s)=v_{\max}-(v_{\max}-v_b)e^{-a_0s/v_{\max}},\qquad
 \tau_u=\frac{v_{\max}}{a_0}
 \log\frac{v_{\max}-v_b}{v_{\max}-u}.
\end{equation}
It is not substituted for the reference's sampled update. If $u=v_{\max}$,
finite exact attainment is not supplied by that expression; this boundary
must not be hidden by a timeout or tolerance.

Birth-step, position and speed records therefore reconstruct free kinetics,
and groups with identical birth conditions share a path. Under general traffic,
the realized speed uses a local downstream cap and the next desired speed
starts from that realized speed. A free birth-age clock does not encode a
later slowdown or its changed position. No transient flow/service map follows
from this kinematic reconstruction alone.

\section*{3. Count and mean speed do not generally close the capped update}
This is a local algebraic test, not a constructed freeway experiment.
Write the level-road desired update as $Y=\phi(v)=b+cv$, with
$b=a_0\Delta t>0$, $c=r\in(0,1)$. With a common next-step downstream
cap $d$, the realized next speed is $\min(d,Y)$.
Choose symbolic $\varepsilon>0$ such that $b<d-2\varepsilon$ and
$(d+2\varepsilon-b)/c\le u$. Two possible input lists have desired speeds
\begin{equation}
 (Y_1,Y_2)=(d-\varepsilon,d+\varepsilon),\qquad
 (\widetilde Y_1,\widetilde Y_2)=(d-2\varepsilon,d+2\varepsilon).
\end{equation}
Their current speed lists are $(Y_i-b)/c$, so both have count two and
the same current speed sum $2(d-b)/c$. However,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{i=1}^2\min(d,Y_i)=2d-\varepsilon,\qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^2\min(d,\widetilde Y_i)=2d-2\varepsilon.
\end{equation}
For any finite desired-speed counting measure $\mu$, the missing quantity is
visible through
\begin{equation}
 \int\min(d,y)\,\mu(dy)
 =\int y\,\mu(dy)-\int(y-d)_+\,\mu(dy).
\end{equation}
Knowing the first integral alone does not determine the cap-dependent tail.
At a cap with no atom, the derivative of the left side with respect to $d$
equals $\mu((d,\infty))$. A changing cap can query different parts of the
distribution. Without capping, the affine sum would be closed by count and
speed sum.

\textbf{Limits.} This rules out that particular exact moment update over the
displayed admissible local inputs. It does not establish their reachability
under a common metering schedule, identical observed histories, a TST effect,
or the impossibility of every finite summary. The relevant task may have an
invariant restricted distribution or a different sufficient aggregate closure.

\section*{4. An unlabelled configuration is an exact endpoint, not compression}
Let $\mathcal K$ denote the ordinary continuous traffic field, waiting and
any cumulative-count state required by the source solver. For lane $\ell$,
let $\eta_\ell$ record retained descriptors with position $x_i$ and realized
speed $v_i$ at a consistently chosen source step:
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:measure}
 \eta_\ell=\sum_{i:\ell_i=\ell}\delta_{(x_i,v_i)},\qquad
 \eta_\ell(\mathbb R\times[0,u])=\hbox{descriptor count, not traffic mass}.
\end{equation}
If type parameters differ, include their fixed marks; no heterogeneity is
introduced by this notation. Given a well-defined source update depending on
physical positions/speeds and fixed structural marks, but not arbitrary names,
its action on the measure can be written schematically as
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:transport}
 \eta_\ell^{k+1}
 = (\Phi_{\mathcal K^k,\eta^k})_\#\eta_\ell^k
   +\mathcal B_\ell^k-\mathcal D_\ell^k.
\end{equation}
$\Phi$ denotes the source motion map, $\#$ its push-forward (moving every
record according to that map), $\mathcal B$ sampled own-transfer births,
and $\mathcal D$ exits or removals proved permanently redundant under the
declared domain. A temporary nonbinding observation is not by itself a
permanent-removal proof. This is a representation identity, not a new PDE,
histogram transport rule, or certified joined solver.

Permuting descriptor names leaves $\eta$ unchanged and, under that coupling
assumption, leaves physical constraints unchanged. Positions still encode
spatial order. At atomic resolution this retains one record per descriptor,
so it does not demonstrate a small fixed-dimensional memory.

A grouped position/speed representation needs a declared projection,
independent update and service law. Averaging trajectories, spreading a point
restriction over a whole bin, or summing separate capacity penalties is not
entailed by \eqref{eq:measure}--\eqref{eq:transport}. None is adopted here. The question about this broader
state family is the next user discussion, S3-G2-Q-M.

\textbf{Overall result.} Event progress is exact; free kinetic memory is
conditionally supported; one general clock or count/mean-speed update is not
qualified. A genuinely compressed source-consistent recovery/service law,
joined events/clearance, complete TST response and all gradient claims remain
open. Stage 4 and experiments remain unopened.
