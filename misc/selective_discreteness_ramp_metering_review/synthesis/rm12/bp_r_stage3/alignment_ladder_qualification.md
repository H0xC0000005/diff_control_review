## 3. Step 2: explicit construction and source-method audit

The anchor reconstruction preserves fixed boundary totals and each retained
count when count drops lie in the nonnegative jam-bounded range. Equal-count
intervals remain vacuum. This proof is inherited, not represented as new
global evolution evidence.

The new two-restriction diagnostic adds an exact own-field evolution before
contacts or downstream exit. Two separate congested blocks move with their
restrictions; the preceding fronts are admissible KW shocks. Integrated
upstream input accounts for the growth of the first block. Both local count
labels are constant. Every position, density, flux and count comes from the
same analytic field. Metadata carry zero mass. The state requires two active
downward density jumps; a single-discontinuity reconstruction cannot represent
that exact field. This is a restriction on importing a particular single-shock
method unchanged, not a refutation of multi-discontinuity reconstruction.

Two viable numerical subfamilies are now explicit: moving anchor boundaries
(A1), and fixed cells with retained internal discontinuities (A2). The first
has the earlier count interpolation and composite-wave precedent; the second
has a conservative coupled field/motion precedent. Generalization remains
necessary for multiple zero-passing records and K.A/source phases.

1. Start from the own anchor-compatible field and all old records; freeze the

2. Resolve ordinary waves and each zero-passing local fan. Integrate K.A

4. Complete field counts, preserve compatibility and perform E1 at the public

An internal event mesh can coexist with a regular public recovery/birth clock.

## 4. Step 3: continuous count/wave representation and transfer consistency

Within source-free sections, count differences encode mass, and the derivative
along a record is relative flux. The zero-passing condition retains its count.
Ordinary and restriction-induced waves give the same local examples without
whole-cell projection. Accepted port histories rebase local gauges once;
interior sources, if introduced, would require source-prefix corrections.
They are not part of the current approved transfer geometry.

A prescribed-path variational/count solution is useful after its admissible
paths and boundary histories are given. Supplying those histories externally
would remove the feedback that this target asks us to close. The reviewed
single-bottleneck continuum results have different FD, passing and motion
conditions. Thus neither replacing cells by counts nor citing existence for
another problem establishes the current joined solve.

### What the new node diagnostic does and does not prove

Two independent incoming roads with initial demands 0.2 and 0.4, the current
triangular lane capacity and receiving 0.3 yield flows 0.1 and 0.2 under a
direct demand-proportional merge. Both are restricted. Their admissible
congested boundary states instead have sending demand equal to capacity;
reapplying that proportional formula yields 0.15 and 0.15. All values and
backward-wave directions are checked exactly.

This demonstrates non-invariance of **that direct continuous proportional
merge**, not invalidity of native CTM or the full IT graph. Oriented IT
requests also contain lane-choice fractions and donor interactions; an
embedding in a reached task needs separate evidence. N.A is fixed priority,
not this proportional rule. Its corresponding restricted example returns the
same split when restricted incoming demands rise to their capacities. The
test therefore identifies a transfer-lift obligation without reopening N.A.

In particular, do not conclude that a continuous realization must invent a
new priority: a source-derived interior-state/continuum-limit construction may
preserve the discrete source's limiting behavior. Do not assume the simple
merge result generalizes automatically to oriented IT plus moving restrictions.
A fully specified numerical source-native construction is another legitimate
way forward. Both are currently unqualified for the complete task.

The richer field can still average ordinary wave detail between selected
locations. Conservation and count accuracy alone do not qualify one-sided
traces used for motion; neither is a universal ban on numerical approximation.
Any approximation needs the joint mechanism/evolution qualification previously
requested, not an arbitrary discrepancy allowance.

## 5. Living alternatives and dependencies

**Problem statement:** how can the retained restrictions and the transfer ports
advance one shared field while preserving the already selected source meaning?

**Model-chain function:** the field controls restriction motion and node
receiving; accepted transfers change that field and determine the next E1
attachments. This interface sits before clearance/TST and reduced comparisons.

**Core gap:** local spatial and motion/count constructions are positive, but
no complete admissible numerical or continuum update has been supplied for all
the approved ports and multiple records. Conservation does not select the
correct allocation or one-sided motion feedback.

### A1 — moving boundaries at all retained count locations

Advance own waves between fixed ports and moving record boundaries. Preserve
the selected port geometry and public clock; qualify their shared numerical
flux update rather than infer it from the reconstruction theorem.

**Pros:** directly preserves every existing anchor and vacuum gap; includes
all distinct records; retains the selected traffic physics and exact metadata.

**Cons:** the moving/node/source solve remains incomplete. The nearest
pairwise stepping precedent does not certify all multi-coincidences or K.A.

**Dependencies/consequences:** an actual stepping/contact/port construction
must satisfy Q.J.F. No unqualified count mismatch, record jitter or frozen
unobstructed supply is licensed. This remains prepared, not adopted.

### A2 — fixed cells with retained internal discontinuities

Retain a richer piecewise field inside affected cells. Derive conservation
fluxes and restriction motion from the same internal pieces; retain enough
pieces for the multiple-record state, not only one shock per affected cell.

**Pros:** directly addresses field/motion feedback while preserving a regular
outer grid and the macroscopic mechanism; has a conservative numerical
precedent. It is a genuine additional numerical candidate rather than error
tolerance B.

**Cons:** the audited source is not a drop-in multiple-zero-passing solution;
its reconstruction, kinetic and FD assumptions differ. The current ports
still need qualification. Local shock preservation is not global convergence.

**Dependencies/consequences:** qualifies within the user-authorized adaptation
route. Any changed passing/recovery law, deleted record or imported acceleration
must reopen the affected closed contract. It is not automatically better than
A1 or C for the whole task.

### C — a continuous count/wave realization in the affected roads

Resolve own restriction/traffic interaction in the continuous field and provide
an explicit source-native numerical or continuum port realization. Use it as
a common reference for A1/A2 where useful.

**Pros:** the field naturally retains the required spatial layout and local
count invariants; avoids a whole-cell projection at the restricted roads.

**Cons:** prescribed-path solutions do not close endogenous motion and flows;
directly transplanting finite-step proportional allocation is not qualified.
The current continuum existence results cannot simply be applied to this task.

**Dependencies/consequences:** distinguish a source-equivalent port realization
from changed allocation or source placement. The latter is a methodological
amendment, not part of automatically replacing the field. C remains unadopted.

**Provisional direction:** qualify the shared source/transfer boundary before
another wholesale representation choice. A2 deserves comparison with A1;
C is a useful common reference. No source proves a whole-task winner. B remains
declined as unqualified tolerance; D remains an unadopted effect-law proposal,
not an implicit fallback. This is a focused continuation proposal, not a
newly adopted source law or an automatically opened fourth ladder step.
