\section*{1. Completed transfer and newborn metadata are separate}
At regular update $t_{k+1}$, let $U^{k+1}$ be the completed own population/count
state, including all accepted transfers during $[t_k,t_{k+1})$.
Pre-existing restrictions are part of that interval's traffic problem.
For directed generating stream $e$, let $\Delta m_e^k\ge0$ be actual continuous
transfer mass, $H_e^k$ its cumulative mass and $m_*>0$ the fixed threshold unit.
\begin{equation}
 H_e^{k+1}=H_e^k+\Delta m_e^k,\qquad
 b_e^k=\left\lfloor H_e^{k+1}/m_*\right\rfloor
       -\left\lfloor H_e^k/m_*\right\rfloor .
\end{equation}
With remainder $z_e^k\in[0,1)$, the exact bookkeeping is
\begin{equation}
 b_e^k=\left\lfloor z_e^k+\Delta m_e^k/m_*\right\rfloor,\qquad
 z_e^{k+1}=z_e^k+\Delta m_e^k/m_*-b_e^k .
\end{equation}
The floor counts descriptors, never rounded physical traffic.
For each of the $b_e^k$ newborns, the selected reset reads
\begin{equation}
 t_b=t_{k+1},\quad \ell_b=\ell_{\rm target},\quad
 x_b=x_{\rm entry,e},\quad v_b^{\rm des}=v_{\rm donor,e}^{k,\rm request},
 \quad h_b=N_{\ell_b}^{k+1}(x_b).
\end{equation}
Here $N$ is the own count field with the declared gauge and accepted source
accounting. Ramp $x_{\rm entry,e}$ is the physical merge; discretionary entry
uses the declared target-section coordinate. These are E1 adaptations.
The first realized speed still obeys source CM:
\begin{equation}
 v_b=\min\{v_b^{\rm des},\,v_b^{\rm dwn,own}\}.
\end{equation}
This relation does not independently supply the joined downstream-speed estimator.

Let $\mathcal D^+$ be the retained old/new descriptor list after reset and
$\mathcal R$ the metadata reset. It satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R:(U^{k+1},H^{k+1},\mathcal D^-)\longmapsto
 (U^{k+1},H^{k+1},\mathcal D^+),\qquad
 M(U^{k+1})_{\rm after}=M(U^{k+1})_{\rm before}.
\end{equation}
$M$ includes outside waiting, the approach and all road lanes. Paired internal
transfers cancel; only offered arrivals and sink exits change total population.
Completed exit histories are unchanged. Equal own inputs/histories through
$t_{k+1}$ yield equal newborns through that time; no future reference is used.
This is conditional reset causality, not existence of the preceding solve.

\section*{2. Count attachment must be a value of the traffic field}
For same-lane newborns $i,j$ at the same update and location,
\begin{equation}
 x_i=x_j\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 h_i=h_j=N_{\ell}^{k+1}(x_i).
\end{equation}
Different speeds or physical parameters do not imply different $N$ values at
one point. For neighboring distinct positions and jam density $\kappa$,
admissibility requires
\begin{equation}
 0\le N_{\ell}^{k+1}(x_i)-N_{\ell}^{k+1}(x_j)
 \le \kappa(x_j-x_i),\qquad x_i<x_j .
\end{equation}
In particular, artificial label spacing at zero spatial separation violates
the upper bound. Descriptor multiplicity is not extra population.
Identical complete signatures may be grouped losslessly; differing signatures
are kept. Equal initial labels do not establish equal future paths.

Existing paths retain the preceding source correction. On a regular sourced
segment, with boundary $x_0$, net lateral source $s$ and $\dot\phi=v$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{d}{dt}N(t,\phi(t))
 =q(t,\phi)-v\rho(t,\phi)-\int_{x_0}^{\phi(t)}s(t,y)\,dy .
\end{equation}
Zero relative passing removes the first two terms, not the source integral.
This does not choose attachment for an atomic source on the path.

\section*{3. One local active trace explains the service mechanism}
Consider only a source-free segment with triangular fundamental diagram,
positive free/wave speeds $u,w$, jam density $\kappa$, and an active zero-passing
restriction moving at prescribed $0\le v<u$. An upstream saturated trace and a
downstream free trace satisfy
\begin{equation}
 Q(\rho)=\min\{u\rho,w(\kappa-\rho)\},\qquad
 Q(\rho_U)-v\rho_U=Q(\rho_D)-v\rho_D=0.
\end{equation}
Selecting those branches gives
\begin{equation}
 \rho_U=\frac{w\kappa}{w+v},\quad
 q_U=\frac{vw\kappa}{w+v},\quad
 \rho_D=q_D=0,\qquad
 \frac{dq_U}{dv}=\frac{w^2\kappa}{(w+v)^2}>0.
\end{equation}
The zero downstream trace is the void adjacent to the moving restriction.
This is \emph{not} a global fixed-section service cap or new node rule.
All node receiving and later exits must follow the same wave solution.
For diagnostic values $u=30$, $w=5$ m/s, $\kappa=0.15$ veh/m and $v=10$ m/s,
$q_U=0.5$ veh/s versus road capacity $uw\kappa/(u+w)=9/14$ veh/s.
These are hand-selected local inputs, not calibrated or reachable metering cases.

\section*{4. Simultaneous prescribed paths have a wider analytical route}
For a homogeneous source-free road with prescribed paths $\phi_j(t)$,
the relative road capacity at observer speed $\xi$ is
\begin{equation}
 R(\xi)=\sup_{0\le\rho\le\kappa}\{Q(\rho)-\xi\rho\},\qquad -w\le\xi\le u.
\end{equation}
Along a bottleneck, its declared maximum relative passing rate $r_j(t)$ reduces
that cost. Applicable coincident restrictions combine by a minimum, not by an
additive population or capacity debit. For compatible data on boundary $\Gamma$,
the variational reference reads
\begin{equation}
 N(t,x)=\inf_{\gamma:B\in\Gamma\to(t,x)}
 \left\{N(B)+\int_{t_B}^{t}R_{\mathcal D}
       (\dot\gamma(\tau),\tau,\gamma(\tau))\,d\tau\right\}.
\end{equation}
$R_{\mathcal D}$ is the road cost reduced on the applicable prescribed curves.
All distinct curves remain. V03, Theorems 1--3, supplies scoped validity and
boundary consistency conditions; V02 discusses graph representations and their
errors. A finite graph is not declared exact or adopted here.

The source pairwise-CRP step bound includes
\begin{equation}
 \Delta t\le\frac{x_{i+2}-x_i}{w+v_i}.
\end{equation}
A triple coincidence makes this bound zero when $w+v_i>0$.
That demonstrates the limit of the pairwise construction, not nonexistence
of the multi-path variational solution.
