\section*{1. Retained state and conservative population}
At a declared update phase, keep the own traffic field $U$, speed-support
history $\mathcal H$, waiting inventories $W$, accepted transfer counters $H_e$
and all restriction records:
\begin{equation}
X=(U,\mathcal H,W,\{H_e\},\{z_j\}),\qquad
z_j=(\ell_j,x_j,v_j,v_{{\rm des},j},\theta_j,h_j,e_j,t_j^{\rm b}).
\end{equation}
Here $\ell_j$ is lane; $x_j$ position (m); $v_j,v_{{\rm des},j}$ realized/desired
speed (m/s), retaining the phase-required value; $\theta_j$ contains fixed
acceleration/type/grade data; $h_j$ is the lane-count association (vehicle
equivalents); $e_j$ is birth-stream metadata; $t_j^{\rm b}$ birth time (s).
$U$ contains the spatial mass/count information needed by the declared KW
method. $\mathcal H$ contains own preceding-period information needed by source
lane-choice speeds, initialized publicly. None is a reference-future oracle.

For cell/volume $c$, mass $n_c$ (vehicle equivalents) and accepted transfer
mass $F_{cb}$, the paired update is
\begin{equation}
n_c^+=n_c+\sum_bF_{bc}-\sum_bF_{cb},\qquad
M^+-M=I-E.
\end{equation}
$M$ includes all waiting, ramp and road mass; $I,E$ are external arrival/exit
increments. All internal transfers cancel. Descriptors add no mass. A source
lateral transfer already advances to the downstream target section.

For upstream ramp permission $r^k$ (veh/s), offered mass $A^k$, waiting mass
$W_r^k$, approach receiving $R_a^k$ (veh/s) and step $\Delta t$ (s),
\begin{equation}
B^k=\min\{W_r^k+A^k,r^k\Delta t,R_a^k\Delta t\},\quad
W_r^{k+1}=W_r^k+A^k-B^k.
\end{equation}
This admitted mass enters the approach, not directly the freeway merge.
At the approved point node let $D_r,D_m,R$ be ramp/non-ramp demands and shared
receiving (veh/s), and $p\in(0,1)$ fixed ramp priority:
\begin{equation}
(f_r,f_m)=
\begin{cases}
(D_r,D_m),&D_r+D_m\le R,\\
(\min\{D_r,\max\{pR,R-D_m\}\},R-f_r),&D_r+D_m>R.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
The first component is evaluated before the second. Existing coupled
restrictions must determine $R$ consistently; this formula does not supply it.
These are previously approved interfaces, not a new reference law.

\section*{2. Exact source correction to a moving traffic label}
\textbf{Reviewer derivation.} On a regular lane interval with fixed upstream
boundary $x_0$, let $\rho$ be density (veh/m), $q$ longitudinal flow (veh/s)
and $s$ net lateral source (veh/(m s)). Conservation is
\begin{equation}
\partial_t\rho+\partial_xq=s.
\end{equation}
Let $C(t)$ be accepted cumulative inflow at $x_0$, with $\dot C=q(t,x_0)$.
Choose one fixed label gauge and define
\begin{equation}
N(t,x)=C(t)-\int_{x_0}^{x}\rho(t,y)\,dy.
\end{equation}
Differentiation and conservation give
\begin{align}
N_x&=-\rho,\\
N_t&=q(t,x)-\int_{x_0}^{x}s(t,y)\,dy.
\end{align}
For a path $x=\phi(t)$ with speed $v=\dot\phi$, set
$h(t)=N(t,\phi(t))$. Thus
\begin{equation}
\dot h=q(t,\phi)-v\rho(t,\phi)-\int_{x_0}^{\phi}s(t,y)\,dy.
\end{equation}
Where the embedded zero-passing condition gives zero relative flux
$q-v\rho=0$, this becomes
\begin{equation}
\dot h=-\int_{x_0}^{\phi}s(t,y)\,dy.
\end{equation}
If $s=0$, $h$ is constant. With lateral entries/exits upstream of the path,
its label changes despite zero passing. This is a coordinate/count correction,
not an extra service penalty or population term.

\textbf{Discrete fixed-section version.} For population $P_x$ between $x_0$
and fixed $x$, accepted boundary mass $\Delta C$, crossing mass $F_x$ and net
lateral addition $L_x$ in that section,
\begin{equation}
\Delta P_x=\Delta C-F_x+L_x,\qquad
\Delta N_x=\Delta C-\Delta P_x=F_x-L_x.
\end{equation}
An upstream net lateral entry of one unit shifts $N$ by $-1$ when $F_x=0$.
An exit shifts it by $+1$. Boundary admissions also increment $C$ and so cancel
their inventory contribution in this coordinate. Weak/discrete balances extend
the bookkeeping beyond smooth fields; they do not choose which side owns a
point source exactly on a path. That attachment remains a joined-law obligation.

At a point merge chosen as $x_0$, the boundary ledger includes both accepted
incoming streams. It is not a lateral source inside $(x_0,x)$.

\section*{3. Motion, birth clocks and count attachment}
\textbf{Source motion.} At the source sample phase, motion obeys
\begin{align}
v_j(t)&=\min\{v_{{\rm dwn},j}(t),v_{{\rm des},j}(t)\},\\
v_{{\rm des},j}(t+\Delta t)&=v_j(t)+
\left[a_{0,j}\left(1-\frac{v_j(t)}{v_{\max,j}}\right)-gG(x_j(t))\right]\Delta t,
\\
x_j(t+\Delta t)&=x_j(t)+v_j(t)\Delta t.
\end{align}
$v_{\rm dwn}$ is own coupled immediately downstream KW speed; $a_0$ is
zero-speed acceleration (m/s$^2$), $v_{\max}$ maximum speed, $g$ gravity and
$G$ dimensionless grade. Lane-choice look-ahead speed is a different quantity.
Exact retention does not justify assigning downstream speed independently.

For generating stream $e$, define public progress $\eta_e\in[0,1)$ and actual
accepted rate $f_e$:
\begin{equation}
H_e(t)=\eta_e+\int_0^t f_e(s)\,ds.
\end{equation}
The sampled source birth count at update $t_k$ is
\begin{equation}
\Delta K_e^k=\lfloor H_e(t_k)\rfloor-\lfloor H_e(t_{k-1})\rfloor.
\end{equation}
No conserved quantity is rounded. The proposed E1 reset adds these records
after the completed transfer update, with declared placement/speed/count data.
It does not retroactively alter completed transfers.

E2 instead proposes each continuous crossing time
\begin{equation}
\tau_{e,m}=\inf\{t:H_e(t)\ge m\},\qquad m=1,2,\ldots.
\end{equation}
It requires coupled continuation after $\tau$, because the new restriction
may alter the remaining $f_e$. It explicitly reopens the selected sampled clock
and dependent onset/inactive classification if approved; neither is reopened
now. It is an extension, not a source theorem.

\textbf{Clock input example only.} Give $H(t)=3/4+t/2$ on $[0,1]$ (veh) and
a sampled update at $t=1$ s. Then
\begin{equation}
\tau_1=\tfrac12\ {\rm s},\qquad \Delta K=1,
\end{equation}
whereas the sampled record starts at $1$ s. No joined traffic solution, feasible
common-control pair or TST difference is asserted from this prescribed counter.

The birth count association must match the own traffic field at its location
and phase. At a point ramp boundary it uses total accepted mainline plus ramp
count in the same gauge. At an internal point source, one-sided attachment
must be explicit. Neither clock choice alone certifies simultaneous events.

\section*{4. Conditional equivalence and full-system-time obligations}
Suppose the complete joined update $\mathcal T_r$ is well defined and invariant
under descriptor renaming. Let $\Pi$ be a lossless representation with the
independent own update $\widetilde{\mathcal T}_r$, and assume
\begin{equation}
\Pi\mathcal T_r(X)=\widetilde{\mathcal T}_r(\Pi X).
\end{equation}
Starting from equivalent public states, induction gives equivalent states and
exits at every common update. Keeping all distinct complete records can meet
the information part of this identity. The identity does not prove existence
of $\mathcal T_r$ or fill an unspecified birth/reset. It also does not prove
that personal identities or every retained record are necessary.

Let $M_0$ be initial total mass, $A(t)$ cumulative offered arrivals and $E(t)$
cumulative sink exits. Include all outside waiting and finite ramp/road mass:
\begin{equation}
M(t)=M_0+A(t)-E(t),\qquad J=\int_0^{T_{\rm clear}}M(t)\,dt.
\end{equation}
$J$ has units veh s. For two schedules $a,b$ with common population and arrival
history, extend cleared exits constantly to common $T$:
\begin{equation}
J^a-J^b=-\int_0^T[E^a(t)-E^b(t)]\,dt.
\end{equation}
Equivalent complete exit histories imply equal TST, including relocated queues.
Finite-horizon equality is insufficient if remaining tails differ. An integrable
certified inventory tail may replace finite exact clearance. Positive final
permission and finite population alone do not prove that tail for this joined law.
