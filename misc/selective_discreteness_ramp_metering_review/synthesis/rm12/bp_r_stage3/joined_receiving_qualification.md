## Receiving can be qualified locally without an extra service law

On a source-free triangular-FD receiving road, take one prescribed forward
path starting at the boundary, zero relative passing and compatible empty
initial downstream traffic. Before the path leaves the domain or downstream
boundary effects arrive, its congested following states propagate backwards
at the FD wave speed. Their arrival map is strictly increasing, including
piecewise-linear sampled paths with speeds between zero and free speed.

At saturation this gives a determinate cumulative boundary receiving envelope
and a flow evaluated at the path's earlier wave-emission time. The constant-
speed formula reproduces the preceding E1 moving trace; the varying-path
formula additionally locates its delayed effect at the fixed merge boundary.
For non-saturated demand, actual cumulative admission is below that envelope
and respects the ordinary road's rate bound. An unused interval creates slack;
admission is not forced to equal the envelope derivative while slack exists.

These effects do not establish retained-record necessity. A qualified spatial
aggregate or memory model may preserve them too; the direct finding concerns
the insufficiency of an instantaneous scalar trace-flow shortcut.

The two explicit diagnostics distinguish (i) present speed from retarded
receiving and (ii) saturated service from actual admission after idle time.
They are analytical operator checks. They do not demonstrate the same public
scenario under two admissible meter controls or source-generated trajectories.
The source-free/prescribed assumptions are conditional reference inputs, not
changes to the admitted multilane metering task or permission to omit births.

N.A can spend the actual receiving once. In the scarcity case, the existing
fixed-priority award lies between the ramp's residual need and its bounded
claim; non-ramp inflows share their award. Integrated internal transfers cancel
in total population. Applying E1 afterwards leaves this conservation identity
unchanged. This qualifies node accounting conditional on receiving; it does
not solve receiving in the full endogenous, sourced case.

## The remaining circular dependence is not an information-cost problem

The source CM needs the traffic immediately downstream over the next interval.
That field depends on the restriction's path, on other paths and on accepted
node/lateral flows. Those accepted flows depend on receiving from that field.
Source IT's current-density recipe does not independently specify this joined
particle/node solve. An exact retained list supplies its arguments, not its
update law. Beginning-state sampling without a qualified domain/encounter rule
is not automatically the interval estimate the journal cites.

The accessible prescribed-path results establish useful conditional wave
operators but remove this feedback. The source-native pairwise algorithm's
coincidence limitation persists as an algorithm obligation. The later M02
algorithm cannot be imported unchanged: its motion/passing model differs and
its encounter completion uses approximation. None of these results makes
exact retention impossible, unnecessarily costly or scientifically unsound.

To freeze one complete reference we must bind: the own downstream-speed
estimate and phase; compatible path/field/node update including multiple
coincidences; accepted-source/count attachment on existing paths; and the
single use of the receiving budget. Only afterwards can the full E1 loop be
declared determinate. The cited full J01 journal is the nearest unverified
method input; reading it may resolve some, not necessarily all, of these.

## Clearance and control accounting audit

All-stream inventory equals offered cumulative population plus initial mass
minus cumulative exits, conditional on conservative transfers. Finite full
TST requires eventual emptying or a justified integrable residual tail.
Identical complete exit histories give equal full TST, even when holding
locations differ. Metering must change exits to establish objective leverage.

Free acceleration on a level road supplies a local release tendency; it does
not prove clearance of interacting restrictions, approach spillback and node
flows. Positive final permission likewise does not certify that joined model's
exit history. The prescribed-path receiving diagnostics do not close this
obligation or supply a nontrivial full-TST rate witness. Step 3 has been audited
conditionally; general proof is downstream of the unresolved Step 2 update.
