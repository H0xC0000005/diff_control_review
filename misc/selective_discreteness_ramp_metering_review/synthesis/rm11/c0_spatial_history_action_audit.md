## 1. What C was trying to add

C asks whether an actual finite acceleration lane creates useful information
about **where traffic can still merge and which receiving space it can reach**.
It differs from A's acceleration/recovery question. The lane has an end; released
traffic travels through it; merging uses adjacent mainline receiving; failed or
delayed access can affect later traffic. A rate change alters release and traffic
encounters, but also changes upstream waiting.

The sources support finite physical geometry, including a genuine two-mainline-
lane site. They do not collectively bind a complete task with our strong observer,
genuine initial knowledge, full-history residual and useful common rate actions.
The task envelope remains single-ramp, continuous traffic mass, finite storage/
receiving, public shared geometry/parameters/forcing, common feasible bounded
open-loop rate schedules, and all-stream TST through clearance. Geometry, exact
merge dynamics and actual speed support are not silently fixed by that envelope.

An admissible synthesis need not appear verbatim in a paper. It can combine
source mechanisms with a sound conservation, information and control argument.
Here the necessary transfer would still require selecting an unresolved
initial-information and merge-law contract; no joined certificate was derived.

## 2. Give the aggregate rival a fair spatial model

| Representation | What it can legitimately capture | What its success/failure would mean |
|---|---|---|
| One point merge / one whole-lane total | Overall demand, receiving and storage | Insufficient as the sole rival when longitudinal location is the proposed mechanism |
| Aggregate CTM with appropriate longitudinal cells and public geometry | Different upstream/downstream inventories, waves, storage, approaching the lane end; a source-grounded aggregate merge closure if justified | Removes distinctions that were only caused by whole-zone averaging. Does not prove every microscopic merge effect closes |
| Lane-resolved spatial macro model | Lane occupancy/accessibility and lateral receiving under its public law | Relevant continuous-state rival. It already retains lane information; success does not establish that lane information can be hidden without consequence |
| Richer local position/gap/interaction representation | Candidate detail beyond declared aggregate state | Physical detail alone does not prove it is unresolved by history, necessary for control, or that integer mass/vehicle identities are required |

The sensor support is common. A finer *internal* grid can improve a public model,
but cannot receive new cell measurements or exact within-cell initial coordinates
that the competing controller never had. Conversely, withholding already admitted
per-cell observations from the macro controller would be unfair. Numerical
resolution and observation resolution are separate.

The accessible ACTM and lane-CTM sources support serious competitors, not an exact
closure theorem for this finite-lane task. Their merge priorities, initial states,
and source domains remain qualified. We did not import a one-cell geometry,
guaranteed entry or a convenient new probability law to produce the conclusion.

## 3. Which proposed distinctions survive the information check?

| Possible distinction | Completed audit | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Public distance from a given cell to the lane end | Known geometry can enter either model | Not hidden traffic information |
| More traffic in the early part versus near the end, with different observed cell totals | Adequate spatial inventories can distinguish this directly | Resolution issue; not an equal-observation witness |
| Equal road totals but different lane occupancy/access | Relevant candidate if not otherwise inferred; lane-macro rival must retain that state honestly | Snapshot possibility; full history/action evidence still required |
| Equal per-cell totals/means but different subcell positions, gaps or relative alignment | Aggregate snapshots need not encode a distribution or correlation | Not rejected universally, but not a genuine full-history certificate by itself |
| Fine state omitted from storage after a complete physical initialization | Exact public deterministic evolution determines it in principle | Conditional information negative; representation loss is a different claim |
| Aggregate-only initial knowledge at a real operational start | Plausible operationally; sources do not establish that it is complete physical knowledge | Must bind an actual knowledge epoch and all available histories; cannot delete earlier observations to make a pair |
| Source loops and intermittent research trajectories | Their support differs from ideal exact cell mass/flow/section speed | Cannot silently weaken the inherited strong information contract |

The decisive difference is between **not stored in today's snapshot** and **not
determined by the controller's entire available information**. Finite geometry
does not bridge that difference automatically.

Known complete physical initialization with exact deterministic laws and public
inputs remains a conditional sufficiency result. Knowing only initial aggregate
statistics does not invoke that result. History can reveal initially missing
coordinates, or may leave some unresolved; neither outcome follows merely from
using a CTM or observing a mean speed. No universal reconstruction claim was
made for heterogeneous/subcell merging traffic.

For a positive result, a source-grounded set of possible initial traffic and a
declared public law would have to yield multiple states consistent with **every**
admitted mass, completed flow, supported speed, ramp and control observation
through the decision time. The published trajectory studies are not that
consistency argument. Different observed trajectories do not imply equal
aggregate histories; intermittent recordings cannot certify an unobserved interval.
Recorded fine trajectories also cannot establish oracle controller initialization.

## 4. Useful rate actions and complete TST

Even a genuine unresolved spatial coordinate must affect the rate decision.
The comparison must use the same feasible schedule set, arrival population,
physical parameters and objective. A difference in one forward prediction is
weaker than evidence that the information changes a useful perturbation, action
ranking or achievable improvement.

Metering postpones ramp entry. Postponed traffic remains in the system for TST,
including external waiting if storage blocks admission. Reducing visible in-road
congestion by keeping traffic outside cannot count as a benefit by itself. With
common arrivals and complete accounting, identical cumulative system exits under
each common schedule imply identical TST, regardless of where the queues lie.
A spatial mechanism could alter exits and reverse useful rate directions, but
this must be demonstrated or soundly derived from the admitted law.

The nearest inspected control comparison cannot close this obligation because
its local-information benefit accompanies a different sensing/timing task. We
did not execute that alternative. The spatial macro references show how public
traffic laws can make rates matter, but not that an unresolved finite-lane state
changes useful actions under our matched histories. Position-dependent merge
behavior alone also does not imply that the lowest/highest rate is universally
best, or that distinct best rates must exist.

The finite lane does not authorize loss of traffic that reaches its end, an
arbitrary extra service penalty, universal failed merging, an assumed exponential
urgency profile, or private random events. Such additions would be methodological
choices. A fitted acceptance probability would require a justified conservative
traffic closure and an information contract before it could serve this task.

## 5. Gradient status without deep model adoption

The general chain is: rate permission → queue-limited release → travel through
the acceleration lane → lateral merging/receiving → longitudinal propagation
and exits → complete population time. Ramp waiting contributes throughout.
This is the existing general control-sensitivity concept, with the finite-lane
travel/merge mechanism still unbound.

No specific gradient is admitted by drawing that chain. A source-grounded law
would have to define active receiving limits, queue caps, merge switches and
lane-end behavior. Smooth intervals can support ordinary chain differentiation;
event boundaries require appropriate one-sided/directional or event-sensitive
treatment. Conservation/feasibility cannot be changed by an unapproved smoothing
choice. The prior [general mathematical companion](../rm10/rm10_information_tst_and_gradient_audit.pdf)
remains conditional background, not a validated C gradient.

C0 stops before this detailed formulation because the history/action admission
obligations are unestablished. No numerical gradient test or SUMO run is needed
to identify that gap. A simulation by itself would not establish genuine initial
knowledge or fair information access.

## 6. Completed admission disposition

**Scoped negative C0 admission:** physical finite-lane merging is supported, but
the inspected frontier plus this static audit does not establish an unchanged
task with unresolved strong aggregate history and useful common rate actions.
Do not proceed to deep C on that physical support alone.

This is **not** a theorem that every finite-lane task is aggregate-closed, that
all position effects vanish, or that aggregate-only initialization is impossible.
Some proposed distinctions are conditionally visible/reconstructible; the richer
subcell/lane-interaction possibility lacks sufficient task-level support here.
The latter is an evidence/adaptation gap, not universal structural refutation.

The bounded pass has completed genuine-task extraction, strongest-spatial-rival
checking, residual history/action checking and admission reporting. No lawful
task has reached a G4 law-selection gate. Choosing a favorable law or reduced
observer now would create a new methodological direction, not finish missing
routine work. T/R remain unselected. Review and acceptance of this result are
the next allowed action; no successor is opened automatically.
