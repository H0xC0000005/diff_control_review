## What we are constructing

A controller must be able to predict the consequences of an upstream permission
schedule using the model's own state: waiting and ramp propagation, actual merges,
the creation and motion of restrictions, their effect on conservative traffic
service, exits and complete system time. All exact restriction information may
be retained. Copying the fine reference's future is still prohibited.

Exact information means no loss in the retained restriction state. It does not
claim that a finite numerical mesh is an exact continuum PDE solution. Preserve
the source's declared numerical lineage and distinguish discretization choices
from an added information-reduction or physical assumption.

## Authorized sequence and acceptance

1. Transcribe complete own state, per-stream transfer/event ownership, motion
   and count association. Keep continuous traffic mass separate from descriptors.
2. Recheck the nearest original coupled moving-boundary and merge methods.
   Attempt to join the approved upstream meter and fixed-priority node without
   reconstructing unknown within-group states or adding a mean service penalty.
3. Audit whether the joint update is determined by admitted data, including
   insertion location/time, count anchoring, downstream-speed feedback,
   simultaneous events and the node boundary. Separate existing source recipes
   from any missing physical convention.
4. Trace the complete rate-to-exit/TST path and clearance obligation. A known
   forward map is distinct from a nontrivial control witness or useful derivative.
   Produce source/static evidence and a readable report. Stop for an actual
   new scientific choice; do not stop because the retained list is large.

An acceptable first result is an exact-information model with a closed causal
forward chain at the declared source level, or a precisely identified source/
interface gap and concrete options. No traffic experiment or code is required.
Cost, compression ratio and a predetermined positive result are not criteria.
