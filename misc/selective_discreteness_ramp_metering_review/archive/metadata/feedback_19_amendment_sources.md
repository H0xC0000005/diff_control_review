## RM0013 — The bottleneck mechanism of a freeway diverge

- Publication: Transportation Research Part A 36(6), 483–505, July 2002; DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/S0965-8564(01)00017-9. The DOI's 2001 segment is not the publication year.
- Primary source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0965856401000179.
- Access: publisher-indexed abstract/introduction inspected; direct page open returned 403. Bibliographic attribution also appears in the primary-paper bibliography at https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405896316318183. Full text not obtained.
- Relevant evidence: observations of an oversaturated exit on I-880; blockage extending across lanes and discharge depending on destination composition.
- Permitted claim: empirical motivation for destination-sensitive diverge restrictions. Not a demonstration of exact-order necessity, metering-gradient usefulness, or adequacy of the proposed local-zone model. No quantitative result is adopted as a project target.

## RM0014 — Increasing the capacity of an isolated merge by metering its on-ramp

- Authors: Michael J. Cassidy and Jittichai Rudjanakanoknad.
- Journal publication: Transportation Research Part B 39(10), 896–913, December 2005; DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2004.12.001.
- Primary publisher source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0191261505000044.
- Related author report: _Increasing Capacity of an Isolated Merge by Metering its On-Ramp_ (2004), https://escholarship.org/uc/item/30s46939. Do not assume versions are identical. Author-report metadata/abstract was returned by search.
- Access: publisher-indexed abstract/introduction inspected; direct publisher open failed. No full-method inspection this pass.
- Relevant evidence: field observations and metering experiments at one merge link capacity recovery to shoulder-lane queuing and lateral spreading. The reported higher-discharge episodes were temporary, not a demonstrated sustained optimum.
- Permitted claim: single-merge metering has empirical control value. Not proof that current LTM plus ordered metadata captures the causal mechanism. No adoption of controller settings, calibration or lane-changing law.
