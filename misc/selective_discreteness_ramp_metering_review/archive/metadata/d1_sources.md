## Metadata/full-text limitations

- RM0023: Gordon F. Newell, *Delays caused by a queue at a freeway exit ramp*, DOI `10.1016/S0191-2615(98)00039-3`. Publisher abstract/introduction and TRID metadata were inspected. The bounded conclusion used here is only that the model separates exiting and through queues on different lane sets along a freeway section; no uninspected equation is adopted.
- The public CiteSeerX mirror for the Muñoz–Daganzo working paper timed out during a 30-second bounded download. The existing RM0013 record remains supported by the publisher and UC Berkeley abstract/introduction; no new source ID was created for the failed mirror.
- A public author-hosted URL for Chen et al. (2021) returned a JavaScript client-challenge HTML page rather than a PDF. The newly created placeholder was deleted and is not listed in the manifest. No claim relies on that failed retrieval.

Initial un-escalated network access failed on sandbox DNS. Public downloads were retried with the user's approved minimum network escalation. No credentials, institutional access, or new dependencies were used.
