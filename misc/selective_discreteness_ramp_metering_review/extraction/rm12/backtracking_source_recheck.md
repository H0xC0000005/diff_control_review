## Logical assessment of recovery and the shared obstacle

There are three separate tests, not one property called a systematic failure:

1. **Information:** if the complete physical state and exact deterministic
   public propagation/forcing are known, its future action responses are fixed.
   Extra deterministic recovery coordinates do not negate that statement.
   Aggregate-only initial knowledge is a different premise. Conversely, finite
   point measurements do not by themselves prove surviving whole-history ambiguity.
2. **Representation:** a source family may already have a sufficient compact
   dynamic state. RM0003 is an example, not a theorem covering every physical
   merge. Density plus memory must compete before asserting lane/cohort necessity.
   Recovering a coordinate in principle and representing its dynamics accurately
   in a chosen reduction are distinct obligations.
3. **Action/accounting:** with common initial population/arrivals and all waiting
   counted, identical cumulative exit histories imply equal complete TST.
   Variable recovery can change exit histories and therefore does not inherit
   the fixed-service queue-relocation obstruction automatically. Different exit
   histories still need a favorable integrated comparison; mean throughput or
   terminal exits alone do not establish it.

These are inherited conditional analytic results and planning deductions, not
new numerical results. Recovery is neither refuted as physics nor certified as
an escape from the joined information/representation/action obligation. The
earlier L proposal addresses principally test 3; it should not have been preferred
as protection against tests 1–2 without comparing backtracking positions first.
