## Interpretation and urban-model boundary

The platoon-leader analogy explains the obstruction's effect, not a fixed cohort
assignment: the followers remain density/flow fields. The source adds an event
law and physical trajectory constraints to that field, rather than extracting
hidden identities from it. A zero relative passing rate can coexist with positive
flow past stationary detectors because the constraint itself moves.

The repository's urban `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` Sections 3 and 5 retains route/order
semantics at local nodes with aggregate longitudinal propagation. Physical
vehicle trajectories, acceleration and explicit lane-changing are deferred.
The proposed freeway reference therefore extends a different physical contract;
it is not a contradiction of conservation, but is not automatic implementation
compatibility either. Method-first review does not use urban implementation
progress as a selection constraint. No root methodology or production file was
changed. The richer reference/reduced-study distinction must remain explicit.

## Source-specific meaning of “hybrid”

The user's distinction between mixed granularity and lane/phase-aware aggregate
modeling is pertinent. A fresh read of RM0011 Section 1 explicitly identifies
the hybrid approach as combining microscopic and macroscopic models: KW for
the stream, individual constrained motion for the slow vehicles. Sections 2–3
also distinguish the aggregate multilane model from its particle augmentation.
The selected vehicles' trajectory/acceleration physics is the limited microscopic
component; continuous-valued positions and speeds do not make it aggregate.
This is not a full microscopic simulator or a geographically separated micro bay.

RM8-P01's
[original merge manuscript](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.gatech.edu/dist/f/3571/files/2023/03/merges.pdf),
abstract and Sections 1–2, explicitly names the multilane hybrid and its discrete
accelerating particles. Laval/Daganzo's related
[2006 journal abstract/metadata](https://its.berkeley.edu/node/4685) independently
confirms the individual-particle mechanism; full journal methods were not newly
accessed. These support the term in this specific lineage, not a universal
definition covering all freeway models.

RM10-P04's author original, abstract and introduction, calls its LWR plus finite
accelerating-leader ODEs macroscopic. Its initial-jump/constant-acceleration laws
are distinct. This is evidence that classifications overlap; the named state and
coupling obligations must carry the review meaning rather than a generic label.
Continuous/discrete mode switching is another mathematical meaning of hybrid;
it is not the reason given by the source lineage audited here. Lane awareness,
phase preservation and CTM discretization alone do not establish mixed traffic
granularity.

Explanatory default: **Laval–Daganzo continuum–particle reference**; **multilane
hybrid (MH)** remains the source name. Entirely aggregate proposed reductions
may be called macroscopic. This terminology correction adopts no reference,
reduction or task law and changes no gate status. This pass reopens existing
primary sources and performs no additional search query or source screening.
