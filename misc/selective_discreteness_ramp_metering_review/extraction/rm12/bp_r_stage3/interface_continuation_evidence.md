## What changed in the evidence

**An explicit dynamic compact rival exists.** Re-read the archived primary
RM0010, Jin, Gan and Lebacque, *A kinematic wave theory of capacity drop*,
arXiv:1310.2660v1. Section 4 supplies a conservative density evolution; Section
6, equation (15), extends the capacity-drop boundary to a priority merge.
The boundary reduces receiving when combined demand exceeds ordinary supply.
The drop magnitude is fixed site calibration, not an inferred acceleration law.
This corrects any broad reading that compact literature supplies only stationary
capacity formulas. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.2660).

**New reviewer finding, not a source claim:** the native boundary does not pass
the chosen moving-constraint reference's entire inactive regime. With no ramp
entry or active constraint, mainline demand at capacity and ordinary receiving
at 90% of capacity, a 20% drop parameter lowers service to 80%. The matched
unobstructed node serves 90%. These are dimensionless hand-assigned values for
an algebraic test, not traffic data, a calibrated scenario or simulation.
This is an admissible local receiving-state check; it does not establish its
reachability under the eventual task controls or a mismatch in every narrower
free-downstream source regime. No task exclusion is adopted to avoid the check.
The [mathematical companion](../../../synthesis/rm12/bp_r_stage3/interface_continuation_math.pdf)
checks this directly. Setting the drop parameter to zero gives the ordinary
node, but does not prove equality at nonzero fixed parameters when disturbances
merely cease to bind. This does not refute the published model's physical domain.

**Mean closures can be embedded dynamically, with an approximation.** Re-read
RM10-P01's 2011 Procedia methods and RM10-P02's 2016 multilane Modeling Framework.
Their local mean calculation can supply a CTM's receiving calculation. Calling
it at successive times is a quasi-steady approximation, rather than a source-
validated transient recovery law. RM10-P02 partitions discretionary and mandatory
regions; its implicit coupled calculation cannot be ported by silently changing
the selected geometry. [2016 author manuscript](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_d4844cb9-1115-47ab-ab36-443cc0ca7df4).
The nonlinear reference acceleration and donor-speed birth law must also be
preserved or their approximation explicitly adopted. This route is not refuted
by the absence of a ready matched implementation.

## Reference transcription and qualification

original RM12-S3-S02, Sections 2–4; and point-node original RM12-S3-S01, Section

a complete uniqueness/event-integration theorem is still not established.
