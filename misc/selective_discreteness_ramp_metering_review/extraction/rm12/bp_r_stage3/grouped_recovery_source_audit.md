## Source evidence and transfer limits

**RM12-S3-A01 — supplied final Laval/Daganzo 2006 journal.** Re-read PDF pages
6, 11 and 12, Section 2.2 and Appendix A. The descriptor is a zero-relative-
passing moving restriction. Current realized speed is limited by desired speed
and downstream traffic; current speed advances position and supplies the next
desired speed. Acceleration parameters, grade and sampling matter. Continuous
mass is conserved separately. These facts support a joint position/kinetic
state, not a histogram reconstruction or an additive mean penalty. Birth
placement and full event/node coupling remain previously identified obligations.
[Unmodified supplied original](../../../archive/user_supplied/rm12_bp_r_stage3/Lane-changing%20in%20traffic%20streams.pdf).

**RM12-S3-S02 — Daganzo/Laval 2003, RR-2003-2.** Re-read Sections 2–4 and
Appendix, PDF pages 4–7. Moving boundaries carry positions, speeds, passing
limits and cumulative counts; between-boundary density and interaction timing
enter the update. Composite Riemann solutions use minimum count contributions.
The method averages the traffic field, while still retaining moving boundaries.
Its coincidence perturbation suggestion is not adopted here. It supports
service coupling and numerical qualification, not replacing several distinct
boundaries with their average.
[Institutional original](https://escholarship.org/content/qt1hp588xx/qt1hp588xx.pdf).
The browser title is wrong; the actual cover/body identify this report.

**RM12-S3-G01 — Gong/Piccoli/Visconti, arXiv:2007.14655v2, 2021 revision
of the 2020 author preprint.**
Read Sections 2.3–2.5, 3.1 and 4, especially equations 2.13–2.15 and 4.1–4.3.
Joint position/velocity measures can evolve independently, with lane-change
sources. The construction uses Bando/FtL-type interaction kernels, a mean-field
population description and Lipschitz/source assumptions. Those assumptions do
not establish our sampled descriptor births or zero-passing KW restrictions.
This is positive precedent for distribution state, not a directly transferable
recovery/service equation or evidence of finite-bin compression.
[Author preprint, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.14655v2).

**RM12-S3-G02 — Helbing/Greiner, arXiv:cond-mat/9806126v1, 1998 author upload
of the 1997 PRE paper, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.55.5498.** Read Sections II–IV,
especially equations 2–3, 27–29, 37–39 and 42–48. It derives multilane kinetic
and moment equations. Compact closure uses distributional/equilibrium
approximations; further reduction averages over a slow time scale. These are
declared modeling choices. Its distribution represents traffic population and
its interaction law differs from our restriction metadata. It is a serious
aggregate alternative, not permission to import those choices into this task.
[Author preprint, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9806126v1).
Its PDF/journal credit both authors; arXiv's landing page lists only Helbing.

**RM12-S3-G03 — Simoni/Claudel, arXiv:1701.01026v1, 2017 author preprint.**
The manuscript's 2016 copyright header is not used as its publication date.
Read Sections 2.3–2.5 and 3.1–3.3. Its method
couples multiple moving restrictions through cumulative-count internal
conditions and their domains of influence. It keeps trajectory-specific data
and handles active/inactive behavior under a passing-capacity model. That
passing law and constant maximum-speed motion are not our nonlinear accelerating
zero-passing source. Minimum-combination structure is useful; native control
examples do not certify our ramp task or gradients.
[Author preprint, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.01026v1).

**RM12-S3-G04 — Liard/Piccoli, arXiv:1801.04814v1, 2018 author preprint.**
Read Section 1.1–1.2, equations 1–2 and Theorem 1. A single moving flux
constraint has a coupled traffic/trajectory solution with continuous dependence
on initial data. The normalized quadratic flux, capped velocity rule and
single-constraint setting differ from our repeated accelerating descriptors.
Its stability result does not supply a grouping-error bound for this task.
[Author preprint, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.04814v1).

## What is a reviewer deduction

The [new mathematical companion](../../../synthesis/rm12/bp_r_stage3/grouped_recovery_math.pdf)
derives a conditional exact duplicate-group rule, a joint-association update
example, a histogram/mean-position transport obstruction and conditional
count/TST error relationships. These are not quoted published results about
the complete ramp task. They do not establish reachability under common
controls, compact-state necessity, a complete coupled solver or useful gradients.

The duplicate rule requires identical full motion and service signatures,
including cumulative-count association where the coupling uses it. Equal
averages, proximity or present nonbinding status do not provide that certificate.
No supplied birth placement is silently changed to create duplicate groups.
