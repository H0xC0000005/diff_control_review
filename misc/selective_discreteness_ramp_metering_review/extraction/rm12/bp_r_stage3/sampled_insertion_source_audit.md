least-cost path and boundary consistency; modified flow constraints fit the

**RM12-S3-V02 — Daganzo, RR-2003-7 (November 2003), A Variational Formulation

well-posedness, duality and applications, NHM 1(4), 601–619,
DOI 10.3934/nhm.2006.1.601.** Section 2.1, Theorems 1–3, PDF pages 3–5:
prescribed-path initial/finite-road validity and boundary consistency. Section 5,
PDF pages 11–12, distinguishes this from endogenous multilane linkages, whose
rigorous general conditions were not established there. These scoped positive
results support an analytic continuation route, not our entire ramp model.
[Publisher original](https://www.aimspress.com/aimspress-data/nhm/2006/4/PDF/1556-1801_2006_4_601.pdf).

## Deductions and limits

Reset conservation, sampled causality, compatible same-point labels and a local
active trace calculation are reviewer deductions in the
[mathematical companion](../../../synthesis/rm12/bp_r_stage3/sampled_insertion_math.pdf).
The diagnostic inputs are not calibrated traffic scenarios or common-control
witnesses. Exact record retention is distinct from an exact continuum field
solve. Existing field discretization is not silently replaced by a new graph
approximation. The general own-speed/node/accepted-transfer update and clearance
remain unqualified; immediate activation alone would not remove those gaps.
