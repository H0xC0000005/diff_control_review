## JIN10 — Wen-Long Jin, Continuous kinematic wave models of merging traffic flow

[Primary author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.3952)

Read arXiv 0810.3952v2, whose header has the 13 April 2010 version stamp
and whose rendered cover also displays an October 2018 date. Cite this
version identifier rather than interpreting that cover date as a new study.
47 PDF pages. Targeted primary methods: section 2 admissible stationary/
interior states; section 3.1 equation 17, theorem 3.1, corollary 3.2;
section 4.1 equations 21–22, theorem 4.1; section 5.2 and conclusion.

The manuscript proves unique Riemann boundary fluxes for two-input merge
schemes. A direct demand-proportional finite flux need not equal its
continuous counterpart; interior boundary states help represent the limit.
Fixed priority with unused-share transfer has an invariant flux function.
The conclusion explicitly treats non-invariant discrete schemes as
approximations rather than incorrect solutions. This supports the distinction
between CTM and a naive continuous transplantation. It does not prove our
oriented multi-port/source/zero-passing/K.A construction or select T.A/T.B.

No arbitrary priority replacement is inferred for IT. The earlier two-road
counterexample remains scoped; the new constructive two-lane examples use
saturated donors, so their own point demand stays at capacity.
