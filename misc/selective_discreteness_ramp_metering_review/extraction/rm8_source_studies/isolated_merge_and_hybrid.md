# Isolated merge mechanism and its information limit

Date: 2026-10-01. Source claims: RM8-C01/02/07.

**RM0014:** [public 2004 report](https://escholarship.org/content/qt30s46939/qt30s46939.pdf),
22 PDF pages; journal version is 2005. Sections 3–4 link restrictive then relaxed
metering to shoulder-queue reduction and temporary discharge recovery.
Section 5/Figure 7 explicitly proposes road-averaged occupancy thresholds.
This is intervention evidence for a merge mechanism, and counterevidence to
assuming that lane decomposition is unavailable to an aggregate-history rule.
It does not compare identical histories or establish cleared all-stream delay.

**RM8-P01:** [author merge manuscript](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.gatech.edu/dist/f/3571/files/2023/03/merges.pdf),
9 pages, Sections 2–4. Kinematic-wave lane streams plus bounded-acceleration
lane-changing particles reproduce field metering recovery. Ramp-boundary
priority is site-adapted. Predicted discharge also depends on whether a ramp
queue makes entering traffic slow. A known ramp queue therefore defeats that
example as a hidden-information certificate. No adjoint or independent control
gradient is supplied. RM0011 is the underlying hybrid-family working paper.

**Review inference:** Meter-only local-lane physics deserves analytic screening.
Neither a full multilane model nor a compact shoulder-state closure follows as
a necessary representation. The proposed compression remains new review work.
