## Conclusion and stop rules

A positive methodological admission needs a source-grounded task, strong aggregate
competitors, a defensible consequential information gap and an explicit conditional
gradient path. Numerical gradient/reference evidence remains future work.
A scoped negative admission is valid if inspected mechanisms are explained by
admitted aggregate information or essential positive obligations lack support.
Distinguish an information-sufficiency proof from missing evidence: absence of a
witness is not universal impossibility. A partial physical mechanism is not a
positive core-task conclusion. Record coverage and remaining boundaries.

Execute the authorized A scope to that conclusion. Stop for user discussion only
if an indispensable change to G0 or genuinely unresolved methodological adoption
prevents further in-scope review. Do not open C, revive B or broaden the actuators.
At the final result, report review completion and distinguish it from user
acceptance of closure or a new research task. No successor opens automatically.
